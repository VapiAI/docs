You can change your Success Package or add capacity and compliance options without changing how Vapi charges for usage. The available actions depend on your package and billing arrangement.

See [Pricing and Success Packages](/billing/pricing-and-success-packages) to compare package support and service commitments before you change your package.

## Prerequisites

- An **Admin** role in the organization.
- A saved payment method and enough credits to cover any prorated charge shown before purchase. See [Manage billing and credits](/billing/manage-billing-and-credits).

## Change your Success Package

An upgrade increases the entitlements associated with a package and can create a prorated charge for the current billing period. A downgrade reduces package entitlements at the start of a future billing period.

### Upgrade to Core or Pro

Upgrade when you need more support, capacity, or service commitments. Your new limits apply right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Packages and add-ons">
    In the [Vapi Dashboard](https://dashboard.vapi.ai), open **Billing**, then open the **Packages & add-ons** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a package">
    Select **Upgrade** on the package you want.

    {/* screenshot: Packages & add-ons view with the package cards and Upgrade actions */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the entitlement changes and the prorated amount due for the rest of the current period, then proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your limits increase immediately. The new monthly fee begins at the start of the next billing period.

### Move to Premier

Premier is a custom package arranged with the Vapi sales team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact sales">
    On the **Premier** card, select **Contact sales**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your details">
    Complete the form with your company, work email, expected call volume, and use case, then submit. An account team member follows up to set custom terms in an agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrade or cancel a package

Move to a lower package, or cancel to return to Usage, when you need less. The change applies at the start of your next billing period.

<Warning>
A downgrade is blocked when the subscription uses more capacity than the destination package includes, for example more organizations than the lower package allows. Delete organizations, or reduce other usage, before scheduling the downgrade. See [Manage organizations and capacity](/billing/manage-organizations-and-capacity).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the downgrade">
    In **Packages & add-ons**, select the lower package you want, or cancel to return to **Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the reductions">
    Review what the change reduces, such as concurrent call lines, organizations, data retention, support, and service-level agreements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the change">
    Schedule the downgrade. It takes effect at the start of the next billing period, so your current entitlements remain until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy capacity add-ons

Capacity add-ons increase a specific entitlement without changing the package:

- **Concurrent call lines** shared by all organizations in the subscription. Each line adds one simultaneous call to the subscription's included concurrency.
- **Additional organizations** in the subscription.

Capacity add-ons renew monthly until you remove them, and you cannot reduce an add-on below the capacity currently in use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open capacity add-ons">
    Open the subscription's add-on settings and select **Change capacity add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amounts">
    Set the number of additional call lines and organizations you want.

    {/* screenshot: Select capacity add-ons dialog with call-line and organization steppers */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the summary and the prorated amount due today, then proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy compliance and data add-ons

Compliance and data add-ons change how Vapi handles data for a subscription. Options can include HIPAA compliance and extended data retention.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open compliance and data add-ons">
    Open the subscription's add-on settings and select **Change compliance & data add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the add-ons">
    Enable **HIPAA compliance** and enter the signatory name and legal entity name. Vapi emails a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) to your billing contact. You can also enable **extended data retention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the summary and proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

HIPAA compliance requires a signed BAA and compatible providers. See [HIPAA compliance](/security-and-privacy/hipaa) for requirements and data-handling behavior.

## Billing arrangements

When you pay by card, purchases use your subscription's credit balance. For invoiced subscriptions, including Premier, your account team makes changes, and add-on availability can depend on your package and billing arrangement.
