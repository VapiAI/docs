The Usage plan remains active when you add, change, or remove a Success Package. You can also purchase add-ons that increase specific capabilities without changing your Success Package.

Available actions depend on your Success Package and billing arrangement.

See [Pricing and Success Packages](/billing/pricing-and-success-packages) to compare package support and service commitments before you change your package.

## Prerequisites

- An **Admin** role in the organization.
- A saved payment method for purchases made through the Dashboard. See [Manage billing and credits](/billing/manage-billing-and-credits).

## Change your Success Package

An upgrade increases the limits and support associated with your Success Package. A downgrade reduces them. Before you confirm a change, the Dashboard shows when it takes effect and any amount due.

### Upgrade to Core or Pro

Buy Core or Pro directly from the Dashboard when you need more support, capacity, or service commitments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Packages and add-ons">
    In the [Vapi Dashboard](https://dashboard.vapi.ai), open **Billing**, then open the **Packages & add-ons** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a package">
    Select **Upgrade** on the package you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the changes, effective date, and amount due, then proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new package limits apply after you complete the purchase.

### Move to Premier

Premier has custom pricing and terms. Request Premier from the Dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact sales">
    On the **Premier** package, select **Contact Sales**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your details">
    Complete the form with your company, work email, expected call volume, and use case, then submit. An account team member follows up to set custom terms in an agreement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrade or remove a Success Package

Move to a lower Success Package when you need less support or capacity. You can also remove your Success Package and continue on the Usage plan. The Dashboard shows when the change takes effect before you confirm it.

<Warning>
You might need to reduce capacity before moving to a package with lower limits. For example, you cannot move to a package that supports fewer organizations while the additional organizations are still active. See [Manage organizations and capacity](/billing/manage-organizations-and-capacity).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the downgrade">
    In **Packages & add-ons**, select a lower Success Package, or remove your current package to continue on the **Usage plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the reductions">
    Review what the change reduces, such as concurrent call lines, organizations, data retention, support, and service-level agreements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Review the effective date shown in the Dashboard, then confirm the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy capacity add-ons

Capacity add-ons increase a specific entitlement without replacing the Usage plan or changing your Success Package:

- **Concurrent call lines** shared by all organizations in the subscription. Each line adds one simultaneous call on top of your subscription's included concurrency. All inbound and outbound calls share this capacity. A campaign's **Max concurrency** setting limits that campaign, but it does not reserve additional call lines.
- **Additional organizations** in the subscription.

Review the recurring charge and effective date before confirming an add-on purchase. You cannot reduce an add-on below the capacity currently in use. See [call concurrency](/calls/call-concurrency) for how shared capacity works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open capacity add-ons">
    Open the subscription's add-on settings and select **Change capacity add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amounts">
    Set the number of additional call lines and organizations you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the summary and the prorated amount due today, then proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buy compliance and data add-ons

Compliance and data add-ons change how Vapi handles data for a subscription. Options can include HIPAA compliance and extended data retention.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open compliance and data add-ons">
    Open the subscription's add-on settings and select **Change compliance & data add-ons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the add-ons">
    Enable **HIPAA compliance** and enter the signatory name and legal entity name. Vapi emails a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) to your billing contact. You can also enable **extended data retention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and pay">
    Review the summary and proceed to payment and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

HIPAA compliance requires a signed BAA and compatible providers. See [HIPAA compliance](/security-and-privacy/hipaa) for requirements and data-handling behavior.

## Billing arrangements

The Dashboard shows the payment method, amount due, and renewal details before you confirm a purchase. For invoiced subscriptions, including Premier, contact your account team. Add-on availability can depend on your Success Package and billing arrangement.
