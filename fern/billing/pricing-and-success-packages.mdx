Vapi pricing has two parts: usage and an optional Success Package. Every subscription runs on usage. A Success Package is optional and sits on top of your usage when you need more support, reliability, compliance, or capacity.

See the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing) for the latest prices.

## Usage

Usage is how Vapi charges for the minutes you run, and it applies to every subscription. You pay a per-minute hosting fee, plus the model, voice, transcriber, and telephony costs for each call.

Usage does not require a Success Package. On usage alone you get:

- Full access to the Vapi platform and its features
- Unlimited assistants and minutes
- Documentation and peer-to-peer community support
- The base limits shown in [What's included](#whats-included)

Usage does not include a response or uptime service-level agreement.

## Success Packages

A Success Package is an optional subscription that sits on top of your usage. It does not change how usage is billed. It adds higher support, service commitments, larger capacity, and compliance and governance options.

Running without a Success Package is the **Usage** row below. **Core**, **Pro**, and **Premier** each add more.

| Level | Support | Service commitments | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Usage** | Documentation and peer-to-peer community | No response or uptime service-level agreement | Included with usage, no Success Package required |
| **Core** | Everything in Usage, plus email support | Two-business-day response service-level agreement | Buy in the Dashboard |
| **Pro** | Everything in Core, plus a dedicated Slack channel | One-business-day response and 99% uptime service-level agreements | Buy in the Dashboard or contact Sales |
| **Premier** | Everything in Pro, plus a named support team | Custom response and 99.9% uptime service-level agreements | Contact Sales |

Core has a fixed monthly price. Pro pricing scales with Vapi hosting spend and has a monthly minimum. Premier pricing and terms depend on your agreement.

## What's included

Each level includes the following limits and features. For the latest prices, see the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing).

| Included | Usage | Core | Pro | Premier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Concurrent call lines](/calls/call-concurrency) | 4 | 10 | 30 | Custom |
| [Organizations](/billing/manage-organizations-and-capacity) | 2 | 5 | 10 | Custom |
| API rate limit (requests per minute, per organization) | 200 | 200 | 300 | Custom |
| Data retention | 14 days | 30 days | 180 days | Custom |
| [Vapi phone numbers](/free-telephony) | 1 | 5 | 10 | Custom |
| Voice simulation concurrency | 1 | 4 | 8 | Custom |
| [Role-based access control (RBAC)](/security-and-privacy/sso#role-based-access-control-rbac) | All members are admins | All members are admins | Basic roles | Full RBAC |
| [Single sign-on (SSO)](/security-and-privacy/sso) | Not included | Not included | Not included | Included |

Levels also include monitoring allowances that scale with the level. Premier values are custom; your agreement defines the limits and capabilities included.

## Contact Sales

Contact the Vapi sales team to buy Pro or Premier through direct sales, negotiate volume pricing, or arrange custom terms such as compliance and security review. There are two ways to reach them:

- In **Billing**, select **Contact sales** on the **Pro** or **Premier** card, then complete the form with your company, work email, expected call volume, and use case.
- Go to [vapi.ai/sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) and submit your details.

A Vapi account team member follows up to discuss the best fit.

## Add-ons

Add-ons extend your capacity or add compliance options without changing your support. Available add-ons can include additional concurrent call lines, additional organizations, and compliance options such as HIPAA.

Add-on availability and purchase methods depend on your level and billing arrangement. See the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing) for current options and prices.
