Every Vapi subscription starts with the Usage plan. The Usage plan covers usage-based charges and remains active when you add an optional Success Package or add-ons.

A Success Package adds support, service commitments, and higher included limits. Add-ons increase individual capabilities without replacing the Usage plan or your Success Package.

See the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing) for the latest prices.

## Usage plan

The Usage plan is the base plan for every Vapi subscription. You pay a per-minute hosting fee, plus the model, voice, transcriber, and telephony costs for each call.

The Usage plan includes documentation, peer-to-peer community support, and the base limits shown in [What's included](#whats-included).

Every minute your assistants run is billed. The Usage plan does not include a response or uptime service-level agreement.

## Success Packages

A Success Package is an optional subscription that sits on top of the Usage plan. It does not change how usage is billed. It adds higher support, service commitments, larger capacity, and compliance and governance options.

The Usage plan remains the base when you add Core, Pro, or Premier.

| Plan or package | Support | Service commitments | How to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Usage plan** | Documentation and peer-to-peer community | No response or uptime service-level agreement | Included with every subscription |
| **Core** | Everything in Usage, plus email support | Two-business-day response service-level agreement | Buy in the Dashboard |
| **Pro** | Everything in Core, plus a dedicated Slack channel | One-business-day response and 99% uptime service-level agreements | Buy in the Dashboard |
| **Premier** | Everything in Pro, plus a named support team | Custom response and 99.9% uptime service-level agreements | Request Premier from the Dashboard |

Core has a fixed monthly price. Pro pricing scales with Vapi hosting spend and has a monthly minimum. Premier pricing and terms depend on your agreement.

## What's included

The Usage plan and each Success Package include the following limits and features. For the latest prices, see the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing).

| Included | Usage | Core | Pro | Premier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Concurrent call lines](/calls/call-concurrency) | 4 | 10 | 30 | Custom |
| [Organizations](/billing/manage-organizations-and-capacity) | 2 | 5 | 10 | Custom |
| API rate limit (requests per minute, per organization) | 200 | 200 | 300 | Custom |
| Data retention | 14 days | 30 days | 180 days | Custom |
| [Vapi phone numbers](/free-telephony) | 1 | 5 | 10 | Custom |
| Voice simulation concurrency | 1 | 4 | 8 | Custom |
| [Role-based access control (RBAC)](/security-and-privacy/sso#role-based-access-control-rbac) | All members are admins | All members are admins | Basic roles | Custom RBAC |
| [Single sign-on (SSO)](/security-and-privacy/sso) | Not included | Not included | Not included | Custom |

Success Packages also include monitoring allowances that scale with the package. Premier values are custom; your agreement defines the limits and capabilities included.

## Buy a Success Package

Buy Core or Pro directly from the Vapi Dashboard:

1. Open **Billing & Payment**.
2. Select **Packages & add-ons**.
3. Select **Upgrade** on the Core or Pro package.

Your new package limits apply after you complete the purchase.

Premier has custom pricing and terms. Select **Contact Sales** on the Premier package in the Dashboard and submit the form.

## Add-ons

Add-ons increase a specific capability without replacing the Usage plan or changing your Success Package. You can add them directly to the Usage plan or use them alongside Core, Pro, or Premier.

Available add-ons can include additional concurrent call lines, additional organizations, and compliance options such as HIPAA.

Add-on availability and purchase methods depend on your Success Package and billing arrangement. See the [Vapi pricing page](https://vapi.ai/pricing) for current options and prices.
