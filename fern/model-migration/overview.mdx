If your assistant runs on GPT-4.1, this page helps you choose the model to move to. Each model has its own page with the prompt changes it needs, and the [migration guide](/model-migration/guide) shows how to test your choice on copies of your assistant before any caller hears it.

## Our recommendation

**Sapien is our recommended default.** It's Vapi's own model and the best overall choice: it balances intelligence and reasoning with extremely low latency, at a lower cost than GPT-4.1. To use it, set `"provider": "vapi"` and `"model": "sapien"` in your assistant's `model` object.

<Note>
Sapien is coming soon. The guidance here and on the Sapien page is ready for when it's available.
</Note>

Choose Terra when you need even more reasoning, or Luna when you're optimizing for price.

| Model | Choose it for | Latency (time to first token, p50) | Cost compared with GPT-4.1 | Prompt guidance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sapien** (recommended default) | Most assistants: the best balance of intelligence, latency and cost | About 200 ms | Much lower | [4.1 → Sapien Tips](/model-migration/sapien) |
| **Terra** (5.6 or 6.1) | When you need even more reasoning | About 800 ms | Similar to Sapien | [4.1 → 5.6/6.1 Terra Tips](/model-migration/terra) |
| **Luna** (5.6 or 6) | Teams optimizing for price | About 600 ms | About 80% lower | [4.1 → 5.6/6 Luna Tips](/model-migration/luna) |

<Note>
This recommendation reflects benchmarks the Vapi team ran as of November 2026 and will change as new models become available.
</Note>

## How to choose

- **Start with Sapien.** It gives you high intelligence and reasoning, with the lowest latency of the three and a lower cost than GPT-4.1.
- **Choose Terra when you need even more reasoning.** It has higher latency than Sapien, at a similar price. Both 5.6 Terra and 6.1 Terra are options, each with its own guidance on the Terra page.
- **Choose Luna** when cost matters most. It costs around 80% less than GPT-4.1. It needs fuller, more specific prompts than GPT-4.1. Both 5.6 Luna and 6 Luna are options, each with its own guidance on the Luna page.
- **Not sure between two?** Test both against the same baseline. The migration guide's test suite, copies and comparison work the same way for each.

## How to migrate

Whichever model you pick, test it on copies of your assistant before you switch. The [migration guide](/model-migration/guide) walks you through it with your coding agent and Vapi's migration [agent skills](/agent-skills): map how your calls go, build a simulation suite from them, create a copy on the new model with the prompt changes from its model page, and compare the two before you roll out.
