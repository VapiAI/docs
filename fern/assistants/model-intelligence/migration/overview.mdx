If your assistant runs on GPT-4.1, this page helps you choose the model to move to. Each model has its own page with the prompt changes it needs, and the [migration guide](/assistants/model-intelligence/migration/guide) shows how to test your choice on copies of your assistant before any caller hears it.

## Our recommendation

**Sapien is our recommended default.** `Coming soon` It's Vapi's own model, with a strong balance of performance and reasoning: high intelligence at extremely low latency, at a much lower cost than GPT-4.1. To use it, set `"provider": "vapi"` and `"model": "sapien"` in your assistant's `model` object.

Choose Terra when your assistant needs more reasoning, or Luna when you're optimizing for price.

| Model | Choose it for | Latency (time to first token, p50) | Cost compared with GPT-4.1 | Prompt guidance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sapien** `Coming soon` (recommended default) | A strong balance of performance and reasoning for most assistants | About 200 ms | Much lower | [Prompting for Sapien](/assistants/model-intelligence/migration/sapien) |
| **6.1 Terra** (or 5.6 Terra) | Assistants that need more reasoning | About 800 ms | Similar to Sapien | [Prompting for Terra](/assistants/model-intelligence/migration/terra) |
| **5.6 Luna** (or 6 Luna) | Teams optimizing for price | About 600 ms | About 80% lower | [Prompting for Luna](/assistants/model-intelligence/migration/luna) |

<Note>
This recommendation reflects benchmarks the Vapi team ran as of November 2026 and will change as new models become available.
</Note>

## How to choose

- **Start with Sapien** for most assistants. It gives you high intelligence at the lowest latency of the three.
- **Choose Terra** when your assistant has to reason through more: multi-step rules, several tools or nuanced decisions. It responds more slowly than Sapien at a similar price. We recommend 6.1 Terra; 5.6 Terra is an alternative, with its own guidance on the same page.
- **Choose Luna** when cost matters most. It costs about 80% less than GPT-4.1 and still performed better than GPT-4.1 in our benchmarks. It needs fuller, more specific prompts than GPT-4.1. We recommend 5.6 Luna; 6 Luna is an alternative, with its own guidance on the same page.
- **Not sure between two?** Test both against the same baseline. The migration guide's test suite, copies and comparison work the same way for each.

## How to migrate

Whichever model you pick, test it on copies of your assistant before you switch. The [migration guide](/assistants/model-intelligence/migration/guide) walks you through it with your coding agent and Vapi's migration [agent skills](/agent-skills): map how your calls go, build a simulation suite from them, create a copy on the new model with the prompt changes from its model page, and compare the two before you roll out.
