This guide details how to create free phone numbers on the Vapi platform, which you can use with your assistants or squads.

<Note>
  Each account can create up to five free Vapi phone numbers. You can create the first number without a saved payment method. To create additional numbers, add a payment method to your account. Free Vapi numbers use US area codes and support US national calling only.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Phone Numbers">
    Open the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.vapi.ai/). Select **Phone Numbers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a free Vapi number">
    Select **Create Phone Number**, then choose **Free Vapi Number** under **Phone Number Options**.

    <Frame caption="Open Phone Numbers and start the creation flow.">
      <img src="../static/images/phone-numbers/free-telephony/phone-numbers-create-button-dashboard.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter an area code">
    Enter a supported US area code in **Area Code**. If the Dashboard displays **Payment method required**, add a payment method before continuing.

    <Note>
      Vapi-managed free phone numbers support US area codes. To use an international number, choose one of the import options under **Phone Number Options**.
    </Note>

    <Frame caption="Choose Free Vapi Number and enter a US area code.">
      <img src="../static/images/phone-numbers/free-telephony/create-free-vapi-number-dashboard.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the number">
    Select **Create**. Vapi assigns an available phone number with the area code you entered.

    <Note>
      It can take a few minutes for the phone number to become active. Calls will not work until activation is complete.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign inbound calls">
    Open the new phone number. Under **Inbound Settings**, choose the **Assistant** or **Squad** that should handle incoming calls, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand billing and usage restrictions

- **The number is free, but calls are not.** Calls and other platform usage consume credits and are billed according to [Vapi pricing](https://vapi.ai/pricing).
- **Free Vapi numbers are for US national use.** They cannot make international calls. For international use, [import a number from another provider](/phone-numbers/import-twilio).
- **Outbound calling not supported.** Import a number from a supported telephony provider when you need higher-volume outbound calling. See [Outbound calling](/calls/outbound-calling).
- **Outbound Campaigns require an imported number.** Free Vapi numbers cannot be used to launch [Outbound Campaigns](/outbound-campaigns/quickstart).
- **You are responsible for lawful use.** Obtain the required consent before making outbound calls and follow applicable calling, telemarketing, and do-not-call rules. See the [TCPA consent guide](/tcpa-consent) and [Vapi Terms of Service](https://vapi.ai/terms-of-service).
