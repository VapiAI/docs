## Overview

Voice AI Portal is a white-label client portal and billing tool for agencies that build AI agents on Vapi, ElevenLabs and Retell AI. You keep building and running each assistant in Vapi. Voice AI Portal gives each of your clients their own portal on your subdomain, with your logo and colours, showing every call, SMS and web chat their assistants handled.

Each client keeps their own Vapi organisation. The portal connects to it with a [private API key](/security-and-privacy/api-keys) and reads conversations directly from Vapi whenever the client opens a page, so there is no server URL or webhook to set up.

## Key Benefits

- **No code, no webhooks:**
  Paste a private API key from the client's Vapi organisation. The portal lists its assistants and phone numbers, and you choose which ones the client's dashboard reports on.

- **Your brand on every page:**
  Your subdomain, logo, colours, login page and emails from your own domain. Clients sign in with a one-time code and never see Vapi or what each call cost you.

- **Everything the assistant handled:**
  Phone calls, web calls, SMS and web chats in one searchable list, with the recording, transcript, summary, outcome and sentiment where the assistant's analysis records it.

- **Analytics clients understand:**
  Call volume, busiest hours, outcomes, after-hours answers, and staff time handled: the hours of front-desk work the assistants covered, counted from resolved conversations only.

- **Client billing included:**
  Sell clients monthly minute and chat plans and minute packs. Vapi calls use minutes and SMS and chats count as chats. Get paid by card, Stripe invoice or bank transfer, with no fee taken on what your clients pay.

- **Outbound campaigns:**
  Set up a campaign playbook once and let a client call a lead list through their own Vapi assistant and phone number, with retries, calling hours and opt-outs.

## How It Works

1. **Create a private key in Vapi:**
   In the client's Vapi organisation, open API Keys and add a private key, for example "Voice AI Portal, Acme Dental".
2. **Add the client in Voice AI Portal:**
   Choose Vapi as the provider, paste the key and validate it. The organisation's assistants and phone numbers load straight away.
3. **Choose what the client sees:**
   Select the client's assistants and numbers, then set their country, currency, time zone and staff cost.
4. **Brand and invite:**
   Add your subdomain, logo and colours, and invite the client. They sign in to your branded portal with a one-time code.

## How Vapi conversations appear

| Conversation in Vapi | Shown in the portal as |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Phone call | Phone |
| Web call | Web call |
| WebSocket call | WebSocket |
| SMS session | SMS |
| Web chat session | Web chat |
| Chat thread | Chat |

Only conversations handled by the assistants you selected for that client are shown, and each client sees only their own workspace.
