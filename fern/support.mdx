## Support options

Your support channels and response commitments depend on your Success Package. The Usage plan includes community support. See [Pricing and Success Packages](/billing/pricing-and-success-packages) for a complete comparison.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Community" icon="fa-brands fa-discord" href="https://discord.com/invite/pUFNcf2WmH">
    Included with the Usage plan and every Success Package. Get peer-to-peer help in our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/pUFNcf2WmH) community and search the docs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@vapi.ai">
    Included with Core, Pro, and Premier. Email support@vapi.ai for help within your Success Package's response commitment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dedicated Slack" icon="fa-brands fa-slack">
    Pro adds a dedicated Slack channel with a one-business-day response service-level agreement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premier support" icon="headset" href="/billing/pricing-and-success-packages#buy-a-success-package">
    Premier adds a named support team and custom response commitments. Request Premier from the Dashboard.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
For the fastest response, Pro and Premier customers should use their dedicated support channels.
</Note>
