## Support options

Your support level depends on your Success Package. See [Pricing and Success Packages](/billing/pricing-and-success-packages) to compare what each plan includes.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Community" icon="fa-brands fa-discord" href="https://discord.com/invite/pUFNcf2WmH">
    Included on every plan. Get peer-to-peer help in our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/pUFNcf2WmH) community and search the docs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@vapi.ai">
    From Core and up. Email support@vapi.ai and our team responds within your plan's service-level agreement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dedicated Slack" icon="fa-brands fa-slack">
    Pro adds a dedicated Slack channel with a one-business-day response service-level agreement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premier support" icon="headset" href="/billing/pricing-and-success-packages#contact-sales">
    Premier adds a named support team and a custom service-level agreement. Contact sales to set it up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
For the fastest response, Pro and Premier customers should use their dedicated support channels.
</Note>

We welcome feature requests and feedback from our users to help improve Vapi. You can:

  <Card

  <Card 
    title="Submit Bug Report" 
    icon="bug"
