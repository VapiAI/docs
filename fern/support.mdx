## Support options

Your Vapi support channels and response commitments depend on your Success Package. Without a Success Package, you have the documentation and peer-to-peer help from the Vapi Discord community. See [Pricing and Success Packages](/billing/pricing-and-success-packages) for the support and service-level agreements included with each package.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discord community" icon="fa-brands fa-discord" href="https://discord.gg/v5Ee6FhAZt">
    Peer-to-peer help from other Vapi developers, open to everyone. For a response from Vapi, use the support channels included with Core, Pro, and Premier.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="envelope">
    Email support is included with Core, Pro, and Premier.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dedicated Slack" icon="fa-brands fa-slack">
    Pro and Premier include a dedicated Slack channel for direct access to the Vapi team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Premier support" icon="headset" href="/billing/pricing-and-success-packages#buy-a-success-package">
    Premier adds a named support team.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
For the fastest response, Pro and Premier customers should use their dedicated support channels.
</Note>
