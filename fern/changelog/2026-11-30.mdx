# What's New: Week of November 30, 2026

## Pricing

1. **Evals are priced like chats**: Starting December 15, 2026, each eval turn costs $0.005, plus the model and tool costs for that turn, including the AI judge's model. The $0.0005 per-run fee is removed. Exact and regex judges stay free, and Vapi doesn't charge for a model that runs on your own provider API key. From that date, an eval run's `costs` shows the turn fees as a `chat` row. See [Eval pricing](/observability/evals-pricing) for a worked example and ways to keep costs down.
