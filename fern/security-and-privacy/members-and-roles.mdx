Members are the people who can sign in to your organization in the Vapi Dashboard. Each member has a role that controls what they can see and change.

## RBAC roles

Role-based access control (RBAC) controls what each member can do in your organization. You assign a role when you invite a member, and you can change it later.

<Note>
The **Editor** and **Viewer** roles are available with the **Pro** or **Premier** Success Package or an Enterprise plan. Without one of these, every member is an **Admin**.
</Note>

Vapi provides these predefined roles:

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Admin** | Read and write everything |
| **Editor** | Read and write everything **except** Org Settings, Billing, Members, and API Keys |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to everything **except** Org Settings, Billing, Members, and API Keys |

Use **Editor** to give developers full build and test access without exposing billing or member management.

## Prerequisites

- An **Admin** role in the organization.

## Invite members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members page">
    Sign in to the [Vapi Dashboard](https://dashboard.vapi.ai), select the organization, open **Settings**, then select **Members**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the people to invite">
    Select **Add Member**. In **Invite Users**, enter an email address, then add it to the invite list. Repeat for each person you want to invite.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a role for each person">
    Choose a **Role** for each person in the list. To give everyone in the list the same role, set the role in **All**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invitations">
    Select **Invite**. Each person receives an invitation to join the organization, and the invitation appears in the members list until they accept it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a member's role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members page">
    Sign in to the [Vapi Dashboard](https://dashboard.vapi.ai), select the organization, open **Settings**, then select **Members**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the new role">
    Find the member in the list. In the **Role** column, select the new role. The Dashboard confirms the change with **Member role changed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revoke an invitation

On the **Members** page, find the pending invitation, open its actions menu, then select **Revoke invitation**.

## Remove a member

On the **Members** page, find the member, open their actions menu, then select **Remove from Organization**.
