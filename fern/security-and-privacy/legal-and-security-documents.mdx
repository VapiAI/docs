Vapi publishes its security and compliance documentation in the [Vapi Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/). Use it to review Vapi's security posture and to request access to documents that are not public.

## Where to find each document

| Document | Where to get it |
| --- | --- |
| Data processing agreement (DPA) | Download it from the **Documents** section of the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/) or from the link in the [Terms of Service](https://vapi.ai/terms-of-service). The DPA incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses, including the UK Addendum and Swiss adaptations where applicable. |
| Subprocessor list | The **Subprocessors** section of the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/) lists Vapi's current subprocessors. |
| SOC 3 report | Available in the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/) without an NDA. |
| SOC 2 Type II report | Request access in the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/). The report is shared under NDA. See [SOC 2 Type II compliance](/security-and-privacy/soc). |
| Penetration test summary | Request access in the **Documents** section of the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/). |
| Business Associate Agreement (BAA) | Enable HIPAA compliance in the Dashboard, and Vapi emails you the BAA to sign. See [HIPAA compliance](/security-and-privacy/hipaa). The [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/) lists the providers Vapi holds BAAs with. |
| Terms of service | [vapi.ai/terms-of-service](https://vapi.ai/terms-of-service) |
| Privacy policy | [vapi.ai/privacy](https://vapi.ai/privacy) |
| Data location and data flow | See [EU region](/security-and-privacy/eu-region) and [Data flow](/security-and-privacy/data-flow). |

Requests for restricted documents are reviewed and approved individually.

## Signing the DPA

On self-serve plans, the DPA is incorporated into the Terms of Service by reference, so no separate signature is needed. Downloading the DPA from the Trust Center records your acceptance. A countersigned or modified DPA is available on [Enterprise](https://vapi.ai/enterprise) agreements.

## Providers you connect with your own keys

When you connect a provider with your own API key, that provider is not a Vapi subprocessor. It is not covered by Vapi's DPA or BAA, and your own agreement with that provider governs how it handles your data, including training.

## Model training

Vapi does not train foundational or general-purpose models on customer data. Enabling [Zero Data Retention](/security-and-privacy/zero-data-retention) or [HIPAA compliance](/security-and-privacy/hipaa) stops Vapi from using your content to improve its call features.

## Security questionnaires and audits

Review the answers already published in the [Trust Center](https://security.vapi.ai/) before you send a security questionnaire. Vapi provides its current audit reports, including the SOC 2 Type II report, on written request. Audit rights are set out in the DPA.

## Contact

Send security and privacy questions to [infosec@vapi.ai](mailto:infosec@vapi.ai) or [privacy@vapi.ai](mailto:privacy@vapi.ai). Vapi runs a private, invite-only bug bounty program on HackerOne. To report a vulnerability or ask for an invitation, email [infosec@vapi.ai](mailto:infosec@vapi.ai).
