Forward a Dialpad menu key or fallback to your agent's US number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a Dialpad department, contact center, or main line number. This works on Dialpad Connect and on Dialpad Support for contact centers.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a menu key or fallback, to a US number.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a department, contact center, or main line number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No. Dialpad gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Dialpad's [IVR External Number Company Setting](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55577605290395) page says menu forwarding is for US numbers only. A non-US number may save, but calls can fail and loop the caller back to the start of the menu. Forward to the free US starter number, or a US number connected in Vapi for CX. A Dialpad company admin does the steps in Dialpad.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's US number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Dialpad**, and copy the number under **In Dialpad, send calls to this number**. If the agent has several numbers, pick one under **Forward to** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on forwarding to outside numbers">
    In Dialpad, go to **Admin Settings** › **My Company** › **Company Settings** › **IVR Forwarding to External Numbers**, and select **Enable IVR forwarding to external numbers**. IVR (interactive voice response) is the phone menu. The setting is company-wide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the line's call routing">
    Under **Admin Settings**, open the department, contact center, or main line, then **Business hours and call routing** (**Business Hours & Call Handling** on a contact center) › **Call Routing** › **Edit Call Routing**. For more information, see Dialpad's [contact center routing](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/54917748425883).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a menu key to the agent's number">
    Under **Routing Options** › **Fallback Options**, choose **To an automated response menu**. For a key, choose to forward it to a number, and enter the agent's number. Repeat for closed hours if you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the Dialpad number from your own phone and press the menu key. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** isn't documented by Dialpad. Dialpad places a new call to the agent and joins it to the caller, so the agent may see the caller's number, a Dialpad number, or none.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent transfers the caller to a Dialpad department's, contact center's, or main line's number. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    In Dialpad, go to **Admin Settings** › **Departments** or **Contact Center**, and open the Info page to find the department's or contact center's number. Don't use the number whose menu forwards to the agent.

    If the team has no number, on the same Info page choose **Add a Number** › **Local** or **Toll-Free**, pick one, then **Select**, **Next**, and **Add Number**. Fees apply. Label the number (**Phone number(s)** › **Options** › **Edit Label**) so your people see where a transferred call came from. For more information, see Dialpad's [add numbers](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55081748118683).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Dialpad number. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. If Dialpad gave the agent no caller number, there's none to pass on.

**Check it:** Once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone, and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Dialpad forwards from a menu to US numbers only.
- Only a spoken summary can travel with a handoff, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number and a person, not a queue or a menu, answers.
- When the agent hangs up, the call ends.
- Dialpad says calls forwarded to outside numbers may incur outbound usage or per-minute charges, depending on your plan or contract. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
