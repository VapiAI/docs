Forward a Dialpad menu key or fallback to your agent's US number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a Dialpad department, contact center or main line number. This works on Dialpad Connect and on Dialpad Support for contact centers.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a menu key or fallback, to a US number.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a department, contact center or main line number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No: Dialpad gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Dialpad forwards to US numbers only: Dialpad may save a non-US number, but the call fails and the caller goes back to the menu. Forward to the free US starter number, or a US number connected in Vapi for CX. A Dialpad company admin does the steps in Dialpad. Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so connect a US number of your own first: see the [overview](/phone-systems).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's US number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Dialpad**, and copy the number under **Forward to**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on forwarding to outside numbers">
    In Dialpad, go to **Admin Settings** › **My Company** › **Company Settings** › **IVR Forwarding to External Numbers**, and select **Enable IVR forwarding to external numbers**. IVR is the phone menu, and the setting is company-wide. For more information, see Dialpad's [IVR External Number Company Setting](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55577605290395).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the line's call routing">
    Under **Admin Settings**, open the department, contact center or main line, then **Business hours and call routing** (**Business Hours & Call Handling** on a contact center) › **Call Routing** › **Edit Call Routing**. For more information, see Dialpad's [contact center routing](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/54917748425883).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a menu key to the agent's number">
    Under **Routing Options** › **Fallback Options**, choose **To an automated response menu**. For a key, choose **Forward to a person / # - HIT ENTER**, type the agent's number and press Enter. Repeat for closed hours if you want.

    Don't use a user's personal forwarding number instead: Dialpad asks whoever answers to press 1, and the agent can't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the Dialpad number from your own phone and press the menu key. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To forward with no menu, choose **To a team member/room phone/number** under **Edit Call Routing**, or **To a team member, room phone, or external number** in **Fallback options**. Either needs Dialpad Customer Care to enable outside numbers for your account first. **Fallback options** show only with **Route Calls to Agents** routing.

**The number the agent sees** isn't documented by Dialpad: Dialpad places a new call to the agent and joins it to the caller, so the agent may see the caller's number, a Dialpad number or none.

## Hand callers to your team

Every Dialpad department, contact center and main line has its own number, and a call to it follows that group's routing. Nothing needs turning on in Dialpad. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    In Dialpad, go to **Admin Settings** › **Departments** or **Contact Center**, and open the Info page to find the department's, contact center's or main line's number. Don't use the number whose menu forwards to the agent.

    If the team has no number, on the same Info page choose **Add a Number** › Local or Toll-Free, pick one, then **Select**, **Next**, and **Add Number**. Fees apply. Label the number (**Phone number(s)** › **Options** › **Edit Label**) so your people see where a transferred call came from. For more information, see Dialpad's [add numbers](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55081748118683).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Dialpad number. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. If Dialpad gave the agent no caller number, there's none to pass on.

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Dialpad forwards from a menu to US numbers only.
- Forwarding a department or contact center straight to an outside number, with no menu, needs Dialpad Customer Care to enable it first.
- No summary or caller details travel with a handoff.
- When the agent hangs up, the call ends: Dialpad doesn't take the caller back to the menu or the department.
- A forwarded call is two Dialpad calls for its whole length, and Dialpad charges the call to the agent per minute. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- Dialpad offers no outbound connection to outside agents, so the agent places calls from its own line.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Dialpad">

- **Dialpad's voice-agent connection over SIP** ("To Third-Party Voice Agents", on Dialpad Pro or Enterprise): **Coming soon**. Dialpad sets it up after a request. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about it.
- **Your own carrier:** your number can go to Vapi at the carrier instead, connected in Vapi for CX. Dialpad can't use your carrier as a route to an outside agent.
- **Dialpad's Google integration:** not supported. It works with Google's voice agents only.
- **Registering the agent as a Dialpad SIP desk phone:** not recommended.

</Accordion>
