Dialpad is a business phone and contact-center system (Dialpad Connect, and Dialpad Support for contact centers). A Dialpad menu key, or a fallback, forwards the call to the agent's US number, and the agent hands callers back by transferring them to a department, contact center or main line number. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Dialpad is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: you copy the agent's number there, and a Dialpad company admin does the steps below. Dialpad's voice-agent connection over SIP is **Coming soon**: <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works with a caveat. A menu key or fallback forwards to the agent's US number; the number the agent sees is unknown.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To a department, contact center or main line number; no caller details travel.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. Dialpad offers no outbound connection to outside agents; the agent uses its own line.
- **Self-serve:** Works with a caveat. A company admin turns on one setting and edits one menu; forwarding with no menu needs Dialpad Customer Care.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

Dialpad forwards to US numbers only: an international number may save, but the call fails and loops the caller back to the menu. Forward to the free US starter number, or a US number connected in Vapi for CX.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a US phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and forward to that number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's US number">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose **A contact center**, and pick **Dialpad** from its list of platforms. Copy the agent's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on forwarding to outside numbers">
    In Dialpad, under Admin Settings › My Company › Company Settings › **IVR Forwarding to External Numbers**, a company admin ticks **Enable IVR forwarding to external numbers** (IVR is the phone menu). The setting is company-wide. Docs: [Dialpad: IVR External Number Company Setting](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55577605290395).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the line's call routing">
    In Dialpad, under Admin Settings, open the department, contact center or main line, then **Business hours and call routing** (**Business Hours & Call Handling** on a contact center) › **Call Routing** › **Edit Call Routing**. Docs: [Dialpad: contact center routing](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/54917748425883).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a menu key to the agent's number">
    In Dialpad, under **Routing Options** › **Fallback Options**, choose **To an automated response menu**. For a key, choose **Forward to a person / # - HIT ENTER**, type the agent's number and press Enter. Repeat for closed hours if you want.

    Do not use a user's personal forwarding number instead: Dialpad asks whoever answers to press 1, and the agent cannot.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Dialpad's own page.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional, for no menu: ask Customer Care first">
    In Dialpad, under Edit Call Routing, choose **To a team member/room phone/number** (no operator) or, in **Fallback options**, **To a team member, room phone, or external number**. Either needs Dialpad Customer Care to enable outside numbers for your account first. Fallback options show only with **Route Calls to Agents** routing.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Dialpad's own page.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the Dialpad number from your own phone and press the menu key. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

    <Note>Not run.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** unknown. Dialpad places a new outbound call and joins it to the caller, and no Dialpad page says which number it shows. One 2023 community post, no longer online, said a call Dialpad's call router forwarded outside showed as anonymous. If the agent sees Dialpad's number or no number, it cannot recognise callers and every call in an experiment lands on one version.

<Note>Unknown. One forwarded test call settles it.</Note>

**Check it:** one forwarded call. The agent answers, so the key or fallback reaches it. Read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX: it may be the caller's, a Dialpad number or anonymous.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** no. No summary or caller details travel with the call.

**Check it:** call in, press the menu key and ask the agent for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when the call reaches the department; note the number the person sees.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. Every Dialpad department, contact center and main line has its own number, and a call to it follows that group's routing. Nothing has to be switched on in Dialpad for a plain transfer to a number. No caller details travel.

<Note>Not verified: not read on Dialpad's own page (department and contact center routing).</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    In Dialpad, under Admin Settings › **Departments** or **Contact Center** › the Info page: the department's, contact center's or main line's number. It must not be the number whose menu forwards to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a number if the team has none">
    In Dialpad, on the same Info page, choose **Add a Number** › Local or Toll-Free, pick one, **Select**, **Next**, review the payment summary, then **Add Number**. Fees apply. A label on the number (Phone number(s) › Options › **Edit Label**) shows your people where a transferred call came from. Docs: [Dialpad: add numbers](https://help.dialpad.com/hc/en-us/articles/55081748118683).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter the Dialpad number. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number by default. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number. If Dialpad gave the agent no caller number on the way in, there is none to pass on.

## Other routes

- **Dialpad's voice-agent connection over SIP** ("To Third-Party Voice Agents"; Dialpad Pro or Enterprise): Coming soon. Dialpad sets it up after a request; it is not self-service. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.
- **Can't:** Dialpad's native Google integration. It works with Google's voice agents only.
- **Can't:** your own carrier inside Dialpad as a route to an outside agent. Your number can go to Vapi at the carrier instead, connected in Vapi for CX.
- **Not recommended:** registering the agent as a Dialpad SIP desk phone. Dialpad supports no more than checking the login, and it has no international calling.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. Dialpad offers no outbound connection to outside agents. The agent dials from its own line and shows its own number.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Forwarding from a menu to a number outside the US.
- Setting up Dialpad's SIP connection yourself: Dialpad says it is not self-service.
- Forwarding a department or contact center straight to an outside number, with no menu, until Dialpad Customer Care enables it.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Which caller number does the agent see on a forwarded call? One forwarded call to the agent's number.
- Does the caller stay on a live Dialpad call after the agent's part ends? Forward a menu key to a test phone and see where the caller goes.
- How many forwarded calls can run at once? Ask Dialpad; not found.
- Does your plan matter for forwarding to outside numbers? Ask your Dialpad admin: no plan requirement is stated, but one Dialpad entry says Standard plans allow in-company transfers only.
- Do the menu names match today's admin screens? They were checked against Dialpad's help pages, not in a live account: a customer admin follows the steps once.

## Costs and limits

- A forwarded call uses two legs for its whole length, both through Dialpad: the caller's call in, and the outbound call Dialpad places to the agent, which Dialpad charges per minute. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- A handoff by phone number arrives at Dialpad as one more call.
- A new local or toll-free number for a handoff team costs extra.
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only, and Dialpad forwards to US numbers only.
- Dialpad's SIP connection needs Pro or Enterprise. Not verified: not read on Dialpad's own page.
- HIPAA: not researched for Dialpad.
