Route calls from Zoom Phone or Zoom Contact Center to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to the number of a Zoom queue.

- **Receive calls:** Yes. On Zoom Phone, the agent sees the forwarding number, not the caller's.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Zoom queue's number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No. Zoom gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Zoom routes calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. What forwards in Zoom (an auto receptionist, call queue, or user) needs its own direct number, and Zoom says forwarding calls to outside numbers can incur metered rate fees for the duration of the call. Your Zoom admin does the steps in Zoom.

In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Zoom Phone** or **Zoom Contact Center**, whichever the calls come through, and copy the number under **In Zoom Phone, send calls to this number** (or **In Zoom Contact Center, send calls to this number**). If the agent has several numbers, pick one under **Forward to** first. Then follow the steps for your product, below.

### Zoom Phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give the auto receptionist or queue a direct number">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Phone system** › **Auto Receptionists** (Classic tab) or **Call Queues**, select the name, then the **Profile** tab. If it has no direct number yet, choose **Number(s)** › **Add**. For more information, see Zoom's [routing targets](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/zoom-phone-bluepaper/configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route to the agent's number">
    On the same **Profile** tab, under **Business Hours**, **Closed Hours**, **Break Hours**, and **Holiday Hours**, set **Route to** › **Phone Number**, pick the **Country/Region**, and enter the agent's number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** on Zoom Phone is the forwarding number, not the caller's. For more information, see Zoom's [caller ID and routing issues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0084977).

### Zoom Contact Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Route To widget">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **CX management** › **Contact Center** › **Flows**, open the voice flow, and drag in a **Route To** widget. Set **Route to** to **OutgoingCall** and **Outgoing Call Target** to **Phone Number**, pick the **Country/Region**, enter the agent's number without spaces or dashes, and set **Max Ring Time**. For more information, see Zoom's [Customizing the Route To widget](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063774).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the caller's number">
    In the **Route To** widget, set **Caller ID** to **Original Caller ID** ("Use the consumer's caller ID name and number"). Connect the **Call Failed** and **Call Not Answered** exits to a queue or voicemail. Save and publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the flow to a number">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Numbers** › **Phone Numbers**. If you need a number, choose **Add Number**, set **Product** to **Contact Center**, search, pick one, and choose **Done**. Then open the number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, select **Voice Flow**, choose the published flow, and save. For more information, see Zoom's [Contact Center numbers](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059452).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** on Zoom Contact Center is the caller's own number, with **Caller ID** set to **Original Caller ID**.

### Check the line

In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the Zoom number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent transfers the caller to the number of a Zoom queue. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### On Zoom Phone

Use a call queue's direct number. An auto receptionist with a direct number works too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the call queue">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Phone system** › **Call Queues**, choose **Add**, fill in **Site**, **Name**, **Extension Number**, and **Member(s)**, then save. Skip this if the queue exists. For more information, see Zoom's [call queues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064844).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the queue a direct number">
    Go to **Number Management** › **Phone Numbers**, open the number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, pick the queue, and save. The agent hands off to this number. For more information, see Zoom's [managing your numbers](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/number-porting-and-management-field-guide/managing-your-numbers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On Zoom Contact Center

Use a second number whose flow sends callers to a queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a number to Contact Center">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Numbers** › **Phone Numbers**, choose **Add Number**, set **Product** to **Contact Center**, pick the **Country/Region** and **State/Province/Territory**, search, pick a number, and choose **Done**. For more information, see Zoom's [Contact Center numbers](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059452).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a flow that queues">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **CX management** › **Contact Center** › **Flows**, and make a voice flow whose **Route To** widget sends callers to your queue. Leave out greetings and menus, so the caller reaches a person first. For more information, see Zoom's [Customizing the Route To widget](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063774).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give that flow the number">
    Open the new number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, select **Voice Flow**, choose the queue flow, and save. The agent hands off to this number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add the handoff

In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Zoom queue's number. Don't use the number that forwards to the agent. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. On a Zoom Phone forward, the agent only has the Zoom number, so it can't pass the caller's number on.

**Check it:** Once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone, and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- On Zoom Phone, the agent sees the forwarding number, so it can't recognize callers. Zoom Contact Center keeps the caller's number.
- Only a spoken summary can travel with a handoff, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number and a person, not a queue or a menu, answers. Zoom isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.
- When the agent hangs up, the call ends.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Zoom">

- **Zoom's third-party voice agent program** (Contact Center's **Bot** widget, and Zoom Phone's Virtual Agent): **Coming soon**, on both products. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about it.
- **Zoom Phone with your own carrier:** If your carrier is Twilio or Telnyx, send the number to Vapi at the carrier instead and connect it in Vapi for CX. A Twilio number must first come off the Zoom trunk.
- **Port the number out of Zoom** to a carrier Vapi supports.
