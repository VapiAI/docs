Zoom sells two phone products: Zoom Phone, its business phone system, and Zoom Contact Center. In both, a Zoom admin routes calls to the agent's number, and the agent hands callers back by transferring them to the number of a Zoom queue. Neither product accepts a SIP address as a destination, so every call crosses the public phone network. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Zoom is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: you copy the agent's number there, and your Zoom admin does the steps below. Zoom's third-party voice agent programme is **Coming soon** on both products.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. A Zoom Phone auto receptionist, call queue or user, or a Contact Center flow's Route To step, sends calls to the agent's number. On Zoom Phone the agent never sees the caller's number.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To a Zoom queue's number; no caller details travel with the call.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. Zoom sells no outbound connection to third parties; the agent dials from its own line.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. Forwarding only: one Zoom routing setting and a test call.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

Zoom routes calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. The thing in Zoom that forwards (an auto receptionist, call queue or user) needs its own direct number, and Zoom bills calls to an outside number at the plan rate.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and route Zoom calls to that number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent and pick **Zoom**. Copy the agent's number. Nothing else is needed from Zoom for this route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Phone: give the auto receptionist a direct number">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Product configuration › Phone system › **Auto Receptionists** (Classic tab) or **Call Queues** › the name › **Profile** tab: if it has no direct number yet, choose Number(s) › **Add**. Docs: [Zoom: routing targets](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/zoom-phone-bluepaper/configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Phone: route to the agent's number">
    In the Zoom web portal, on the same Profile tab, under **Business Hours**, **Closed Hours**, **Break Hours** and **Holiday Hours** set **Route to** › **Phone Number**, pick the Country/Region and enter the agent's number.

    <Note>Not verified: an outside number on a sub-menu auto receptionist did not ring in one community report, while the main auto receptionist worked. Test it before relying on a sub-menu.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Phone, instead: one user's line">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Settings › Business Hours › **Call Handling** › Edit, set "When a call is not answered" to **Forward to External Contacts**, then **Add Phone Number** with the agent's number. Clear **Require to press 1 before connecting the call**: it is on by default, and the agent cannot press keys.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Zoom's own page.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Contact Center, instead: add a Route To step">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Contact Center Management › **Flows**, open the voice flow and drag in a **Route To** widget. Set **Route to** to OutgoingCall and **Outgoing call target** to Phone, then set the Country/Region, the agent's number and Max Ring Time. Docs: [Zoom: Customizing the Route To widget](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063774).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Zoom's own page. Zoom's page confirms the Caller ID choices and the exits (next step), not these field names.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Contact Center: keep the caller's number">
    In the Route To widget, set **Caller ID** to **Original Caller ID** ("Use the consumer's caller ID name and number"). Wire the **Call Failed** and **Call Not Answered** exits to a queue or voicemail. Save and Publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom Contact Center: assign the flow to a number">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Product configuration › Numbers › **Phone Numbers**: add a number if needed (**Add Number**, Product **Contact Center**, Search, pick one, Done). Then open the number's ellipsis (...), choose **Assign**, tick **Voice Flow**, choose the published flow and Save. Docs: [Zoom: Contact Center numbers](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059452).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the Zoom number from your own phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

    <Note>Not run.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:**

- **Zoom Phone:** Zoom's number, never the caller's, and no setting changes it. Zoom: "Call forwarding to external numbers displays the forwarding source number rather than the original caller ID." So the agent cannot recognise callers, and every call in an experiment lands on one version. Docs: [Zoom Phone caller ID and routing issues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0084977).
- **Zoom Contact Center:** the caller's own number, with Caller ID set to Original Caller ID on the Route To step.

**Check it:** call the Zoom number from a mobile. The agent answers, so the routing reaches it. Read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX: on Contact Center it should be the caller's; on Zoom Phone it is the Zoom number.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. The agent transfers the caller to the number of a Zoom queue. No caller details travel with the call, and Zoom is not told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.

**Zoom Phone:** a call queue's direct number. An auto receptionist or user with a direct number works as the destination too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the call queue">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Product configuration › Phone system › **Call Queues**, choose **Add** and fill Site, Name, Extension Number and Member(s), then Save. Skip this if the queue exists. Docs: [Zoom: call queues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064844).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the queue a direct number">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Number Management › **Phone Numbers**, open the number's ellipsis (...), choose **Assign**, pick the queue and Save. That number is the one the agent hands off to. Optionally set the queue's **Greeting Prompt** and **Audio While Connecting** to Disable, so the caller hears no greeting before a member answers. Docs: [Zoom: managing your numbers](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/number-porting-and-management-field-guide/managing-your-numbers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Zoom Contact Center:** a second number whose flow sends callers to a queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a number to Contact Center">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Product configuration › Numbers › **Phone Numbers**, choose **Add Number**, set Product to **Contact Center**, pick the Country/Region and State, choose Search, pick a number and choose Done. Docs: [Zoom: Contact Center numbers](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059452).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a flow that queues">
    In the Zoom web portal, under Admin Center › Contact Center Management › **Flows**, make a voice flow whose Start widget (**Manage Entry Point**) leads to **Route To**, with Queue as the target. Keep it free of greetings and menus, so the caller reaches a person first.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Zoom's own page.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give that flow the number">
    In the Zoom web portal, open the new number's ellipsis (...), choose **Assign**, tick **Voice Flow**, choose the queue flow and Save. This is the number the agent hands off to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Then, on the agent:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter the Zoom queue's number. It must not be the number that forwards to the agent. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number by default. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number. On a Zoom Phone forward the agent only ever saw the Zoom number, so it cannot pass the caller's number on.

**Does a summary travel:** no. No summary or caller details travel with the call.

**Check it:** call the Zoom number that sends callers to the agent, ask for a person, and let the agent transfer to the queue's number. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when a queue member's Zoom client rings; on Contact Center, also check the engagement shows the caller's number.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

Zoom accepts a call passed on over SIP only from a trunk Zoom itself builds under its third-party voice agent programme; there is no public Zoom SIP address that takes a call from any sender. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.

### Return the call to their system

Not documented: treat the call as released when the agent hangs up. On Zoom Phone, nothing says the auto receptionist gets the call back. On Zoom Contact Center, the Route To step has the exits Call Answered, Call Failed and Call Not Answered, and none for "the outside party hung up". A return exists only inside Zoom's third-party voice agent programme, which is Coming soon.

<Note>Not verified. The test that settles it: a sandbox flow with Call Answered wired to Route To › Queue; let the agent hang up and see whether the caller lands in the queue.</Note>

## Other routes

- **Zoom's third-party voice agent programme** (Contact Center's Bot widget; Zoom Phone's Virtual Agent): Coming soon, on both Zoom Phone and Zoom Contact Center. Zoom staff build the connection; it is not self-serve. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.
- **Zoom Phone with your own carrier** (Business Plus licence; Zoom staff build the trunk): if your carrier is Twilio or Telnyx, the number can go to Vapi at the carrier instead, connected in Vapi for CX, and never touches Zoom until the handoff. A Twilio number must first come off the Zoom trunk. This is a carrier setting, not a Zoom one.
- **Port the number out of Zoom** to a carrier Vapi supports. It takes the line out of Zoom; it is not an integration.
- **Can't:** a SIP address as a Zoom routing target. It is not found on any Zoom page.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. Zoom sells no outbound connection to third parties. The agent dials from its own line and shows its own number.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Typing a SIP address as a routing target in Zoom Phone or a Contact Center Route To step.
- Seeing the caller's number on a Zoom Phone forward: every call shows the Zoom number, so the agent cannot recognise callers and every experiment call lands on one version. Contact Center keeps the caller's number with Original Caller ID.
- Setting up Zoom's programme yourself: Zoom staff build it.
- Turning off the press-1 prompt for call queue calls. Not verified: not read on Zoom's own page.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does the press-1 prompt apply to call queue overflow? Set queue overflow to the agent's number and listen for a prompt.
- Does the caller come back after the agent hangs up on a Contact Center Route To? A sandbox flow with Call Answered wired to a queue.
- Does an outside number ring from a sub-menu auto receptionist? Forward from a sub-menu and call in.
- Is there a cap on simultaneous forwarded calls? Ask Zoom; no Zoom page was found.
- Which licences does the programme need? Ask Zoom's partner team.

## Costs and limits

- A forwarded call uses two legs for its whole length: the caller to Zoom, and Zoom to the agent, which Zoom may bill by usage depending on your Zoom Phone plan. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- A handoff by phone number adds a third leg, from the agent's line back to Zoom.
- The auto receptionist, queue or user that forwards needs its own direct number, which may mean a paid add-on.
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only.
- HIPAA: not researched for Zoom.
