Route calls from Zoom Phone or Zoom Contact Center to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to the number of a Zoom queue. Neither product accepts a SIP address as a destination, so every call crosses the public phone network.

- **Receive calls:** Yes. On Zoom Phone, the agent sees Zoom's number, not the caller's.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Zoom queue's number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No.
- **Return the call to their system:** Coming soon, through Zoom's third-party voice agent program.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Zoom routes calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. What forwards in Zoom (an auto receptionist, call queue or user) needs its own direct number, and Zoom bills calls to an outside number at your plan's rate. Your Zoom admin does the steps in Zoom. Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so connect a number of your own first: see the [overview](/phone-systems).

In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Zoom Phone** or **Zoom Contact Center**, whichever the calls come through, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Then follow the steps for your product, below.

### Zoom Phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give the auto receptionist or queue a direct number">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Phone system** › **Auto Receptionists** (Classic tab) or **Call Queues**, select the name, then the **Profile** tab. If it has no direct number yet, choose **Number(s)** › **Add**. For more information, see Zoom's [routing targets](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/zoom-phone-bluepaper/configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route to the agent's number">
    On the same **Profile** tab, under **Business Hours**, **Closed Hours**, **Break Hours** and **Holiday Hours**, set **Route to** › **Phone Number**, pick the Country/Region and enter the agent's number. Use the main auto receptionist rather than a sub-menu one. If a "press 1" prompt plays on your check call from a call queue, route from the auto receptionist instead: the agent can't press keys.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To forward one user's line instead, go to **Settings** › **Business Hours** › **Call Handling** › **Edit**. Set "When a call is not answered" to **Forward to External Contacts**, then **Add Phone Number** with the agent's number. Clear **Require to press 1 before connecting the call**: it's on by default, and the agent can't press keys.

**The number the agent sees** on Zoom Phone is always Zoom's number, never the caller's, and no setting changes it. For more information, see Zoom's [caller ID and routing issues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0084977).

### Zoom Contact Center

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Route To widget">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **Contact Center Management** › **Flows**, open the voice flow, and drag in a **Route To** widget. Set **Route to** to OutgoingCall and **Outgoing call target** to Phone, then set the Country/Region, the agent's number and Max Ring Time. For more information, see Zoom's [Customizing the Route To widget](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063774).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the caller's number">
    In the **Route To** widget, set **Caller ID** to **Original Caller ID** ("Use the consumer's caller ID name and number"). Connect the **Call Failed** and **Call Not Answered** exits to a queue or voicemail. Save and publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the flow to a number">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Numbers** › **Phone Numbers**. If you need a number, choose **Add Number**, set Product to **Contact Center**, search, pick one and choose **Done**. Then open the number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, select **Voice Flow**, choose the published flow and save. For more information, see Zoom's [Contact Center numbers](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0059452).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** on Zoom Contact Center is the caller's own number, with **Caller ID** set to **Original Caller ID**.

### Check the line

In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the Zoom number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent transfers the caller to the number of a Zoom queue. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### On Zoom Phone

Use a call queue's direct number. An auto receptionist or user with a direct number works too.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the call queue">
    In the Zoom web portal, go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Phone system** › **Call Queues**, choose **Add**, fill in Site, Name, Extension Number and Member(s), and save. Skip this if the queue exists. For more information, see Zoom's [call queues](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064844).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the queue a direct number">
    Go to **Number Management** › **Phone Numbers**, open the number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, pick the queue and save. The agent hands off to this number. For more information, see Zoom's [managing your numbers](https://library.zoom.com/zoom-workplace/zoom-phone/number-porting-and-management-field-guide/managing-your-numbers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On Zoom Contact Center

Use a second number whose flow sends callers to a queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a number to Contact Center">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **Product configuration** › **Numbers** › **Phone Numbers**, choose **Add Number**, set Product to **Contact Center**, pick the Country/Region and State, search, pick a number and choose **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a flow that queues">
    Go to **Admin Center** › **Contact Center Management** › **Flows**, and make a voice flow whose Start widget (**Manage Entry Point**) leads to **Route To**, with Queue as the target. Leave out greetings and menus, so the caller reaches a person first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give that flow the number">
    Open the new number's menu (...), choose **Assign**, select **Voice Flow**, choose the queue flow and save. The agent hands off to this number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add the handoff

In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Zoom queue's number. Don't use the number that forwards to the agent. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. On a Zoom Phone forward, the agent only has the Zoom number, so it can't pass the caller's number on.

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- On Zoom Phone, the agent sees Zoom's number on every call, so it can't recognize callers. Zoom Contact Center keeps the caller's number.
- Zoom can't route to a SIP address.
- No summary or caller details travel with a handoff, and Zoom isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.
- When the agent hangs up, the call ends: Zoom doesn't take the caller back outside its third-party voice agent program.
- A forwarded call uses two Zoom calls for its whole length, which Zoom may bill by usage depending on your plan, plus Vapi minutes. A handoff adds a third call, back to Zoom.
- Zoom sells no outbound connection to third parties, so the agent places calls from its own line.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Zoom">

- **Zoom's third-party voice agent program** (Contact Center's Bot widget, and Zoom Phone's Virtual Agent): **Coming soon**, on both products. Zoom staff build the connection, and it lets Zoom take the caller back after the agent. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about it.
- **Zoom Phone with your own carrier** (Business Plus license, with a trunk Zoom staff build): if your carrier is Twilio or Telnyx, send the number to Vapi at the carrier instead and connect it in Vapi for CX. A Twilio number must first come off the Zoom trunk.
- **Port the number out of Zoom** to a carrier Vapi supports. This takes the line out of Zoom.

</Accordion>
