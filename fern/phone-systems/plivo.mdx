Point a Plivo Zentrunk trunk at Vapi, set the trunk up in Vapi's dashboard (**Connect in Vapi**), then pick the number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai).

- **Receive calls:** Through a Zentrunk inbound trunk pointed at Vapi, as [Plivo's Vapi guide](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice-agents/sip-trunking/integration-guides/vapi) describes. Indian numbers can't be used.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Offered. Plivo accepts a passed-on call only to an address on your own Plivo trunk.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Offered. Plivo accepts a passed-on call only to an address on your own Plivo trunk.
- **Return the call to their system:** Only when a contact center or phone system that takes calls back is linked to the line. See [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).
- **Place calls:** Through the outbound trunk, as Plivo's Vapi guide describes. The caller ID must be a Plivo number.

## Send calls to the agent

You need a Plivo account with Zentrunk and a voice-enabled number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the Origination URI">
    In the [Plivo console](https://cx.plivo.com/sip-trunking), open **Zentrunk (SIP)** (Plivo's own guide calls this menu **SIP Trunking**), then **Inbound Trunks › Origination URI**, and choose **Create New IP URI** (in Plivo's guide, **Add New URI**). Name it `Vapi-Primary` and enter the SIP URI `sip.vapi.ai;transport=udp`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region enter `sip.eu.vapi.ai;transport=udp`. For more information, see Plivo's [Vapi guide](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice-agents/sip-trunking/integration-guides/vapi).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the inbound trunk">
    Under **Inbound Trunks**, choose **Create New Inbound Trunk** and set its **Primary URI** to the one you just made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the number to the trunk">
    Open **Phone Numbers** (in Plivo's guide, **Your Numbers**), then the number. Set **Application Type** to **Zentrunk**, pick the inbound trunk, and choose **Update Number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Vapi's IP addresses">
    Open **Zentrunk (SIP) › Outbound Trunks › IP Access Control List** and choose **Create New IP Group** (in Plivo's guide, **Create New IP Access Control List**). Add `44.229.228.186/32` and `44.238.177.138/32`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region add `63.182.83.170/32`. For more information, see [Vapi's SIP networking guide](/advanced/sip/sip-networking).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the outbound trunk and copy its domain">
    Under **Outbound Trunks**, choose **Create New Outbound Trunk** and select the IP group. Copy the trunk's **Termination SIP Domain**. It looks like `12700668357XXXXXX.zt.plivo.com`. Calls out go through this trunk, and the trunk in Vapi's dashboard uses its domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the trunk and the number in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open **Keys** and choose **Add New SIP Trunk**. Under **Gateway Configuration**, set **IP Address/Domain** to the termination domain and turn on **Allow outbound calls** only.

    Then open **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number**, then **BYO SIP Trunk Number**. Enter the number, pick the trunk under **SIP Trunk Credential**, and add a label.

    Add the number on this trunk. This page doesn't use the separate inbound credential in [Vapi's Plivo SIP guide](/advanced/sip/plivo). For more information, see [Vapi's SIP trunking guide](/advanced/sip/sip-trunk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number in Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, and pick the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number.

**Check it:** when Vapi for CX shows **Call this number from your phone**, call the number from a mobile. The check passes when the agent answers.

**Disconnect.** In Vapi for CX, disconnecting a number set up in Vapi's dashboard only detaches the agent. Nothing changes at Plivo or in Vapi's dashboard.

## Hand callers to your team

On a Plivo trunk, Vapi passes each handoff to Plivo (SIP's own transfer message) rather than placing a new call, for both **Transfer to a phone number** and **Transfer to a SIP address**. Plivo's own page says its trunks accept a passed-on call only to an address on your own Plivo trunk, so Plivo can refuse a handoff to a plain number. The summary rides as a SIP header, so it doesn't reach a phone number. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Limitations

- Indian numbers can't be used with Plivo on Vapi.
- Calls out must show a Plivo number as the caller ID, or Plivo hangs up. A destination in another country needs Geo Permissions, and premium numbers are blocked.
- A Plivo number alone doesn't allow **Return the call to their system**. See [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).
- No audio other than G.711, no call recording on Zentrunk, and no fax (T.38).
- Plivo bills Zentrunk minutes each way (a passed-on call as two call records), and Vapi bills its own minutes. A trunk takes 1 new call a second by default. Plivo adds a charge in a month with more than 20% zero-second calls, or more than 10% calls of six seconds or less.
- Vapi doesn't port numbers.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Plivo">
- **A Plivo Voice API application that sends the call to SIP.** The only way to add caller details as `X-` headers. You build and host the application. Not self-serve.
- **Forward the number to a Vapi number.** A Plivo application. It bills a second Plivo leg.
- Vapi has no Plivo number import or audio streaming.
</Accordion>
