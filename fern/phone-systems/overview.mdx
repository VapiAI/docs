These pages explain how to connect a phone line to an agent in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) and how the agent hands callers to your people. There is one page for each carrier, phone system and contact center. Each page covers both directions: how a caller reaches the agent, and how the agent passes the caller to a person or back to your own system.

## Vapi for CX and Vapi's dashboard

Vapi for CX is the app at [cx.vapi.ai](https://cx.vapi.ai), where you build and run agents. An agent in Vapi for CX is an assistant in Vapi's dashboard. Vapi's dashboard is Vapi's own console, at [dashboard.vapi.ai](https://dashboard.vapi.ai). Both use the same login, and a phone number set up in one shows in the other.

## Connect a phone line

You connect a phone line from the agent's setup checklist, in the step **Connect your phone line**.

- Outside Vapi's EU region, every agent starts with a free starter number from Vapi. Starter numbers are US only. **Keep the starter** finishes the step with that number.
- Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number. They connect Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage by key, a number set up in Vapi's dashboard, or a SIP address.

After a line is connected, Vapi for CX asks you to **Call this number from your phone** (for a SIP address, **Send a call to it now**). The line shows as **Working** when that first call reaches the agent.

## How each integration connects

Each integration connects in one of these ways:

- **Connect in the app.** Vapi for CX connects the line itself: Twilio, Telnyx and Vonage numbers by key, a business line forwarded to the agent, and a SIP address for the agent.
- **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number.** Vapi for CX has no form for this carrier. You set the number up in Vapi's dashboard (**Connect in Vapi**), then pick it in Vapi for CX with **Use a number already in Vapi**.
- **The contact-center screen, then this page.** You forward calls from the phone system or contact center to the agent's number. One screen in Vapi for CX, **A contact center**, covers every platform and links to the platform's page here for the steps on their side.
- **Coming soon.** Not available at launch. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about it.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent: on the **Call handling** tab, the **Handing callers to people** card has **Add a handoff**. Each handoff says when the agent should hand off and where the caller goes. There are three types:

- **Transfer to a phone number:** the agent passes the caller to a number of yours, such as a queue, a team or a person.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** the agent passes the caller to an address on your phone system. Where the line allows it, a summary and named details travel with the call.
- **Return the call to their system:** the agent says its last words and ends its part of the call, and your phone system or contact center carries on with the caller. Nothing tells your system why the agent handed the caller back, so the caller goes to your flow's default path.

Which types a line allows depends on the line the call arrives on. Each page says which apply.

**When "Return the call to their system" is offered.** Only when a phone system or contact center that takes calls back is linked to the agent's line, in **Organization settings**. Carriers have no call flow of their own to take a caller back. Until a platform is linked, the type is grayed with the reason "Link a contact center or phone system to this agent's line in Organization settings first." The type is judged for all of the agent's lines together: when one of them has such a platform, it is offered, but it works only for calls on that line.

**A Twilio number on the agent.** A SIP handoff from a Twilio number is always dialed by Vapi, with no written summary or details. One handoff serves all of the agent's lines, so when the agent also answers on a Twilio number, its SIP handoffs are dialed on every line, with no summary or details.

**The agent leaves after the handoff.** "The agent waits until a person picks up" is not offered on any line. Once the caller is handed off, the agent leaves the call and doesn't come back. Whatever answers the number you hand off to, a person, voicemail or a queue, gets the caller. On some lines the call ends if nothing answers; the Telnyx and Vonage pages say when, under Limitations.

**Check a handoff.** Once the change with the handoff is live, call the line from your own phone and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent", then asks whoever answered to confirm that it arrived.

## The three groups

- **Carriers** sell phone numbers and calls. Their numbers connect to the agent directly, either in Vapi for CX or in Vapi's dashboard.
- **Phone systems** are your business's own phone setup, such as an office phone system or a phone company's line, that forwards calls to the agent.
- **Contact centers** route calls to the agent from their own call flows, and often take the caller back after the agent.

## SIP address

SIP is the internet phone protocol. A SIP address is a phone address that starts with `sip:`, used to send a call over the internet instead of to a phone number.

Vapi for CX can make a SIP address for an agent, in the form `sip:<name>@sip.vapi.ai`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region use `sip.eu.vapi.ai` instead. A phone system that can dial a SIP address can send calls to the agent this way.

- A SIP address always asks the system sending the call to sign in. Vapi for CX makes the username and password and shows them to you once. With **Only let calls in with this username and password** off, any username and password passes, but the sending system must still answer the sign-in. A system that can't answer a sign-in can't use a SIP address.
- Caller details can travel with the call as headers whose names start with `X-`. The agent can read them.
- Vapi can't ring a SIP address itself, so to check one you send a call to it from your phone system.

A trunk is a standing SIP connection between a carrier or phone system and Vapi. Vapi for CX has no trunk form: a trunk is set up in Vapi's dashboard (**Connect in Vapi**).

## UUI

UUI (User-to-User Information) is a small data field that many contact centers pass along with a call, often to say who the caller is or why they called. Vapi doesn't read UUI on calls in to an agent. Where a contact center can send caller details as `X-` headers instead, the agent can read those.
