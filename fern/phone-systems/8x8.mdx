8x8 sells a business phone service, 8x8 Work, and a contact-center product, 8x8 Contact Center. In both, your admin points one script step or menu item at the agent's number. On Contact Center, the script carries on at its **Resume** exit when the agent hangs up, so the caller goes back to your queue on the original call. No SIP address can be typed in anywhere in 8x8. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), 8x8 is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: you copy the agent's number there, and your 8x8 admin does the steps below. An 8x8 partner trunk is **Coming soon**: <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. Contact Center's Forward to External IVR, or an 8x8 Work Auto Attendant, calls the agent's number; one test call should confirm a public number.
- **Transferring out:** Works today. On Contact Center the agent hangs up and the script resumes at Forward to Queue; elsewhere, a transfer to an 8x8 number.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. 8x8 Contact Center numbers take inbound calls only; the agent uses its own line.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. Your admin edits one script or menu. Anything over SIP needs 8x8 professional services.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

8x8 sends calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. On Contact Center, use the script object **Forward to External IVR**, not Forward to External Number: Forward to External Number has no exits, and the call never comes back to the script.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and forward to that number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number, digits only">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose **A contact center**, and pick **8x8** from its list of platforms. Copy the agent's number. For Contact Center, drop the `+`: the field takes digits only with the country code, so in the US that is 1, the area code, then the number (for example `14155550124`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Center: open the channel's script">
    In 8x8 Configuration Manager, under **Channels** › Phone tab › the channel › **Properties**, see which script the channel uses (8x8 assigns a default one if none is picked). Then under **Scripts** › Phone tab, **Edit** that script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Center: add Forward to External IVR">
    In the script, add **Forward to External IVR**. Give it a label (letters and digits, up to 21 characters, unique in the script) and, as the access phone number, the agent's number, digits only. Save the script. Docs: [8x8: External IVR API](https://help.8x8.com/docs/8x8-contact-center-external-ivr-api).

    <Note>Needs a test call: every example 8x8 gives uses its own voice gateway, not a public phone number. The test call below proves it.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="8x8 Work, instead: route the Auto Attendant to the agent">
    In the 8x8 Admin Console, under **Auto Attendants** › Edit › Call Handling Menus › **Main Menu** › **Edit Settings**, set "And then," to **Route call to** and enter the agent's number as the Target, with `+` or `00` and the country code. Save. The Auto Attendant or its site needs its own phone number, or 8x8 refuses the outbound leg. Docs: [8x8: forward Ring Group and Auto Attendant calls](https://help.8x8.com/docs/how-do-i-forward-ring-group-and-auto-attendant-calls-to-an-external-number).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on 8x8's own page (8x8 refusing the outbound leg).</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call your 8x8 number from your own phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

    <Note>Not run.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** not known. 8x8 does not say; only one third-party guide says the caller's own number shows.

<Note>Unknown. One test call settles it: read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX.</Note>

**Check it:** one forwarded call. The agent answers, so the script or Auto Attendant reaches it. Then read the caller's number on the call.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab. On Contact Center, use "Return the call to their system". On 8x8 Work, use "Transfer to a phone number".

**Does a summary travel:** no. Nothing can travel on a phone-number handoff: 8x8 can only be told about a call by its own call id, and the new call into 8x8 gets a new one. If a script or queue greeting answers first, nobody would hear a spoken briefing.

**Check it:** call the agent and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when the queue (or the 8x8 Work number) rings and a person picks up; note the number that person sees.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works today. The agent transfers the caller to a new 8x8 number. Nothing can travel with it, and it adds a third call leg.

**Contact Center:** a second number, made a channel, whose script goes straight to a queue.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make a second number a Contact Center channel">
    In the 8x8 Admin Console (apps.8x8.com), under 8x8 Contact Center › **Channels**, Search for the number, tick it, and choose **Assign to Contact Center**; a green banner confirms. This is for single-tenant accounts; multi-tenant accounts follow 8x8's "Setting Up 8x8 Contact Center Service Channels". Docs: [8x8: assign numbers to Contact Center](https://help.8x8.com/docs/assign-or-unassign-numbers-from-8x8-contact-center).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give that channel a script that queues">
    In 8x8 Configuration Manager, make a script that goes straight to **Forward to Queue** (pick the queue in its Queue name list). Then under **Channels** › Phone tab › the channel › **Properties**, pick that script and Save. Docs: [8x8: assign a script to a channel](https://docs-alt.8x8.com/8x8WebHelp/VCC/configuration-manager-general/content/scriptsassignedchannel.htm).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**8x8 Work:** the number of an Auto Attendant, Ring Group, Call Queue or user.

**Then, on the agent:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number** and enter that number. It must not be the number that forwards to the agent. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number by default. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number.

### Transfer to a SIP address

8x8 gives you no SIP address to send callers to; use "Transfer to a phone number".

### Return the call to their system

Yes, on paper, on Contact Center only. The agent hangs up, and the script carries on at the **Resume** exit of the same Forward to External IVR object. The caller stays on the original call, so your person sees the real caller, 8x8's reporting stays whole, and there is no third leg.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Under Resume, add Forward to Queue">
    In 8x8 Configuration Manager, on the same Forward to External IVR object in the script, add **Forward to Queue** under its **Resume** exit, with the queue name. Optionally put a Test Variable object before it. Save. Docs: [8x8: Data Augmentation](https://docs-alt.8x8.com/8x8WebHelp/VCC/configuration-manager-vovcc/content/data-augmentation.htm).

    Resume runs when the agent hangs up, or when 8x8 cannot connect to the agent's number. If the caller hangs up, the call simply ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: play a short menu at Resume">
    In the same script: nothing tells 8x8 why the agent hung up, so every call the agent ends lands at Resume. Either the agent ends the call only when a person is needed, or Resume plays a short menu such as "press 1 for a person".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Return the call to their system**. The agent ends its call (not a transfer) when a person is needed, and 8x8 carries on at Resume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Yes, on paper. Needs a test call: point Forward to External IVR at the agent's number and Resume at an announcement, have the agent end the call, and listen for the announcement.</Note>

**Number their people see:** the caller's own number: the original call stays intact.

**Does a summary travel:** no. Nothing tells the script why the caller came back.

**On 8x8 Work:** nothing documents a return from an Auto Attendant or Ring Group forward. Treat the call as released, and use "Transfer to a phone number".

## Other routes

- **8x8 partner trunk:** Coming soon. A SIP connection 8x8 builds for a partner. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.
- **8x8 Work Ring Group forwarding:** Admin Console › Ring Groups › Edit › Call forwarding rules › **Forward all calls**, enter the agent's number. The Ring Group or its site needs its own number. It cannot forward to more than one number.
- **8x8 Work user forwarding:** Admin Console › Users › Edit › **Call forwarding rules**, set a rule (all calls, no answer, busy, internet down) to the agent's number with the country code. Needs a licensed user.

  <Note>Not verified: not read on 8x8's own page.</Note>
- **Not recommended:** Contact Center's Forward to External Number. It has no exits, so the call never returns; Forward to External IVR takes the same effort and brings the caller back.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. 8x8 Contact Center numbers take inbound calls only, and 8x8 Work places no calls for Vapi. The agent dials from its own line and shows its own number.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Entering a SIP address anywhere in 8x8 a customer can reach.
- Sending a summary or details with a phone-number handoff.
- Telling the script why the agent hung up: every call the agent ends lands at Resume.
- Taking the caller back on 8x8 Work after an Auto Attendant or Ring Group forward (nothing documents it).

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does Forward to External IVR to a public number, then Resume, work? A sandbox tenant: the object pointed at the agent's number and Resume at an announcement; have the agent end the call and listen.
- Which caller number does the agent see on a forward? One call; read the caller's number on the call.
- Is there a cap on simultaneous forwarded calls? Ask 8x8; not found.

## Costs and limits

- Phone-number routes use two call legs for the whole call; ask your 8x8 account team whether forwarding to outside numbers is charged on your plan. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- Return by Resume adds no third leg; a handoff by phone number does.
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only.
- HIPAA: not researched for 8x8.
