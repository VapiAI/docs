Send your Bandwidth numbers to Vapi over a SIP trunk (a standing internet phone connection between Bandwidth and Vapi), set the trunk up in Vapi's dashboard, then pick the number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). Bandwidth is **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number**: Vapi for CX has no Bandwidth form.

## At a glance

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Offered. Vapi passes the handoff to Bandwidth.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Offered. Vapi passes the handoff to Bandwidth.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Yes, with a Bandwidth SIP realm and credential, which Bandwidth may have to turn on for your account.

## Receiving calls

You need a Bandwidth Universal Platform account with SIP trunking, a number, and the right to create Voice Configuration Packages.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy Bandwidth's signaling IP address">
    In the Bandwidth App, open **Account › Trunk Group Configurations**. Copy the standard SIP (Orig/Term) address, not the TLS, SIP Auth or Registrar one. If more than one standard SIP address is listed, copy each: the trunk needs a gateway for every one. For more information, see Bandwidth's [Vapi guide](https://dev.bandwidth.com/docs/voice/integrations/vapi/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the SIP trunk in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open **Keys** and choose **Add New SIP Trunk**. Under **Gateway Configuration**, set **IP Address/Domain** to the signaling IP, **Port** to 5060 and **Netmask** to 32, and turn on **Allow inbound calls**. If you copied more than one signaling IP, add a gateway for each. With one missing, calls work sometimes and fail other times.

    The trunk's **Credential ID** makes the address Bandwidth sends calls to: `<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region use `<credential id>.sip.eu.vapi.ai`.

    Keep this one trunk for both directions. For calls out, you add a second gateway to it later. Don't make a second trunk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Voice Configuration Package">
    In the Bandwidth App, open **Service Management › Voice Configuration** and choose **Create New** (Bandwidth's ElevenLabs guide calls it **Add Package**). Name it, set the route type to **Route to Domain Name**, and enter the trunk's address from the step above. Bandwidth's [Vapi guide](https://dev.bandwidth.com/docs/voice/integrations/vapi/) gives this menu path, and its [create a package](https://dev.bandwidth.com/docs/universal-platform/create-a-vcp/) page makes the same package through the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the numbers to the package">
    In the Bandwidth App, open each number's **Voice settings**, assign it to the package, and confirm that inbound voice is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each number in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number**, then **BYO SIP Trunk Number**. Enter each number in full international format with a leading `+`, pick the trunk under **SIP Trunk Credential**, and add a label. For more information, see [Vapi's SIP trunking guide](/advanced/sip/sip-trunk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number in Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, and pick the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    Call one of the numbers from your own phone. The agent answers, which shows that the package, the signaling IP and the number line up.

    If nothing arrives, the most common cause is the signaling IP: Vapi refuses a call from an address that isn't on the trunk's gateway list, and Bandwidth then reports a 550 error. Vapi keeps no record of a refused call, so Vapi for CX can only say that nothing arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. The dialed number arrives with a leading `+`.

**Disconnect.** In Vapi for CX, disconnecting a number set up in Vapi's dashboard only detaches the agent. Nothing changes at Bandwidth or in Vapi's dashboard.

## Handing callers to people

On a Bandwidth trunk, Vapi passes each handoff to Bandwidth (SIP's own transfer message) rather than placing a new call, and Bandwidth decides how it places the call and which number shows to whoever answers. The summary rides as a SIP header, so it doesn't reach a phone number. For where handoffs are set and how to check one, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### Transfer to a phone number

Vapi for CX offers this type on a Bandwidth line, and Vapi passes the handoff to Bandwidth. Bandwidth's Vapi guide doesn't cover transfers, and its ElevenLabs guide passes calls to an address on Bandwidth rather than to a plain number.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Vapi for CX offers this type on a Bandwidth line, and Vapi passes the handoff to Bandwidth. Bandwidth's ElevenLabs guide passes calls to an address written `sip:+1…@bandwidth.com`, where `+1…` is the number to ring. When Bandwidth rings a phone number this way, the summary and named details may not reach the person who answers: they ride as SIP headers.

### Return the call to their system

Not available on a Bandwidth number: Bandwidth has no call flow to take the caller back. For more information, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Other routes

- **Older accounts (Locations, also called SIP Peers).** Not possible until the account moves to the Universal Platform: Vapi would have no way to tell which customer a call is for.
- **A Programmable Voice (BXML) application that sends the call to SIP.** The only way to add caller details as headers. You build and host the application. Not self-serve.
- **Forward the number to a Vapi number.** A number feature at Bandwidth. It adds a second Bandwidth leg.
- Vapi has no Bandwidth number import or audio streaming.

## Calls out

Calls out go through a realm gateway on the same trunk.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get SIP credentials turned on">
    In the Bandwidth App, open **Account › SIP Credentials**. If the tab is missing, or adding a realm fails, ask Bandwidth support or your account manager to enable SIP credentials on the account. For more information, see Bandwidth's [realms and credentials](https://dev.bandwidth.com/docs/account/sipRegistrar) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a realm and a SIP credential">
    Under **SIP Credentials**, choose **Manage Realms**, then **Add**, and pick the trunk group. Then add a SIP credential with a **Username**, **Password** and the **Realm**. Bandwidth generates the realm's host name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the realm to the same trunk in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open the trunk under **Keys**. Add the realm's host name as a second gateway with **Allow outbound calls** only and **Port** 5060. Under **Authentication (Optional)**, enter the username and password. The username is bare, with no realm added to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- Signing in by IP address alone doesn't work for calls out: Vapi's addresses are shared by every Vapi customer.
- The caller ID must be a number on the same Bandwidth account as the realm.
- Numbers go out in full international format.
- If a call out fails with a 500 error, the request carried no SIP credential, or the account isn't enabled for SIP credentials. A 403, or a repeated 407, means the username, password or realm host name doesn't match.

## Costs and limits

- Bandwidth bills trunk minutes each way, and Vapi bills its own minutes.
- A call in is one Bandwidth leg.
- One trunk carries calls both ways. More are bought through your Bandwidth account manager.
- If Bandwidth moves your trunk group, its signaling IP changes, and calls in stop until you edit the gateway in Vapi's dashboard.
- The Build trial reaches only your verified mobile number, in the US and Canada, with five calls at once and 30 minutes a call.

## Limitations

- Calls out need SIP credentials, which Bandwidth support or sales may have to turn on.
- Calls out can't sign in by IP address alone.
- Older Locations (SIP Peers) accounts can't connect until they move to the Universal Platform.
- A refused call leaves no record in Vapi, so Vapi for CX can't say which address was refused.
- Vapi doesn't port numbers.

For what a SIP address and UUI are, see the [overview](/phone-systems).
