Some phone systems send a call's audio to an outside service over a WebSocket, a live two-way connection over the web: Twilio Media Streams, Telnyx media streaming, Vonage's WebSocket connection, Genesys Cloud's Audio Connector, Asterisk's WebSocket channel, and custom dialers. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), these systems are **Coming soon**. None of them can be pointed at the agent directly today: each call would need a small bridge that someone builds and hosts. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name the phone system you use.

If your calls come in on a Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage number, you don't need a WebSocket at all: connect the number to Vapi for CX the normal way ([Twilio](/phone-systems/twilio), [Telnyx](/phone-systems/telnyx), [Vonage](/phone-systems/vonage)). If you use Genesys Cloud, see [Genesys Cloud](/phone-systems/genesys).

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Coming soon. Nothing can be pointed at the agent directly; each call needs a bridge.
- **Transferring out:** Coming soon. Your phone system would do the transfer, not the agent.
- **Calls out:** Coming soon. Your dialer would place the call and open the stream.
- **Self-serve:** Coming soon. Only connecting a Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage number is self-serve today.

<Note>
Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.
</Note>

## Receiving calls

Coming soon. A WebSocket from your phone system can't reach the agent by itself: every call needs a bridge in between, and Vapi for CX doesn't offer one. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name your phone system.

For a Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage number, connect the number instead and Vapi takes the call itself, with no bridge: see [Twilio](/phone-systems/twilio), [Telnyx](/phone-systems/telnyx) or [Vonage](/phone-systems/vonage).

## Handing callers to people

Coming soon. On a WebSocket call the agent can't transfer the caller itself; your phone system would do it. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a>.

### Transfer to a phone number

Doesn't apply. Vapi refuses its own transfer on a WebSocket call; your phone system would make the transfer instead.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Doesn't apply. A WebSocket call has no SIP, so there is nothing to transfer over.

### Return the call to their system

Coming soon. By design your phone system never lets go of the caller, so it would carry on with its next step when the agent ends its part. Nothing would tell it why. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a>.

## Other routes

If your phone system also speaks SIP (Asterisk, for example, or Genesys Cloud with its own trunk), it can send calls to the agent over SIP instead, with no bridge: see [Your own phone system](/phone-systems/own-phone-system).

## Calls out

Coming soon. Your dialer would place the call and open the stream; the agent would never dial, so Vapi's caller ID and voicemail detection wouldn't apply. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a>.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Pointing a phone system straight at the agent over a WebSocket. Each call needs its own start step first.
- The agent transferring the caller, or hearing keypad presses, on a WebSocket call.
- Seeing these calls as a phone line in Vapi for CX. The call has no number.
- Keeping each caller on one version in an experiment. Without a caller's number, calls are split at random.

Not yet known:

- Which phone systems the customers who asked actually run. That decides whether Vapi builds a bridge.
- How much delay a bridge adds to the call.

## Costs and limits

- There is one phone call, and it stays on your phone system. You pay your phone system for the call and the stream, and Vapi for the agent.
- Genesys bills for the time its Audio Connector is open.
- These calls count toward your Vapi limit on calls at once.

For how a phone line, a SIP address and UUI fit together in Vapi for CX, see [Connect your phone line](/phone-systems).
