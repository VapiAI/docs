Webex Contact Center is Cisco's cloud contact center. Its Flow Designer can transfer a caller to an outside number. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Webex Contact Center is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: it connects by phone number. A Bridged Transfer (or a Blind Transfer, where your tenant lacks Bridged Transfer) calls the agent's number. On tenants with Bridged Transfer, your flow can take the caller back when the agent ends its part. Cisco's own virtual-agent connector is Coming soon: [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales).

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. A Flow Designer transfer, Bridged or Blind, calls the agent's number.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. By phone number today; back into your flow only with Bridged Transfer, and that needs a test call.
- **Calls out:** Not applicable. Webex is not a carrier the agent can dial through.
- **Self-serve:** Partly. Your flow author adds one activity and publishes.

<Note>
Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.
</Note>

## Receiving calls

Webex keeps the caller's call and places a second call to the agent's number. Cisco's bundled phone service, Webex Contact Center PSTN, dials the contiguous US and Canada only.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and send Webex's calls to that number.

Read from Cisco's documentation on Nov 22, 2026. Not run.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent, choose **Connect your phone line**, and pick **Cisco Webex**. Copy the agent's number (for example +14155550124).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your tenant has Bridged Transfer">
    In Webex Control Hub, look in **Service Details** for the media platform "Real Time Media Service", or look for **Bridged Transfer** in Flow Designer's **Activity Library**. Cisco: "Available on Next-Generation Voice Media platforms (VPOP and Webex Calling) only." Without it, use **Blind Transfer** in the next steps; your flow then can't take the caller back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the flow">
    In Webex Control Hub, go to **Services › Contact Center › Flows › Manage Flows** and open the flow that answers the number. See Cisco's [Flow Designer](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nhovcy4/Webex-Contact-Center-Flow-Designer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the transfer activity">
    In Webex Flow Designer, drag **Bridged Transfer** (or **Blind Transfer**) from the **Activity Library** into the branch that should go to the agent. Bridged Transfer must come before any **Queue Contact**. Under **Transfer Dial Number**, choose **Specific Dial Number** and paste the agent's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise the timeout">
    In Webex Flow Designer, raise **Transfer Timeout Settings › Timeout** from its default of 10 seconds to about 30. Cisco: "The call is terminated if not answered in time."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire the error path and publish">
    In Webex Flow Designer, connect **Undefined Error** to a prompt and the route to a person, then publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the entry point uses the flow">
    In Webex Control Hub, under **Contact Center › Channels › Entry Point**, check that the entry point for the number runs this flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** unknown. Cisco's guide doesn't say which number a Blind or Bridged Transfer presents, the caller's or the entry point's. If Webex shows the entry point's number, an experiment on the agent would put every call on one version.

<Note>
Unknown. One test call settles it.
</Note>

**Check it:** in Vapi for CX, the check says "Call this number from your phone". Call the entry point's number. The line is marked working when the first call reaches the agent. Then open that call and read the caller's number.

## Handing callers to people

On tenants with Bridged Transfer, use "Return the call to their system". On tenants with Blind Transfer, which releases the call, use "Transfer to a phone number".

**Does a summary travel:** no. Nothing travels over the phone network but the call.

**Warm handoff (the agent waits with the caller):** doesn't apply. The agent can wait with the caller only on a Twilio number and only for a person's own line; a Webex number is answered by a flow first, and a return makes no transfer.

**Check it:** once the agent's version with the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row in Vapi for CX. Call the agent from your phone (for a return, the entry point's number) and ask for a person. The check shows "Handoff sent" when the agent hands over or ends its part, then asks whoever answered to confirm. To hear a returned call carry on, put a **Play Message** after the Bridged Transfer.

### Transfer to a phone number

Works today. The agent calls a Webex number mapped to an entry point whose flow goes straight to **Queue Contact**. If the caller reached the agent through Webex, this makes a second Webex interaction and leaves extra phone legs up.

Read from Cisco's documentation on Nov 22, 2026. Not run.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map a number to a queue's entry point">
    In Webex Control Hub, under **Contact Center › Channels › Entry Point**, map a Webex number to an entry point whose flow goes straight to **Queue Contact** (**Static Queue** or **Variable Queue**). It must skip the branch that sends callers to the agent, or the call loops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff**. Say when the agent should hand over, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Webex number. Under **More options**, choose that their people see the caller's number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the caller's own number on a Twilio line, or when the handoff is set to show it. Otherwise the agent's number.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Not available at launch. Webex reads details sent with a call only on tenants that use Webex Calling with their own Local Gateway, and no published example of that route with Vapi was found.

### Return the call to their system

Needs a test call: yes with **Bridged Transfer** on the newer platform, no with **Blind Transfer**. With Bridged Transfer, the agent ends its part of the call and your Webex flow carries on to the activity after it. Cisco: "When the third-party ends the call, the call flow continues and reengages as required, such as queuing the call to an agent." Nothing tells your flow why or which queue, so every returned caller follows the same path and may be asked again. Where the reason matters, add a separate "Transfer to a phone number" per queue.

Read from Cisco's documentation on Nov 22, 2026. Not run.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put Queue Contact after Bridged Transfer">
    In Webex Flow Designer, after the **Bridged Transfer** activity that calls the agent, add **Queue Contact** and pick the queue with **Static Queue** or **Variable Queue**. Publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff**. Say when the agent should hand back and choose **Return the call to their system**. There is nowhere to send it: the agent ends its part of the call. It works on every kind of line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the same call; Webex kept it and the flow queues it. Cisco's guide doesn't say what the person's desktop shows.

## Other routes

- **Bring Your Own Virtual Agent, Cisco's connector programme** (calls in): Coming soon. Needs work at Vapi, Cisco's approval and Cisco's paid virtual-agent add-on. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales).
- **Webex Calling with your own Local Gateway** (calls in): Not available at launch. Built from documented pieces, but no published example was found.
- **Unified CCX or CCE on premises, through Cisco CUBE** (calls in): Not available at launch. No per-product steps.
- **Webex Calling forwarding or an auto attendant** (calls in): Not verified. Cisco's articles on these weren't read.
- **Vapi as the Webex Calling trunk itself** (calls in): Can't. Webex accepts only listed devices on its trunks.

## Calls out

Not applicable. Webex hands calls to the agent; it isn't the agent's carrier. Calls the agent places go out on a phone line connected in Vapi for CX.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Vapi as a Webex Calling trunk.
- Details sent with a call on Cisco's own phone service, Cloud Connected PSTN or VPOP. Cisco: supported "only for organizations using Webex Calling with Local Gateway".
- A return to the flow after Blind Transfer, or Bridged Transfer after Queue Contact or in an outbound flow.

Not yet known:

- Which number Webex shows the agent as the caller: the caller's or the entry point's. One call settles it, and whether an experiment would put every call on one version.
- Whether Bridged Transfer resumes cleanly, and how fast, when the agent hangs up. The same call, with a **Play Message** after the activity, settles it.

## Costs and limits

- A Blind or Bridged Transfer adds a call to the agent on your Webex phone service for the whole conversation, on top of the caller's call and Vapi minutes.
- A return adds no call. A handoff to a Webex number makes a second Webex interaction and leaves extra calls up.
- Bridged Transfer's timeout defaults to 10 seconds; raise it.
- Cisco's bundled phone service dials North America only.
- The agent's part of the call is not in Webex's recording.
- HIPAA: not researched for Webex.
