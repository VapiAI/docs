Send calls from a Webex Contact Center flow to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). On tenants with Bridged Transfer, Cisco's documentation describes your flow taking the caller back when the agent ends its part.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a **Bridged Transfer** or **Blind Transfer** activity
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Webex number mapped to a queue
- **Transfer to a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address):** Not with these steps
- **Return the call to their system:** described in Cisco's [Flow Designer](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nhovcy4/Webex-Contact-Center-Flow-Designer) guide with **Bridged Transfer**; no with **Blind Transfer**

## Send calls to the agent

With Bridged Transfer, Webex keeps the caller's call and places a second call to the agent's number; Blind Transfer hands the call over. Cisco's bundled phone service, Webex Contact Center PSTN, dials the contiguous lower 48 US states and Canada only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, in the agent's **Connect your phone line** step, choose **Connect your phone system**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Cisco Webex Contact Center**, and copy the number under **In Cisco Webex Contact Center, send calls to this number** (for example `+14155550124`). If the agent has several numbers, pick one under **Forward to** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that your tenant has Bridged Transfer">
    Look for **Bridged Transfer** in Flow Designer's **Activity Library**. Cisco offers it on Next-Generation Voice Media platforms (VPOP and Webex Calling) only. Without it, use **Blind Transfer** in the next steps. Your flow then can't take the caller back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the flow">
    Go to **Contact Center › Customer Experience › Flows** and open the flow that answers the number. For more information, see Cisco's [Flow Designer](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/nhovcy4/Webex-Contact-Center-Flow-Designer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the transfer activity">
    In Flow Designer, drag **Bridged Transfer** (or **Blind Transfer**) from the **Activity Library** into the branch that should go to the agent. Bridged Transfer must come before any **Queue Contact**. Under **Transfer Dial Number**, choose **Specific Dial Number** and paste the agent's number. Cisco's guide describes a Bridged Transfer flow carrying on to the activity after it when the outside call ends. Every call the agent ends carries on there, including calls where the caller had finished, so choose a next activity that suits both kinds of call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise the timeout">
    On a Bridged Transfer, raise **Transfer Timeout Settings › Timeout** from its default of 10 seconds to about 30 seconds. If the agent doesn't answer within this time, the transfer fails with failure code 3 (**No answer**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the error path and publish">
    Connect **Undefined Error** to a prompt and your route to a person, then publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the entry point uses the flow">
    In Webex Control Hub, under **Services › Contact Center › Customer Experience › Channels**, check that the entry point for the number runs this flow. For more information, see Cisco's [Set up voice channels](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/2dputx/Set-up-voice-channels-for-Webex-Contact-Center).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** Cisco's guide doesn't say whether a Blind or Bridged Transfer shows the caller's number or the entry point's.

**Check it:** on the contact-center screen, choose **Check the line**, then call the entry point's number. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

## Hand callers to your team

On tenants with Bridged Transfer, use **Return the call to their system**. With Blind Transfer, which lets go of the call, use **Transfer to a phone number**. Where the reason matters, add a **Transfer to a phone number** handoff for each queue.

### Return the call to their system

Cisco's documentation describes how this works with Bridged Transfer: the agent ends its part of the call and your Webex flow carries on to the activity after it, on the same call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put Queue Contact after Bridged Transfer">
    In Webex Flow Designer, after the **Bridged Transfer** activity that calls the agent, add **Queue Contact** and pick the queue with **Static Queue** or **Variable Queue**. Publish the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Name it, say when the agent should use it and choose **Return the call to their system**. There is no number to enter. This handoff type is offered on any kind of line once that line is linked to Cisco Webex Contact Center. To link the line, pick **Cisco Webex Contact Center** under **Your contact center or phone system** and choose **Check the line**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Transfer to a phone number

The agent calls a Webex number mapped to an entry point whose flow goes straight to **Queue Contact**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map a number to a queue's entry point">
    In Webex Control Hub, under **Services › Contact Center › Customer Experience › Channels**, map a Webex number to an entry point whose flow goes straight to **Queue Contact** (**Static Queue** or **Variable Queue**). That flow must skip the branch that sends callers to the agent, or the call loops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff**. Name it, say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Webex number. Under **More options**, set **Their people see** to **The caller's number** if your line offers it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** once a handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the entry point's number from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over or ends its part. To hear a returned call carry on, put a **Play Message** after the Bridged Transfer.

## Limitations

- Webex isn't told why the agent handed the caller back, so every returned caller follows the same path and may be asked again.
- Your flow can't take the caller back after Blind Transfer, or after a Bridged Transfer placed after **Queue Contact** or in an outbound flow.
- The call to the agent is a second call on your Webex phone service, and Vapi minutes are on top.
