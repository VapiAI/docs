This page is for businesses that run their own phone system: Asterisk, FreePBX, 3CX, Cisco Unified CM behind Cisco CUBE, Mitel MiVoice Business, or any SBC (the box at the edge of a phone network that accepts SIP calls). In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), your own phone system is **Connect in the app**: Vapi for CX makes a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address) for the agent, and your phone system sends calls to it. Forwarding chosen calls to the agent's phone number works on any phone system. A SIP trunk is **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number**.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. Your phone system forwards or transfers chosen calls to the agent's number; sending them to its SIP address works with a caveat.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To an extension's or queue's outside number; the agent's number shows unless set.
- **Calls out:** Works with a caveat. Over a trunk only: Vapi sends the call to your system, which must allow it and route it on.
- **Self-serve:** Works with a caveat. The SIP address is made in Vapi for CX. Your phone-system admin does your side; a trunk is set up in Vapi's dashboard.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

The agent's SIP address takes calls from any phone system that can dial a SIP address and answer a sign-in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the agent's SIP address">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent and choose a SIP address for this agent. Vapi for CX makes an address in the form `sip:<name>@sip.vapi.ai` and shows it with **Copy**. Organizations in Vapi's EU region get `sip:<name>@sip.eu.vapi.ai`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the sign-in">
    In Vapi for CX: a SIP address always asks the system sending the call to sign in. Vapi for CX makes a username and a password, shown here as `<username>` and `<password>`, and shows them once: copy them now. With the password switch off, any answer passes, but your system must still answer the sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send calls to the address">
    In your phone system: send the calls the agent should take to the SIP address, signing in with the username and password. On Asterisk and FreePBX this is the simplest SIP route: Asterisk answers the sign-in with an `outbound_auth` setting on the endpoint. Caller details can travel as headers whose names start with `X-`; the agent can read them.

    A system that signs in only by IP address cannot use a SIP address. Forward to the agent's number instead (below), or use a trunk (under [Other routes](#other-routes)).

    <Note>Not run: no phone system has sent a call to a Vapi for CX SIP address yet.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a call to it now">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Send a call to it now**, then send one call to the address from your phone system. Vapi cannot ring a SIP address itself, so Vapi for CX waits for the call you send. The line is marked as working when that call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Forward to the agent's number instead.** Every phone system can forward or transfer chosen calls to the agent's phone number (its starter number, or a Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage number connected in Vapi for CX). Each forwarded call uses two of your outside lines, and no headers travel.

- **Asterisk:** in the dialplan, `Dial()` to your outside trunk with the agent's number. To get the call back when the agent hangs up, add option `g` (see [Return the call to their system](#return-the-call-to-their-system)). Docs: [Asterisk: Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/).
- **FreePBX:** Asterisk underneath, so the dialplan route applies. Its forwarding screen was not read.
- **3CX:** 3CX needs an outbound rule that sends the agent's number out your trunk (Admin › outbound rules). The outbound-rule screens were not read. Docs: [3CX: Configuring SIP Trunks](https://www.3cx.com/docs/manual/sip-trunks/).
- **Cisco Unified CM:** the setting that forwards a call to an outside number was not read.
- **Mitel MiVoice Business:** the setting that forwards a call to an outside number was not read.

<Note>Not verified: not read on FreePBX's, 3CX's, Cisco's or Mitel's own page. Their pages on forwarding a call to an outside number were not read.</Note>

**Number the agent sees:** it depends on your system. Over a SIP address, whatever your system puts in the From header. On a forwarded call, the caller's own number if your phone system and its carrier pass it on, otherwise your business's main number. When every call shows the main number, every call in an experiment lands on one version.

<Note>Unknown per system. Ring your business line from another phone and read the caller on the call in Vapi for CX.</Note>

**Check it:** the first call arrives at the agent. It shows that a call arrived, not why an earlier one was refused: Vapi cannot show why a call was refused.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** not on a call to a phone number. A spoken summary to whoever answers is possible only when the agent's line is a Twilio number.

**Check it:** call the agent and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent", and the call reaches the outside number.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. It works from any line and needs nothing on your system beyond an outside number that reaches the right people.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have an outside number that reaches your people">
    In your phone system: an outside number for the extension, ring group or queue that should take the caller.

    - **Asterisk:** a dialplan route for that number. Docs: [Asterisk: Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/).
    - **FreePBX:** an inbound route: Connectivity → Inbound Routes › Add Inbound Route › **Description**, **DID Number** (blank means any), **Set Destination**. The DID must match the format your trunk sends.
    - **3CX:** a DID on your trunk, assigned to its destination. For an IVR, ring group, queue or digital receptionist: Admin › Call Handling › the system extension › **Assigned DID Number(s)**. For a person: Admin › Users › the user › General tab › **Assigned DID Number(s)**. A DID left unassigned goes to the trunk's default route. Docs: [3CX: Configuring SIP Trunks](https://www.3cx.com/docs/manual/sip-trunks/).
    - **Cisco Unified CM:** the trunk's inbound-call fields were not on the pages read; Cisco's guide sends readers to its Online Help.
    - **Mitel MiVoice Business:** the forms **Trunk Attributes** (how incoming digits reach an answer point) and **SIP Peer Profile Assignment by Incoming DID**. From Mitel's configuration note for one provider, so option values are examples. Docs: [Mitel: configuration note](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/2025-05/Technical%20Configuration%20Notes%20for%20NISC%20IVR%20with%20MiVB%20-%20HO3860_0.pdf).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on FreePBX's or Cisco's own page. FreePBX's inbound routes and Cisco's inbound-call fields were not read.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter that outside number. Use a number that does not forward back to the agent. On the starter number, Vapi dials the number and joins the two calls.

    Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number. From the starter number or a trunk number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number. Your carrier may refuse a number your trunk does not own. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call, caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

### Transfer to a SIP address

Vapi for CX offers **Transfer to a SIP address** on a SIP-address line: the agent passes the caller to an address on your phone system, and a summary and named details can travel with the call as `X-` headers. This page gives no phone-system steps for it yet: how Asterisk, Cisco CUBE and the other systems take such a call and read its headers has not been walked. It needs a test call with a SIP trace on each system.

### Return the call to their system

Works with a caveat: Asterisk and FreePBX only, Yes, on paper.

Vapi for CX offers this type only when a phone system that takes calls back is linked to the agent's line. A SIP address made under **Another carrier or my own phone system** links none, so on that line alone this type is greyed, with the reason and a link to Organization settings. Once your phone system is linked, add a handoff of this type in the **Handing callers to people** card.

If your system sent the call to the agent with `Dial(...,g)`, the dialplan carries on at its next step when the agent's part of the call ends, so the caller can go on to a queue. Your system sees only that the call was answered (`DIALSTATUS=ANSWER`), not why the agent handed back. From then on every call the agent ends carries on at that next step, also a call where the caller had finished, so send it somewhere that suits both. Docs: [Asterisk: Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/).

<Note>Yes, on paper. FreePBX's screen for this was not read. Needs a test call: one call with `Dial(...,g)` on Asterisk.</Note>

On 3CX, Cisco Unified CM and Mitel, on a plain trunk call a hang-up from the far side normally ends the call. A call your system forwarded over the public phone network has been let go, and nothing returns.

<Note>Not verified: not read on 3CX's, Cisco's or Mitel's own page. None of them was read on whether a call comes back to it.</Note>

## Other routes

- **SIP trunk (Connect in Vapi, then pick the number).** A trunk is a standing SIP connection between your phone system and Vapi. Vapi for CX has no trunk form: you set the trunk up in Vapi's dashboard, then pick the number in Vapi for CX. It needs a fixed public IPv4 address for your system or its SBC, and the G.711 audio format. Vapi's [SIP trunk guide](/advanced/sip/sip-trunk) and [SIP networking guide](/advanced/sip/sip-networking) give the addresses, ports and firewall rules.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Add the trunk">
      In Vapi's dashboard, under **Keys**, choose **Add New SIP Trunk**. Add a gateway with your phone system's fixed public IPv4 address and turn on **Allow inbound calls** (netmask /24 to /32).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add each number">
      In Vapi's dashboard, under **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number**, then **BYO SIP Trunk Number**: one for each number or extension your system will dial. For an extension such as `7001`, tick **Allow non-E164 phone numbers**. Set **SIP Trunk Credential** to the trunk you added.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Pick the number">
      In Vapi for CX, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, pick the number, then **Call this number from your phone**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Send calls to the trunk">
      In your phone system or SBC: send calls to `sip:<number>@<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`, where `<credential id>` is the ID of the trunk you added under **Keys** in Vapi's dashboard (EU: `<credential id>.sip.eu.vapi.ai`), on port 5060 over UDP or TCP, or 5061 over TLS. Put the dialled number in the To user and the real caller in From, offer G.711, send keypad tones as RFC 2833, allow Vapi's addresses and media ports from the networking guide, and put caller details in `X-` headers.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>The names in Vapi's dashboard above were not clicked through for this page.</Note>

  Your side, per system, as far as each was read:

  - **Asterisk (chan_pjsip):** the trunk is an `endpoint`, an `aor` with `contact=sip:<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai:5060`, and an `identify` section with `match=` Vapi's two addresses, so calls from Vapi are recognised; `disallow=all`, `allow=ulaw`. Our advice: set `direct_media=no` so the audio stays with Asterisk. Caller details: add `X-` headers with `Set(PJSIP_HEADER(add,X-MyHeader)=value)` in a pre-dial handler. Docs: [Asterisk: res_pjsip examples](https://docs.asterisk.org/Configuration/Channel-Drivers/SIP/Configuring-res_pjsip/res_pjsip-Configuration-Examples/), [Asterisk: PJSIP_HEADER](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Functions/PJSIP_HEADER/).
  - **FreePBX:** Asterisk underneath, so the same settings apply. Version 16 and older: Connectivity → Trunks › Add Trunk › **Add SIP (chan_sip) Trunk**; the **General** tab (Trunk Name, Outbound Caller ID), **Dialplan Manipulation Rules**, and **SIP Settings** › Outgoing (Trunk Name, PEER Details). Version 17: **Add SIP (chan_pjsip) Trunk**, with the tabs General and pjsip Settings. Docs: [Sangoma: SIP trunk set up manually in FreePBX](https://sangomakb.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SAF/pages/15925762/Setting+up+Standard+SIPStation+Manually+in+FreePBX).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on FreePBX's own page. The version 17 screens were not read there.</Note>
  - **3CX (V20):** Admin › Voice & Chat › **Add Trunk**. Name the trunk and set the default route (where any DID not assigned to an extension goes). Under SIP Trunk Details, pick the country and the provider; with the generic template you choose the sign-in yourself, by registration (an authentication ID and password) or by IP address. Enter the main trunk number, add DIDs under **DID Numbers** › **+Add**, set **Options** › **Limit to**, and **Save**. A green dot means registered; 3CX has a built-in SIP Trunk checker. Self-hosted installs should run the firewall check first. Docs: [3CX: Configuring SIP Trunks](https://www.3cx.com/docs/manual/sip-trunks/).
  - **Cisco:** Vapi connects to CUBE (the box between Unified CM and outside SIP trunks), not to Unified CM. On CUBE, add Vapi's addresses to its **ip address trusted list**, send keypad tones with `dtmf-relay rtp-nte` on the dial-peer, and use a SIP profile to add caller-detail headers. On Unified CM (Release 14), with a **Device Pool** in place: Device › Device Settings › **SIP Profile**; System › Security › **SIP Trunk Security Profile** › Add New; then Device › Trunk › Add New › **Trunk Type** = SIP Trunk › **Protocol Type** › **Destination Address** › **SIP Trunk Security Profile** › **SIP Profile**. To send calls to the trunk: Call Routing › Route/Hunt › **Route Pattern** (Route Pattern, Gateway/Route). The trunk's inbound-call fields were not on the pages read. Docs: [Cisco CUBE: overview](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/ios-xml/ios/voice/cube/ios-xe/config/ios-xe-book/voi-cube-overview.html), [Cisco Unified CM system configuration guide, Release 14](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/voice_ip_comm/cucm/admin/14/systemConfig/cucm_b_system-configuration-guide-14su2/cucm_b_system-configuration-guide-14_chapter_01000.html).
  - **Mitel MiVoice Business (9.0 SP1):** the forms, in order: License and Option Selection (SIP Trunk Licences); Class of Service Options Assignment; Network Element Assignment (the far end); Trunk Attributes; SIP Peer Profile; SIP Peer Profile Assignment by Incoming DID; ARS Digit Modification Plans; ARS Routes (routing medium SIP Trunk); ARS Route Lists; ARS Digits Dialed. The form names are Mitel's; the option values in Mitel's note are examples for one provider. To keep the caller's number, Mitel says: "Do not use a Default CPN if you want public numbers to be preserved through the SIP interface." Docs: [Mitel: configuration note](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/2025-05/Technical%20Configuration%20Notes%20for%20NISC%20IVR%20with%20MiVB%20-%20HO3860_0.pdf).

  A trunk signs in by IP address only, and gives no way to see why a call was refused or whether the trunk is up.
- **Audio over WebSocket** (Asterisk 20.16, 21.11, 22.6, 23.0 or later): Coming soon. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.
- **Can't:** no vendor virtual-agent programme exists for a generic phone system.

## Calls out

Works with a caveat, over a trunk only. Vapi sends the call to your phone system as a trunk: in Vapi's dashboard, add your system's host or address to the trunk as a gateway for calls out. Your system must allow Vapi's two addresses (Asterisk's `identify` section, CUBE's trusted list) or challenge for a username and password, then route the call on to its own carrier.

The From is the trunk number; your carrier decides what the person called sees. No custom headers travel on an ordinary call out.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- A trunk from a changing or private address: Vapi's gateway must be a fixed public IPv4 address. Use a SIP address or forwarding.
- A username-and-password sign-in on a trunk for calls in.
- Registering to Vapi like a desk phone.
- Phone systems that speak only the G.729 audio format.
- Showing why a call in was refused, or whether the trunk is up.
- Reading caller details in `User-to-User` ([UUI](/phone-systems#uui)), P-Asserted-Identity or Diversion headers.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does Asterisk carry on cleanly to its next dialplan step when the agent hangs up? One call with `Dial(...,g)`.
- Does Asterisk, and Cisco CUBE with `refer consume`, take a call passed on from Vapi with an `X-` header intact? One call each with a SIP trace.
- Does Vapi answer OPTIONS keepalives (Asterisk "qualify", CUBE keepalive)? Send one from an allowed address; if not, turn the keepalive off or the trunk shows as down.
- Are encrypted calls (TLS and SRTP) accepted on calls in? One call to port 5061.
- What do 3CX's outbound-rule screens, Unified CM's inbound-call fields and FreePBX 17's screens look like? Read each vendor's own guide.

## Costs and limits

- Forwarding uses two of your outside lines per call, and your carrier bills both.
- A handoff where Vapi dials and joins the calls through the same trunk holds two more channels on your system for the rest of the call.
- Vapi's limit on calls at once applies to SIP calls as to any call.
- Cisco CUBE licences count SIP sessions.
- Vapi's EU region is available only with the Premier Success Package. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to discuss EU access. EU organizations use `sip.eu.vapi.ai`.
- HIPAA: nothing in Vapi's SIP path changes. Unencrypted SIP on the internet is your choice; TLS and SRTP on calls in have not been tested.
