Send calls from your own phone system (Asterisk, FreePBX, 3CX, Cisco Unified CM behind Cisco CUBE, Mitel MiVoice Business or any SBC) to your agent in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). Vapi for CX makes a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address) for the agent, and your phone system sends calls to it. Any phone system can also forward chosen calls to the agent's phone number.

- **Receive calls:** At the agent's SIP address or its phone number.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to an outside number that reaches your people.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Offered on the agent's SIP address, its starter number, a trunk number, and Twilio and Telnyx numbers. Your phone system must accept the transfer.
- **Return the call to their system:** Offered once your phone system is linked. On Asterisk and FreePBX, Asterisk's [Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/) documentation describes `Dial(...,g)` carrying on in the dialplan.
- **Place calls:** Over a SIP trunk only.

## Send calls to the agent

The agent's SIP address takes calls from any phone system that can dial a SIP address and answer a sign-in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the agent's SIP address">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent, choose **Connect your phone line**, then **Another carrier or my own phone system**, then **A SIP address for this agent**. Enter an **Address name**. To let any username and password pass, turn off **Only let calls in with this username and password** first; your phone system must still answer the sign-in. Then choose **Make the address**. The address has the form `sip:<name>@sip.vapi.ai`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the username and password">
    Copy the address, the username and the password. The password is shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point a route at the address">
    In your phone system, send the calls the agent should take to the SIP address, and answer the sign-in with the username and password. On Asterisk and FreePBX, answer the sign-in with an `outbound_auth` setting on the endpoint. To send caller details, add headers whose names start with `X-`: the agent can read them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a call to the address">
    Choose **Send a call to it: check**, then send one call to the address from your phone system. Vapi can't ring a SIP address itself, so Vapi for CX waits for your call. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A phone system that signs in only by IP address can't use a SIP address. Forward calls to the agent's number instead, or use a SIP trunk (see [Other options](#other-options)).

**The number the agent sees** is whatever your phone system puts in the From header.

### Forward calls to the agent's number

A phone system can forward or transfer chosen calls to the agent's phone number: its starter number, or a Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage number connected in Vapi for CX. Each forwarded call uses two of your outside lines, and no headers travel.

- **Asterisk and FreePBX:** in the dialplan, use `Dial()` to your outside trunk with the agent's number. For the `g` option, see [Return the call to their system](#return-the-call-to-their-system). For more information, see Asterisk's [Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/).

On a forwarded call, the agent sees the caller's own number if your phone system and its carrier pass it on. Otherwise it sees your business's main number.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands callers to an outside number of your phone system. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an outside number for your people">
    In your phone system, pick the outside number of the extension, ring group or queue that should take the caller. Use a number that doesn't forward back to the agent.

    - **Asterisk:** add a dialplan route for that number.
    - **3CX:** assign a DID on your trunk to the destination. For an IVR, ring group, queue or digital receptionist, go to **Admin** › **Call Handling**, select the system extension, then **Assigned DID Number(s)**. For a person, go to **Admin** › **Users**, select the user, then the **General** tab › **Assigned DID Number(s)**. An unassigned DID goes to the trunk's default route.
    - **Mitel MiVoice Business:** use the forms **Trunk Attributes** and **SIP Peer Profile Assignment by Incoming DID**. For more information, see Mitel's [configuration note](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/2025-05/Technical%20Configuration%20Notes%20for%20NISC%20IVR%20with%20MiVB%20-%20HO3860_0.pdf); its option values are examples for one provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the outside number. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. On the agent's SIP address or a trunk number, your carrier decides, and it may refuse a number your trunk doesn't own. For more information, see [Caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

### Transfer to a SIP address

On the agent's SIP address, its starter number, a trunk number, and Twilio and Telnyx numbers, Vapi for CX offers **Transfer to a SIP address**: the agent passes the caller to an address on your phone system. Vapi can add a summary and named details to the call as `X-` headers, except that nothing but the call travels from a Twilio number, and a Telnyx number sends named details but no summary. Your phone system must accept the transfer, and be set up to keep and read those headers.

### Return the call to their system

Vapi for CX offers **Return the call to their system** once your phone system is linked to the line its calls arrive on. A SIP address made under **Another carrier or my own phone system** links no phone system by itself.

1. Open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**, pick **Your own phone system (Asterisk, 3CX, Cisco, Mitel)**, and choose **Check the line**. This links your phone system to the agent's phone number (the one you pick under **Forward to**, if the agent has several), and adds your phone system's row in **Organization settings** › **Telephony**, or adds the number to the row it already has. If the agent has only a SIP address, Vapi for CX first gives it a free Vapi number; you can keep it, or remove it with **Give it back** once step 2 is done.
2. If your calls arrive on the agent's SIP address, go to **Organization settings** › **Telephony**, choose **Edit** on your phone system's row, tick the SIP address under **Its calls arrive on**, and choose **Save**. You don't need to finish the check on the phone number.

Asterisk's [Dial](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Applications/Dial/) documentation describes option `g`: when the called party hangs up, the dialplan carries on at its next step. If your dialplan sends the call to the agent with `Dial(...,g)`, whether to the SIP address, over a trunk or out your own trunk to the agent's number, that next step can send the caller on to a queue. Every call the agent ends would follow that path, including calls where the caller had finished, so send it somewhere that suits both. Your system sees only that the call was answered (`DIALSTATUS=ANSWER`), not why the agent handed back. FreePBX runs Asterisk underneath, so add the same `g` option in your own dialplan.

On 3CX, Cisco Unified CM and Mitel, use **Transfer to a phone number**. A call your phone company forwarded for you, such as with carrier call forwarding, has been let go, and nothing returns, on any system.

## Limitations

- A phone system that signs in only by IP address can't use a SIP address: forward to the agent's number or use a trunk.
- Calls to a phone number carry no summary. A spoken summary needs a Twilio number as the agent's line and a person, not a queue or a menu, to answer.
- A trunk needs a fixed public IPv4 address and the G.711 audio format. Phone systems that speak only G.729 can't connect.
- Vapi doesn't read caller details in `User-to-User` ([UUI](/phone-systems#uui)), P-Asserted-Identity or Diversion headers: use `X-` headers.
- Vapi can't show why a call in was refused, or whether a trunk is up. If your trunk sends OPTIONS keepalives and shows as down, try turning the keepalives off.
- Forwarding uses two of your outside lines per call, and your carrier bills both.

## Other options

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="A SIP trunk">

A trunk is a standing SIP connection between your phone system and Vapi. Vapi for CX has no trunk form: set the trunk up in Vapi's dashboard (**Connect in Vapi**), then pick the number in Vapi for CX. The trunk needs a fixed public IPv4 address for your system or its SBC, and the G.711 audio format. For addresses, ports and firewall rules, see [SIP trunking](/advanced/sip/sip-trunk) and [Networking and firewall](/advanced/sip/sip-networking).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the trunk">
    In Vapi's dashboard, under **Keys**, choose **Add New SIP Trunk**. Add a gateway with your phone system's fixed public IPv4 address and turn on **Allow inbound calls** (netmask /24 to /32).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add each number">
    In Vapi's dashboard, under **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number**, then **BYO SIP Trunk Number**, once for each number or extension your system will dial. For an extension such as `7001`, select **Allow non-E164 phone numbers**. Set **SIP Trunk Credential** to the trunk you added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, pick the number, then **Call this number from your phone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send calls to the trunk">
    In your phone system or SBC, send calls to `sip:<number>@<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`, where `<credential id>` is the ID of the trunk under **Keys** in Vapi's dashboard. Use port 5060 over UDP or TCP. Put the dialed number in the To user and the real caller in From, offer G.711, send keypad tones as RFC 2833, allow Vapi's addresses and media ports from the networking guide, and put caller details in `X-` headers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Calls out** go over the trunk only. In Vapi's dashboard, add your system's host or address to the trunk as a gateway for calls out. Your system must allow Vapi's two addresses or challenge for a username and password, then route the call on to its own carrier. The From is the trunk number, and your carrier decides what the person called sees.

A handoff where Vapi dials and joins the calls through the same trunk holds two more channels on your system for the rest of the call.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Trunk settings for each phone system">

- **Asterisk (chan_pjsip):** the trunk is an `endpoint`, an `aor` with `contact=sip:<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai:5060`, and an `identify` section with `match=` Vapi's two addresses, so calls from Vapi are recognized. Set `disallow=all` and `allow=ulaw`. Set `direct_media=no` so the audio stays with Asterisk. To send caller details, add `X-` headers with `Set(PJSIP_HEADER(add,X-MyHeader)=value)` in a pre-dial handler. For more information, see Asterisk's [res_pjsip configuration examples](https://docs.asterisk.org/Configuration/Channel-Drivers/SIP/Configuring-res_pjsip/res_pjsip-Configuration-Examples/) and [PJSIP_HEADER](https://docs.asterisk.org/Asterisk_22_Documentation/API_Documentation/Dialplan_Functions/PJSIP_HEADER/).
- **FreePBX:** Asterisk underneath, so the same settings apply. On version 16 and older, go to **Connectivity** › **Trunks** › **Add Trunk** › **Add SIP (chan_sip) Trunk**, and use the **General** tab, **Dialplan Manipulation Rules** and **SIP Settings**. On version 17, choose **Add SIP (chan_pjsip) Trunk**. For more information, see Sangoma's [SIP trunk set up manually in FreePBX](https://sangomakb.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SAF/pages/15925762/Setting+up+Standard+SIPStation+Manually+in+FreePBX).
- **3CX (V20):** go to **Admin** › **Voice & Chat** › **Add Trunk**. Name the trunk and set the default route. Under SIP Trunk Details, pick the country and the provider, choose the generic template and sign in by IP address. Enter the main trunk number, add DIDs under **DID Numbers** › **+Add**, set **Options** › **Limit to**, and **Save**. A green dot means the trunk is up. On a self-hosted install, run the firewall check first. For more information, see 3CX's [Configuring SIP Trunks](https://www.3cx.com/docs/manual/sip-trunks/).
- **Cisco:** Vapi connects to CUBE, not to Unified CM. On CUBE, add Vapi's addresses to its **ip address trusted list**, send keypad tones with `dtmf-relay rtp-nte` on the dial-peer, and add caller-detail headers with a SIP profile. On Unified CM (Release 14), with a **Device Pool** in place, set up a **SIP Profile**, a **SIP Trunk Security Profile** and a SIP trunk (**Trunk Type** SIP Trunk, with **Protocol Type**, **Destination Address**, **SIP Trunk Security Profile** and **SIP Profile**), then send calls to it with a **Route Pattern**. For more information, see Cisco's [CUBE overview](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/ios-xml/ios/voice/cube/ios-xe/config/ios-xe-book/voi-cube-overview.html) and [Unified CM system configuration guide, Release 14](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/voice_ip_comm/cucm/admin/14/systemConfig/cucm_b_system-configuration-guide-14su2/cucm_b_system-configuration-guide-14_chapter_01000.html).
- **Mitel MiVoice Business (9.0 SP1):** fill in these forms, in order: License and Option Selection, Class of Service Options Assignment, Network Element Assignment, Trunk Attributes, SIP Peer Profile, SIP Peer Profile Assignment by Incoming DID, ARS Digit Modification Plans, ARS Routes, ARS Route Lists and ARS Digits Dialed. To keep the caller's number, don't use a Default CPN. For more information, see Mitel's [configuration note](https://www.mitel.com/sites/default/files/2025-05/Technical%20Configuration%20Notes%20for%20NISC%20IVR%20with%20MiVB%20-%20HO3860_0.pdf).

A trunk signs in by IP address only. A username-and-password sign-in, or registering to Vapi like a desk phone, isn't supported for calls in.
