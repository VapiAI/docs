Forward calls from Google Voice to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), through a Workspace ring group or a linked number, and hand callers back to a Google Voice number. Google says forwarding calls "to an automated system" is unsupported.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a Workspace ring group or auto attendant. A linked number is described below.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Google Voice number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No. Google Voice gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Google Voice forwards calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On Workspace Standard or Premier, an admin points a ring group or auto attendant at it. Anyone else links the agent's number to their own Google Voice account.

### From a Workspace ring group

You need the Google Voice Admin role, on Workspace Standard or Premier.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Google Voice**, and copy the number under **In Google Voice, send calls to this number**. If the agent has several numbers, pick one under **Forward to** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forward a ring group to the agent">
    In the Google Admin console, go to **Menu** › **Apps** › **Google Workspace** › **Google Voice** › **Ring groups**, and create a ring group with its own number. Under **Configuration** › **Unanswered calls**, choose **Forward the caller** › **Phone number** and enter the agent's number. Calls forward after 30 seconds unanswered. Set the after-hours action to forward as well if you want. For more information, see Google's [Set up ring groups](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/voice/set-up-google-voice-ring-groups).

    An auto attendant can transfer to the agent's number the same way. For more information, see Google's [Set up an auto attendant](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/voice/set-up-a-voice-automated-attendant-for-your-organization).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the ring group's number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From a linked number

Each Google Voice user does this for their own account, personal or Workspace. Google's [linked numbers](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/165221) page describes these steps for any phone number. It doesn't say whether Google accepts a number that an agent answers. A number can be linked to only one Google Voice account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Google Voice**, and copy the number under **In Google Voice, send calls to this number**. If the agent has several numbers, pick one under **Forward to** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it as a linked number">
    In Google Voice (voice.google.com), go to **Menu** › **Settings** › **Devices and numbers** › **New linked number**, and enter the agent's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify by phone">
    Choose **Verify by phone** › **Call**, not the text option. Google calls the agent's number and speaks a verification code. In Vapi for CX, open that call, read the code in its transcript (the digits may be written as words), and enter it in Google Voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn call screening off">
    In Google Voice, go to **Settings** › **Calls** and turn call screening off. For more information, see Google's [call screening](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9262934).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the caller's number">
    In Google Voice settings, turn off **Show my Google Voice number as caller ID when forwarding calls**, so the agent sees the caller's own number. Google says this setting is for accounts managed by a work or school. For more information, see Google's [caller ID setting](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9526789).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call your Google Voice number from another phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** on a linked number is the caller's own number when the caller ID setting above is off, and your Google Voice number when it's on.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands the caller to a Google Voice number over the phone network. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    Pick a Google Voice user's number, a ring group's number, or an auto attendant's number. Don't use the number that forwards to the agent, or the call loops back. Give each handoff ring group or user its own number, or use staff mobiles.

    Call screening on a person's Google Voice number may ask them to press 1 to accept the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Google Voice number. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number. For more information, see [Caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

**Check it:** Once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone, and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Google Voice describes no way to take the caller back after the agent hangs up.
- Only a spoken summary can travel with a handoff, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number and a person, not a queue or a menu, answers.
- One agent's number can be linked to only one Google Voice account.
- Ring groups and auto attendants aren't available on Voice Starter.
- Each forwarded call is a Google call plus a Vapi call, and Vapi bills its part.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Google Voice">

- **Port the number out of Google Voice** to a carrier that accepts it, then connect it in Vapi for CX. Ask that carrier first whether it takes numbers from Google Voice. Calls the agent places then show your number. Workspace admins port under **Number porting**; personal accounts unlock the number for $3.
- **Forward from a user's mobile** with the mobile carrier's code. This adds a second call and a person's phone to the chain, so it isn't recommended.
- **SIP Link or Carrier Link:** Not supported. They bring outside carrier numbers into Google.
- **A desk phone or other SIP device:** Not supported. Only listed Poly models connect, set up by MAC address.

</Accordion>
