Google Voice is Google's phone service, for personal accounts and for Google Workspace (the Voice plans Starter, Standard and Premier). It gives outside systems no SIP address and no API, so everything runs as ordinary phone calls: Google Voice forwards calls to the agent's number, and the agent transfers callers back to a Google Voice number. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Google Voice is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**. Google says forwarding calls "to an automated system" is unsupported.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works with a caveat. A Workspace ring group or auto attendant forwards to the agent's number, or a user links it; Google calls automated systems unsupported.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To a Google Voice user's, ring group's or auto attendant's number; only a spoken summary can travel.
- **Calls out:** Can't. Google Voice cannot place calls for Vapi.
- **Self-serve:** Works with a caveat. Each user, or a Workspace admin, does the Google side.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

Google Voice forwards calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On Workspace Standard or Premier, an admin points a ring group or auto attendant at it. Everyone else links the agent's number to their Google Voice account.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and forward to that number.

**Workspace Standard or Premier: a ring group.** Needs the Google Voice Admin role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent and pick **Google Voice**. Copy the agent's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forward a ring group to the agent">
    In the Google Admin console, under Menu › Apps › Google Workspace › Google Voice › **Ring groups**, create a ring group with its own number. Under **Configuration** › **Unanswered calls**, choose **Forward the caller** › **Phone number** and enter the agent's number. It forwards after 30 seconds unanswered; set the after-hours action to forward as well if you want. Docs: [Google: set up ring groups](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/voice/set-up-google-voice-ring-groups).

    An auto attendant can transfer to the agent's number the same way. Docs: [Google: auto attendant](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/voice/set-up-a-voice-automated-attendant-for-your-organization).

    <Note>Not verified: an auto attendant or ring group that sends every call at once, with no key press and no 30-second wait. Which caller number shows on these forwards, and whether a "press 1" prompt plays, is not documented by Google.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the ring group's number from your own phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Personal Google Voice, or any Voice user: a linked number.** Each user does this for their own account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent and pick **Google Voice**. Copy the agent's number. A number can be linked to only one Google Voice account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it as a linked number">
    In Google Voice (voice.google.com), open Menu › Settings › **Devices and numbers** › **New linked number** and enter the agent's number. Docs: [Google: linked numbers](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/165221).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify by phone">
    In Google Voice: choose **Verify by phone** › **Call**, never the text option. Google calls the agent's number and speaks a six-digit code. In Vapi for CX, open that call and read the code from its transcript (the digits may be written as words), then enter it in Google Voice.

    <Note>Not tested: whether Google accepts a Vapi number as a linked number. Other voice-agent vendors document it working.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn call screening off">
    In Google Voice, under Settings › **Calls**, turn call screening off. Calls then ring the agent alongside your Google Voice apps. Docs: [Google: forwarding rules](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/11420769).

    <Note>Not settled: one page read says screening cannot be turned off, while others say to turn it off; whether a "press 1" prompt can still play with screening off is not known.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the agent see the caller">
    In Google Voice settings: turn off **Show my Google Voice number as caller ID when forwarding calls**, so the agent sees the caller's number. Google says the setting is for accounts managed by a work or school. Docs: [Google: caller ID setting](https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9526789).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call your Google Voice number from another phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** on a linked number, the caller's own number when the caller ID setting above is off; your Google Voice number when it is on, and then every call in an experiment lands on one version. On ring group and auto attendant forwards, Unknown: Google does not document it.

**Check it:** call your Google Voice number from another phone and read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. The call goes over the public phone network to a Google Voice number. Nothing needs switching on in Google Voice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the number">
    In the Google Admin console: a Google Voice user's number, a ring group's number (each ring group has its own) or an auto attendant's number. It must not be the number that forwards to the agent, or the call loops back. Give each handoff ring group or user its own number, or use staff mobiles.

    Call screening on a person's Google Voice number may ask them to press 1 or say a name, and the transfer then lands in voicemail if they do not. A ring group that rings everyone at once avoids one person's screening.

    <Note>Not verified: whether screening applies to ring group members.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter the Google Voice number. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number; the carrier may refuse a number the line does not own. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call, caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

**Does a summary travel:** only a spoken summary, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number. No written details travel. If an auto attendant answers first, nobody would hear a spoken briefing.

**Check it:** call the agent and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when the Google Voice user's phone rings and they can talk to the caller.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Google Voice gives you no SIP address to send callers to; use "Transfer to a phone number".

### Return the call to their system

Can't be relied on. No Google page says a ring group, auto attendant or voicemail step runs after an outside number has answered and hung up: once the agent answers it owns the call, and the caller's call ends when the agent hangs up.

<Note>Not verified by a call. Forward a ring group to the agent, let it answer and hang up, and see whether the caller reaches voicemail or the call drops.</Note>

## Other routes

- **Port the number out of Google Voice**, then connect it in Vapi for CX (for example as a Twilio number). It keeps your number on calls out and gives SIP features. Workspace admins port under Number porting; personal accounts "Unlock" the number for $3. It takes days to weeks; Google gives no port time.

  <Note>Not verified: whether Twilio or Telnyx accept a number ported out of Google Voice.</Note>
- **Forwarding from a user's mobile** with the mobile carrier's code: adds a second leg and a person's phone to the chain. Not recommended. Not verified: not read on Google's own page.
- **Can't:** SIP Link or Carrier Link. They bring outside carrier numbers into Google; Google sends nothing out.
- **Can't:** a desk phone or SIP device of another kind. Only listed Poly models connect, set up by MAC address.
- **Can't:** an API. Google Voice has none.

## Calls out

Can't. Google Voice cannot place calls for Vapi: it has no SIP connection and no API. Calls the agent places show the agent's own number, unless your number is ported out of Google Voice and connected in Vapi for CX.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- A SIP address, an API or a trunk for Google Voice calls.
- Forwarding only when busy, on linked numbers: linked numbers ring alongside the Google Voice apps.
- Linking one agent's number to two Google Voice accounts.
- Ring groups or auto attendants on Workspace Starter.
- Relying on Google Voice to take the call back when the agent hangs up.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does Google accept a Vapi number as a linked number? Link one on a test account and read the code from the transcript.
- Does a "press 1" prompt appear with screening off? Call each setup and listen to the recording.
- Which caller number shows on ring group and auto attendant forwards? One forwarded call, reading the caller on the call.
- Can a ring group or auto attendant send every call at once? A greeting-only auto attendant, and a ring group with one reachable member.

## Costs and limits

- Each forwarded call has two legs, the Google leg and the Vapi leg; Vapi bills its leg. Google warns forwarding may use outside carrier rates (Not verified: not read on Google's own page).
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only.
- Google Voice costs $10 (Starter) to $30 (Premier) per user per month.
- Transfers to a Google Voice number are blind, or with a spoken summary on a Twilio number.
- HIPAA: Workspace Voice is covered by Google's BAA (the HIPAA contract); personal accounts are not. Not verified: not read on Google's own page.
