Send calls from a Five9 IVR script to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). Five9's documentation describes the script carrying on when the agent ends its part, so it can send the caller to a skill.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a **Third Party Transfer** module
- **Return the call to their system:** described in Five9's [IVR Administrator's Guide](https://documentation-be.five9.com/bundle/ivr-administrators-guide/raw/resource/enus/ivr-administrators-guide.pdf), with **Return After 3rd Party Call** on
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Five9 number on an inbound campaign
- **Transfer to a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address):** Coming soon ([Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales))

## Send calls to the agent

Five9 keeps the caller's call and places a second call to the agent's number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, in the agent's **Connect your phone line** step, choose **Connect your phone system**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Five9**, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Write it the way your Five9 domain dials: 10 digits with no leading 1, dashes or brackets (for example 4155550124), or, if your domain is enabled for E.164, starting with + and the country code (for example +14155550124).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the campaign's IVR script">
    In Five9's VCC Administrator, open the script the campaign uses in the **IVR Scripts** folder. To start a new one, right-click **IVR Scripts** and choose **Add IVR Script**. For more information, see Five9's [IVR Administrator's Guide](https://documentation-be.five9.com/bundle/ivr-administrators-guide/raw/resource/enus/ivr-administrators-guide.pdf).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Third Party Transfer module">
    Drag a **Third Party Transfer** module onto the branch the agent should answer. On its **General** tab, under **Third Party Number**, choose **Constant** and paste the agent's number. Five9's guide covers the module on pages 279 to 281.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the two timers">
    On the same tab, set **Max Time of 3rd Party Call (seconds)** (up to 3,600) and **Ringing Timeout (seconds)** (up to 30).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the script">
    Choose **Actions**, then **Save**. New calls use the saved script.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** Five9's guide doesn't say which number the Third Party Transfer module shows. For transfers a person makes, Five9 sends your domain's own number by default, not the caller's. For more information, see Five9's [Campaign Administrator's Guide](https://documentation-be.five9.com/bundle/campaign-administrators-guide/raw/resource/enus/campaign-administrators-guide.pdf), pages 39 to 40 and 46.

**Check it:** on the contact-center screen, choose **Check the line**, then call the campaign's number. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

## Hand callers to your team

Use **Return the call to their system** to send callers back into your IVR script. Where the reason matters, add a **Transfer to a phone number** handoff for each queue as well.

### Return the call to their system

Five9's IVR Administrator's Guide describes how this works: the agent ends its part of the call, and Five9, which kept the caller, carries on to the next module in your script. The caller keeps the original campaign and every Five9 call variable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Return After 3rd Party Call">
    In Five9's VCC Administrator, open the IVR script that sends callers to the agent, then its **Third Party Transfer** module, **General** tab. Turn on **Return After 3rd Party Call**. From then on, every call the agent ends goes on to the next module, including calls where the caller had finished, so send it somewhere that suits both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a next module">
    Connect a next module after the Third Party Transfer, for example a **Skill Transfer** to the queue every returned caller should reach. Without one, Five9 reports error 2705, "Third party transfer does not have a descendant".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the script">
    Choose **Actions**, then **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Name it, say when the agent should use it and choose **Return the call to their system**. There is no number to enter. The type is offered on every kind of line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

By the same guide, the caller also returns to the script by pressing the **Digit to Interrupt 3rd Party Call and Return** key, if one is set, or when **Max Time of 3rd Party Call** runs out.

### Transfer to a phone number

The agent calls a Five9 number on an inbound campaign whose script sends the caller to a skill. This adds a second Five9 call while the first one is still up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a number to an inbound campaign">
    In Five9's VCC Administrator, open **Campaign Properties** for an inbound campaign and select the **DNIS** tab. Choose **Add**, pick a number assigned to your account, choose **OK**, then **Save** or **Apply**. A number belongs to one campaign. For more information, see Five9's [Campaign Administrator's Guide](https://documentation-be.five9.com/bundle/campaign-administrators-guide/raw/resource/enus/campaign-administrators-guide.pdf), pages 58 to 61.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the caller to a skill">
    Give that campaign a script that sends the caller to a skill with a **Skill Transfer** module. It must not lead back to the agent's number, or the call loops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff**. Name it, say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Five9 number. Under **More options**, set **Their people see** to **The caller's number** if your line offers it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** once a handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the campaign's number from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over or ends its part. To hear a returned call carry on, put a **Play** module after the Third Party Transfer.

## Limitations

- Five9 isn't told why the agent handed the caller back, so every returned caller follows the script's next module and may be asked again.
- A call to the agent can last up to 3,600 seconds and ring for up to 30 seconds.
- Both calls are on your Five9 bill for the whole conversation (the caller's call and the call to the agent), plus Vapi minutes. Long-distance rates apply to the call to the agent.
- You're responsible for telling callers that a call is recorded, and for getting consent where the law requires it.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Five9">
- **AI Agent Connect over SIP, with call details:** Coming soon. Five9 reads call details over SIP only from AI vendors it has accredited, and it is a paid Five9 add-on. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) and name Five9's SIP route with details.
- **IVA Transfer module:** not supported. It is for chat only.
- **Five9 Studio IVA:** not supported. It is Five9's own voice bot.
- **A SIP address in the script:** not supported. **Third Party Number** takes a phone number only.
