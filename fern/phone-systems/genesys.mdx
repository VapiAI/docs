Send calls from a Genesys Cloud Architect flow to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a Genesys number that queues them.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a **Transfer to Number** action
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Genesys number that queues the caller
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Not with these steps
- **Return the call to their system:** No

## Send calls to the agent

Genesys calls the agent's number over the phone network and stays on the call for its whole length.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, in the agent's **Connect your phone line** step, choose **Connect your phone system**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Genesys Cloud**, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Genesys wants it in full international format, starting with + (for example +14155550124).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Transfer to Number action">
    In Genesys Architect, open the inbound call flow. In the **Toolbox**, expand **Transfer** and drag **Transfer to Number** into the menu or task. Your organization must be able to place outside calls (Genesys Cloud Voice, or a BYOC trunk on a site with an outbound route), and you need Architect edit rights. For more information, see Genesys's [Transfer to an external number](https://help.genesys.cloud/articles/transfer-external-number-2/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the agent's number">
    Give the action a **Name**, and paste the agent's number in **Number**. Optionally set **Connect Timeout**, **Pre-Transfer Audio** and **Failed Transfer Audio**. Leave **Perform release link transfer** off. For more information, see Genesys's [Transfer to Number action](https://help.genesys.cloud/articles/transfer-to-number-action/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the flow">
    Publish the flow. New calls use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** depends on your Genesys trunk's caller ID settings (**Caller Address**, **Caller Name** and **Prioritized caller ID locations**). For more information, see Genesys's [External trunk settings](https://help.genesys.cloud/130654).

**Check it:** on the contact-center screen, choose **Check the line**, then call the Genesys number that runs the flow. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands callers to a Genesys number whose call route queues them. Add one handoff for each queue the agent may send callers to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an inbound number for the queue">
    In Genesys Cloud, have your telephony admin set up an inbound number for the queue. It must exist before it shows in a call route's **Inbound Numbers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the caller to the queue">
    In Genesys Architect, in a flow, add the **Transfer to ACD** action and pick the queue in **Queue**. Publish the flow. For more information, see Genesys's [Transfer to ACD action](https://help.genesys.cloud/articles/transfer-acd-action/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a call route for the number">
    In Genesys Cloud, open **Admin › Routing › Call Routing** (or **Menu › Orchestration › Routing › Call Routing**) and choose **Add Call Route**. Fill in **Name**, **Division** and **Inbound Numbers** (type the number and select it). Under **When is this call route open?**, choose **Always** or **Based on a schedule group**. Under **What call flow should be used?**, pick the flow from the last step in **Route to**, then save. Only published flows appear. For more information, see Genesys's [Add a call route](https://help.genesys.cloud/articles/add-a-call-route/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Name it, say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Genesys number. Under **More options**, set **Their people see** to **The caller's number** if your line offers it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Genesys can't take the caller back into its flow after **Transfer to Number**, so the agent hands callers over by phone number only.
- Your Genesys flow isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.
- No caller details travel over Genesys Cloud Voice or any call on the phone network.
- Genesys stays on the first call while the agent talks, and a handoff adds a second Genesys call. You pay Genesys for both, plus Vapi minutes while the agent talks. Long-distance charges apply on Genesys Cloud Voice.
- The first Genesys conversation stays up under the handoff, so reporting and recording are split in two.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Genesys Cloud">
- **Audio Connector (AudioHook):** Coming soon. It keeps the call in your Genesys flow, so the flow can take the caller back when the agent ends its part. It needs Genesys's approval. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) and name Genesys Cloud's Audio Connector.
- **A BYOC trunk to the agent's SIP address, with details in UUI:** not available.
- **Bot Connector:** not supported. It carries text, not calls.
- **Genesys Virtual Agent:** not supported. It is Genesys's own bot, not a way to connect an outside agent.
</Accordion>
