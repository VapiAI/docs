Redirect calls from a Microsoft Teams Phone auto attendant, call queue or user to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a Teams number. Teams has no direct SIP connection to Vapi, so every call crosses the public phone network or your own SBC (session border controller: the box at the edge of a phone network that accepts SIP calls).

- **Receive calls:** Yes, redirected from an auto attendant, call queue or user.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Teams number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Direct Routing only, to your SBC. Microsoft's [SIP protocol](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-protocols-sip) documentation describes what the SBC sends on to Teams; your SBC admin must set it to accept the call.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Direct Routing only, through your SBC.

## Send calls to the agent

Calling Plan and Operator Connect numbers need no extra equipment. Direct Routing numbers also need a voice route to your SBC. Your Teams admin does the steps in Teams.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Microsoft Teams**, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Teams wants it in full international format, starting with `+` (for example `+14155550124`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redirect calls to the agent">
    In the Teams admin center, do one of these:

    - **Auto attendant:** go to **Voice** › **Auto attendants** › **Add**. In the call flow, choose **Redirect call** › **External phone number** and enter the agent's number, or add a menu option whose destination is **External phone number**. For more information, see Microsoft's [Set up an auto attendant](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-setup-auto-attendant).
    - **Call queue:** go to **Voice** › **Call queues**. Set the **Overflow**, **Call timeout** and **No agents** exceptions to **Redirect call** › **External phone number** with the agent's number. For more information, see Microsoft's [Set up a call queue](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-setup-call-queue).
    - **One user:** go to **Users** › **Manage users**, select the user, then the **Voice** tab. Under call answering rules, choose **Be immediately forwarded** and enter the agent's number. The calling policy setting "Call forwarding and simultaneous ringing to external phone numbers" must be on (it is by default). For more information, see Microsoft's [user call settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/user-call-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="License the resource account">
    Each auto attendant or call queue has a resource account, the Teams account that holds its number. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to **Users** › **Active users**, select the account, then **Licenses and Apps**, and give it the free **Microsoft Teams Phone Resource Account** license.

    To transfer to outside numbers, Calling Plan numbers also need a funded pay-as-you-go license or Communications Credits, and Direct Routing numbers need a voice routing policy (next step). For more information, see Microsoft's [licensing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-reference-prerequisites-licensing) and [resource accounts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-manage-resource-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Direct Routing, route the agent's number to your SBC">
    In the Teams admin center, go to **Voice** › **Direct Routing** › **Voice routes** › **Add**, and add a voice route whose number pattern matches the agent's number and whose SBC is yours. Give the resource account a custom voice routing policy that uses it. Your SBC admin then sends the agent's number on over the phone network, or to a Vapi SIP trunk (see [Other options](#other-options)). For more information, see Microsoft's [voice routing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-voice-routing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call your Teams number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** is the caller's own number on auto attendant and call queue transfers from Calling Plan and Operator Connect numbers. On Direct Routing, it depends on the SBC's **Forward P-Asserted-identity (PAI) header** setting: off (the default) shows the caller's number, and on shows the resource account's number. For more information, see Microsoft's [limits and supported configurations](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-reference-limits-supported-configurations).

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands the caller to a Teams number without waiting. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the Teams number">
    Pick the number that takes the handoff: a user's number, or the number on a call queue's or auto attendant's resource account. It must be a Teams number (Calling Plan, Operator Connect or Direct Routing), and not the number that redirects to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the queue a number, if it has none">
    In the Teams admin center, go to **Voice** › **Resource accounts** › **Add**. Fill in **Display name** and **Username**, set **Resource account type** to Call queue (or Auto attendant), and **Save**. License it in the Microsoft 365 admin center as in **License the resource account**, above; the license is free, but you must first add it under **Billing** › **Purchase services** › **Add-ons**.

    Back in the Teams admin center, select the account, then **Assign/unassign** › **Phone number type** › **Assigned phone number**, and save. Then go to **Voice** › **Call queues** › **Add** › **General info** › **Add an existing resource account**. You need a Teams admin role plus User Administrator. Direct Routing numbers with an extension need PowerShell. For more information, see Microsoft's [resource accounts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/aa-cq-manage-resource-accounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="On Direct Routing, route the number to Teams">
    Have your SBC admin set the SBC to take the call from Vapi and send the number to Teams. Teams needs a `+` at the start of the number and the SBC's name in the Contact header. For more information, see Microsoft's [SIP protocol](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-protocols-sip).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the Teams number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the agent's number by default, or the caller's own number if the handoff is set to show it. A Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX passes the caller's number by default. Teams looks the number up for a name and wants it with a `+`. On a queue, Microsoft says "representatives see the resource account name or call queue name".

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

### Transfer to a SIP address on Direct Routing

On Direct Routing, Vapi for CX can send the handoff to your SBC's address in the form `sip:+14155550177@<your SBC's address>`. Microsoft's [SIP protocol](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-protocols-sip) documentation describes what the SBC must send on to Teams; your SBC admin must set the SBC to accept the call Vapi passes on and route it there. In Vapi's dashboard, the trunk needs a gateway with your SBC's address for calls out. Teams shows no summary or details to the person who answers.

## Limitations

- Vapi can't connect to Teams directly: Direct Routing pairs only with a certified SBC, and Microsoft doesn't support a proxy in between.
- Teams can't take the call back when the agent's part ends: the call just ends.
- On a transfer to a phone number, only a spoken summary can travel, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number and a person, not a queue or a menu, answers. Teams shows no summary or caller details to the person who answers.
- A trunk isn't possible on Operator Connect, Teams Phone Mobile or Calling Plan numbers, so those can't place calls from Vapi.
- Each redirected call is a Teams call plus a Vapi call. Vapi bills its minutes, and Microsoft or your carrier bills the phone-network part.
- Vapi allows 10 calls at once by default.
- A Teams call queue holds at most 200 waiting calls, each for up to 45 minutes.

## Other options

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Direct Routing to a Vapi SIP trunk">

Your SBC sends the agent's number straight to a Vapi SIP trunk instead of the phone network. You need a certified SBC, its admin and a fixed public IPv4 address.

1. In Vapi's dashboard, under **Keys**, choose **Add New SIP Trunk**, with the SBC's address as a gateway with **Allow inbound calls**.
2. Under **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number** › **BYO SIP Trunk Number** for the agent's number.
3. In Vapi for CX, choose **Use a number already in Vapi** and pick the number.
4. Have your SBC admin send that number to `sip:<number>@<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`, where `<credential id>` is the ID of the trunk you added. The SBC must allow Vapi's addresses and use G.711 audio.

For addresses and ports, see [SIP trunking](/advanced/sip/sip-trunk) and [Networking and firewall](/advanced/sip/sip-networking).

**Calls out** on Direct Routing go through your SBC and carrier: in Vapi's dashboard, add the SBC to the trunk as a gateway for calls out. The caller ID is the trunk number, and your carrier decides whether to accept it.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Routes that aren't supported">

- **A Teams calling bot:** Teams users find a bot by name inside Teams, so it isn't a route for outside callers.
- **A trunk on Operator Connect, Teams Phone Mobile or Calling Plan numbers:** the operator, or Microsoft, owns the line, so these can only redirect.

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
