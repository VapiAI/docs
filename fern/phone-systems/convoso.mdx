Convoso is a cloud dialer for outbound calling, with its own phone lines and its own AI agent. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Convoso is **Coming soon**. Convoso's help pages describe a way to send its calls to an outside number, but nothing has been walked with a Convoso admin or run with a real call, so this page gives no steps. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name Convoso.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Coming soon. Not walked with a Convoso admin.
- **Transferring out:** Coming soon. Not walked with a Convoso admin.
- **Calls out:** Coming soon. Convoso places your calls; nothing connects it to the agent.
- **Self-serve:** Coming soon. Setup needs your Convoso admin.

<Note>
Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.
</Note>

## Receiving calls

Coming soon. On paper, a Convoso number can send its calls to an outside number, the agent's, or a Convoso agent can transfer an answered call to it, but no one has run either. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name Convoso.

## Handing callers to people

Coming soon. None of the three handoff types is set up for Convoso. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name Convoso.

### Transfer to a phone number

Coming soon. The agent would hand callers back to a Convoso inbound number that routes to a queue, and Convoso would match the lead by the caller's number. Not run. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a>.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Doesn't apply. Convoso documents no SIP address to hand callers to.

### Return the call to their system

Not verified. No Convoso page says what happens to the call when the agent's side ends first.

## Other routes

Coming soon. Whether Convoso's own Voice AI Agent connection can send calls to an outside agent is not known: Convoso says it "integrates specifically with the Convoso Dialer Platform". <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> and name Convoso.

## Calls out

Coming soon. Convoso normally places your calls; the agent placing calls and then handing them into Convoso would bypass Convoso's dialer and its caller-ID tools. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a>.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Starting, ending or transferring a live Convoso call by API. None was found.
- Handing a caller to Convoso over SIP. Convoso documents no SIP address.

Not yet known:

- Whether a Convoso number sends its calls straight to an outside number today. The Convoso article that describes it is several years old, and no one has run it.
- Which number Convoso shows the agent as the caller: your campaign's number or the lead's. If it is the campaign's, an experiment on the agent would put every call on one version.
- What happens in Convoso when the agent ends the call first.
- Whether Convoso charges for its leg for the whole conversation.

## Costs and limits

- The same call is likely billed twice, Convoso's minutes and Vapi's, while Convoso keeps its leg up (inferred; not verified).
- Vapi answers 10 calls at once by default. A predictive dialer can send calls in bursts above that, and the extra calls fail.
- Your consent wording must cover an AI voice, which counts as "artificial" under the TCPA, the US law on automated calls. That is yours to check.
- HIPAA: no Convoso statement was found.
