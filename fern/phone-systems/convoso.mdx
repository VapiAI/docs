Convoso is a cloud dialer for outbound calling, with its own phone lines and its own AI agent. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Convoso is **Coming soon**, so this page has no steps yet. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) and name Convoso.

- **Receive calls:** Coming soon
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Coming soon
- **Transfer to a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address):** No
- **Return the call to their system:** Coming soon

## What Coming soon covers

- **Calls in:** a Convoso number or a Convoso agent would send calls to the agent's number.
- **Handoffs:** the agent would hand callers back to a Convoso inbound number that routes to a queue, and Convoso would match the lead by the caller's number.
- **Calls out:** Convoso normally places your calls. If the agent placed them and then handed them into Convoso, they would bypass Convoso's dialer and its caller ID tools.

## Limitations

- Convoso documents no SIP address, so the agent can't hand callers to Convoso over SIP.
- Convoso documents no API to start, end or transfer a live call.
- Vapi answers 10 calls at once by default. A predictive dialer can send calls in bursts above that, and the extra calls fail.
- Your consent wording must cover an AI voice, which counts as "artificial" under the TCPA, the US law on automated calls.
