Connect a Telnyx number to your agent by pasting a Telnyx API key into [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) and picking your numbers. Telnyx is **Connect in the app**. A Telnyx SIP trunk is **Connect in Vapi** (see [Other routes](#other-routes)).

## At a glance

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, once the Vapi application is on an Outbound Voice Profile. Their people see the agent's number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Yes, with named caller details and no summary.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Yes, once the Vapi application is on an Outbound Voice Profile.

## Receiving calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a v2 API key">
    In the Telnyx portal, open [API Keys](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/api-keys) and create a v2 API key. Keys are account-wide. For more information, see Telnyx's [authentication](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/development/api-fundamentals/authentication) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Another carrier or my own phone system**, then **Telnyx**. Paste the key and choose **Find my numbers**. Vapi keeps the key and never shows it again. Vapi for CX doesn't keep it.

    Vapi for CX connects one Telnyx account per organization. A second account connects through the trunk route under [Other routes](#other-routes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your numbers">
    Vapi for CX lists each number and the connection it is on today. Tick the numbers this agent answers and choose **Connect**. Vapi for CX saves each number's old connection so that **Disconnect** can put it back.

    A Telnyx number has one connection. Connecting it to the agent stops its calls going to its old SIP connection, TeXML app or Voice API app at once, so your phone system stops receiving them. Vapi for CX warns you before you confirm. A number outside North America shows a warning too: on this route its calls can sound garbled, and the trunk route avoids that.

    Vapi creates an application named "Vapi" in your Telnyx account (in the Telnyx portal under **Voice › Programmable Voice**) and moves each number onto it. Nothing else on the number changes. For more information, see [Vapi's Telnyx guide](/telnyx).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    Call the number from your own phone. The agent answers and sees your number. Handoffs and calls out need the Outbound Voice Profile below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. Telnyx sends only the caller's number and the dialed number. No other caller details arrive on this route.

**Disconnect.** Disconnecting a Telnyx number never deletes it. Vapi for CX asks you to paste the API key again, moves the number back onto its old connection, then detaches the agent. The number stays in Vapi. If that fails, the agent stays attached and the message says what to set in the Telnyx portal.

## Handing callers to people

On a line connected by key, no summary travels with a handoff, and the phone that answers shows the agent's Telnyx number. For where handoffs are set and how to check one, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### Transfer to a phone number

Vapi asks Telnyx to transfer the call, and Telnyx dials the number. If the number is busy or doesn't answer, the call ends. It doesn't come back to the agent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Vapi application to an Outbound Voice Profile">
    In the Telnyx portal, open **Voice › [Outbound Voice Profiles](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/outbound-profiles)**. Create or open a profile and choose its destinations (the default is the US and Canada). Use **Add connections/apps to profile**, pick **Vapi**, and save. For more information, see Telnyx's [outbound voice profiles](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/voice/sip-trunking/configuration/outbound-voice-profiles) page.

    Vapi for CX reminds you of this step when you add a handoff on a Telnyx line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    On the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff** and pick **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off, and enter the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the agent's own Telnyx number, not the caller's. Telnyx sets it and ignores the agent's caller ID setting.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Telnyx transfers the call to the SIP address. Named caller details travel with it as `X-` headers. No summary travels on a line connected by key. The Outbound Voice Profile above applies here too.

### Return the call to their system

Not available on a Telnyx number: Telnyx has no call flow to take the caller back. For more information, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Other routes

- **SIP trunk (Connect in Vapi).** For a summary with each SIP handoff, numbers outside North America, or a second Telnyx account. This page gives no steps for it. It takes a SIP connection in the Telnyx portal, set up as [Vapi's Telnyx SIP guide](/advanced/sip/telnyx) describes, and one SIP trunk in Vapi's dashboard that holds both Telnyx's addresses for calls in (`192.76.120.10` and `64.16.250.10`) and the sign-in for calls out. In the Telnyx portal, each number's **Translated Number** names a Vapi SIP address, and Telnyx sends the number's calls to that address, so this setup makes two Vapi numbers for one line. On a trunk line, a SIP handoff passes the call on, keeps the caller's number, and carries the summary and named details.
- **Forward the Telnyx number to a Vapi number.** Call forwarding on the number in Telnyx. It costs a second Telnyx leg, so it is a fallback, not a main route.
- **Telnyx AI Assistants or media streaming:** nothing to connect. Connecting by key already uses media streaming.

## Calls out

Calls out work on a line connected by key and on a trunk line.

- Add the "Vapi" application (or the trunk's connection) to an Outbound Voice Profile with the destinations turned on, as in the handoff step above. Without it, calls out fail.
- The default destinations are the US and Canada. Many others need Telnyx's Level 2 verification.
- The caller ID must be valid for the account, or Telnyx refuses the call. Telnyx accepts another number at a lower trust rating.
- A trial account can only call its one verified number.
- On a trunk line, turn on **Leading Plus for Outbound** in the trunk's **Advanced Settings** in Vapi's dashboard, so numbers reach Telnyx with their leading `+`.

## Limitations

- On a line connected by key, no summary travels with a handoff, and their people see the agent's number.
- If nobody answers a handoff, the call ends. It doesn't come back to the agent.
- Only the caller's number and the dialed number arrive with a call in.
- One Telnyx account per organization. A second account uses the trunk route.
- Vapi doesn't port numbers in or out.

## Costs and limits

- Telnyx bills the call at its Voice API and media-streaming rates on top of the number (its SIP trunking rates on the trunk route), and Vapi bills its own minutes.
- A handoff adds a second Telnyx leg for the rest of the call.
- Trial accounts get one number, calls only to and from their one verified number, 2 calls out at once and 10 minutes per call. Check the portal for your account's limits.
- Telnyx limits calls to 20 a second from one source.
- Audio is G.711 µ-law only on this route. Numbers outside North America can sound garbled: use the trunk.
- A number leaves its old connection as soon as it is connected. **Disconnect** puts it back.

For what a SIP address and UUI are, see the [overview](/phone-systems).
