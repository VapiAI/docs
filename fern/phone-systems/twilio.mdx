Connect a Twilio number to your agent by pasting your Account SID and auth token into [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). The number stays in your Twilio account. Twilio is **Connect in the app**. A number on a Twilio Elastic SIP trunk is **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number**.

## At a glance

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, with an optional spoken summary.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Yes, without a written summary or caller details.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Yes.

## Receiving calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your Account SID and auth token">
    In the Twilio Console, open the dashboard and find **Account Info**. Copy the **Account SID** (it starts with `AC`) and the **Auth Token**. Use the ones for your account's main region (US1). For more information, see Twilio's [Account resource](https://www.twilio.com/docs/iam/api/account).

    Only upgraded Twilio accounts can be connected. Vapi for CX refuses a trial account, because a trial account takes calls only from verified numbers. A number set up in Twilio's Ireland (IE1) or Australia (AU1) region can't be connected with this form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line** and choose **Use my Twilio numbers**. Paste the Account SID and auth token, then choose **Find my numbers**.

    Vapi for CX uses them for that request only and keeps neither. Vapi keeps the auth token with each number it imports, so the number can answer calls and place handoffs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your numbers">
    Vapi for CX lists each number on the account and what answers it today. Tick the numbers this agent answers and choose **Connect** (the button counts the numbers you ticked).

    - A number already in Vapi is marked. A number in another Vapi organization can't be connected.
    - Ticking a number that a TwiML app (a saved Twilio call-handling setup) or an Elastic SIP trunk answers today shows a warning. Twilio ignores a number's voice address while either is set, so Vapi for CX clears it and saves the old setup for **Disconnect** to put back.

    Vapi for CX imports each number into Vapi and attaches it to the agent. Vapi sets the number's voice address and status callback. For organizations in Vapi's EU region, it also sets the number's voice routing region to Ireland (IE1). Nothing else on the number changes. Texts stay on Twilio's old routing unless you turn them on in the agent's text tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    Call the number from your own phone. The agent answers and sees your number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. Twilio gives Vapi the caller's number and the dialed number, and no SIP headers.

**Disconnect.** Disconnecting a Twilio number never deletes it from your Twilio account. For a number connected with this form, Vapi for CX asks you to paste the auth token again, then puts back what answered the number before (its old voice address, TwiML app or trunk). Only when Twilio has taken that change does Vapi for CX detach the agent and remove the number from Vapi, so you can connect it again later with this form. If Twilio refuses the change, the agent stays attached, so calls to the number still reach the agent, and the message says what to set in the Twilio Console.

If someone changed the number in the Twilio Console so that it no longer sends its calls to Vapi, **Disconnect** changes nothing at Twilio. The number stays in your Twilio account and in Vapi, answering nothing. To connect it again, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**.

A Twilio number that was already in Vapi when you picked it with **Use a number already in Vapi** is only detached, and stays in Vapi.

## Handing callers to people

From a Twilio number, only the call travels: no written summary and no caller details. A spoken summary can be played to whoever answers a handoff to a person's line. For where handoffs are set and how to check one, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### Transfer to a phone number

Each transfer is a new call out of your Twilio account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Allow the destination's country">
    If the number is outside your own country, turn that country on in the Twilio Console, in **Voice geographic permissions**. For more information, see [Twilio connection errors](/calls/troubleshoot-call-connection-errors#twilio).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    On the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff** and pick **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off, and enter the number: a queue, a team, a person's line, or a Flex number with its own flow. The agent's own number is refused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the caller's own number, by default.

**Spoken summary.** For a handoff to a person's own line, **Say a short summary to whoever answers** plays a short summary before the caller is connected. This option exists only on Twilio numbers.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Vapi dials the SIP address as a new call: Twilio refuses the other way of passing on a call that came in from the phone network. Only the call travels, with no written summary and no caller details. For a summary, use **Say a short summary to whoever answers** on a handoff to a person's own line.

### Return the call to their system

Not available on a Twilio number: Twilio Studio and Flex don't take the caller back from the agent. For more information, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Other routes

- **Keep your Studio flow and send its calls to the agent.** This is also the route for Flex, whose calls run through a Studio flow. Vapi for CX doesn't set it up. A number imported with the form above leaves its Studio flow, so this route replaces importing. In Twilio, a Studio flow's [Connect Call To](https://www.twilio.com/docs/studio/widget-library/connect-call) widget can send a call to a SIP address. Pointed at the agent's [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address), the widget uses the username and password Vapi for CX showed when the address was made.
- **Elastic SIP Trunking (Connect in Vapi).** For a number that already sits on a Twilio Elastic SIP trunk shared with a phone system. It takes more work than importing and brings no benefit for a plain Twilio number, so this page gives no steps for it. It needs an upgraded account, a new Elastic SIP trunk in Twilio for the agent (never the trunk the phone system uses), and a SIP trunk in Vapi's dashboard as [Vapi's Twilio SIP trunk guide](/advanced/sip/twilio) describes. You then pick the number in Vapi for CX with **Use a number already in Vapi**. A Twilio number sits on one trunk at a time, so moving it onto the agent's trunk takes it off the phone system's trunk, and the phone system stops receiving that number's calls. For more information, see Twilio's [Elastic SIP Trunking](https://www.twilio.com/docs/sip-trunking).
- **Your own server in front of Vapi.** For developers who need conferences or hold. Not self-serve.
- The Twilio Connect app (a "Connect Twilio" approval button) doesn't reach a main account's numbers, and Studio's **Connect Virtual Agent** widget is for Dialogflow CX only.

## Calls out

Vapi places calls out from an imported number through your Twilio account.

- The destination's country must be turned on in **Voice geographic permissions**.
- Calls to `+1` numbers can be blocked until the account has a Primary Customer Profile in Twilio's Trust Hub.
- The caller ID must be a number on your Twilio account.

Over an Elastic SIP trunk, the caller ID must be a Twilio number on the account or a verified caller ID. Numbers must reach Twilio in full international format, with the leading `+`: turn on **Leading Plus for Outbound** on the trunk in Vapi's dashboard. The trunk places 1 call a second by default, up to 5, set in the Twilio Console.

## Limitations

- Trial accounts and numbers in Twilio's IE1 or AU1 regions can't be connected.
- Handoffs from a Twilio number carry no written summary or caller details.
- Connecting takes your auth token: there is no one-click "Connect Twilio".
- Vapi doesn't port numbers.

## Costs and limits

- Twilio bills its usual per-minute price for the call, and Vapi bills its own.
- A handoff to a phone number is a second Twilio call for as long as it lasts.
- A Studio flow that sends calls to the agent keeps two Twilio legs open for the whole time the caller is with the agent, plus Studio's per-execution price.
- Texts: US numbers only, registered for US business texting (10DLC).
- The auth token is Twilio's master secret. If you rotate it in Twilio, calls out and handoffs on the line stop, and calls in still arrive. Vapi for CX marks the line "Twilio sign-in changed: reconnect". Give Vapi the new token on the number in Vapi's dashboard: the line's row in Vapi for CX links there with **Open Vapi's phone numbers**.
- HIPAA: Twilio needs its own BAA (business associate agreement) with you.

For what a SIP address and UUI are, see the [overview](/phone-systems).
