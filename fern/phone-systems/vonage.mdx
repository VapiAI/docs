Connect a Vonage number to your agent by pasting your Vonage API key and secret into [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) and picking your numbers. Vonage is **Connect in the app**. Handoffs from a Vonage number go to phone numbers only.

## At a glance

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes. Whoever answers sees the agent's number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Yes.

## Receiving calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your API key and secret">
    In the Vonage dashboard, open **Settings › [API Settings](https://dashboard.vonage.com/settings)**. Copy the **API key** and reveal the **API secret**. Keys are account-wide. For more information, see Vonage's [API settings](https://developer.vonage.com/en/dashboard/control/api-settings) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Another carrier or my own phone system**, then **Vonage**. Paste the key and secret and choose **Find my numbers**. Vapi checks them, then creates an application named "Vapi" in your Vonage account. Vapi never shows the secret again, and Vapi for CX doesn't keep it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your numbers">
    Vapi for CX lists each number and what answers it today (an application, **Forward to SIP** or **Forward to Phone**). Tick the numbers this agent answers and choose **Connect**. Vapi for CX saves each number's old setting so that **Disconnect** can put it back.

    A Vonage number has one voice handler. Connecting it to the agent stops its old application or forward getting calls at once. Vapi for CX warns you before you confirm.

    Vapi links each number to the "Vapi" application, and calls then reach the agent over a live audio connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    Call the number from your own phone. The agent answers and sees your number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. Vonage sends only the caller's number and the dialed number. No other caller details arrive.

**Disconnect.** Disconnecting a Vonage number never deletes it. Vapi for CX asks you to paste the key and secret again, puts back what answered the number before, then detaches the agent. The number stays in Vapi. If that fails, the agent stays attached and the message says what to set in the Vonage dashboard.

## Handing callers to people

A Vonage number hands callers to phone numbers only. No summary and no caller details travel, written or spoken, and the phone that answers shows the agent's Vonage number. For where handoffs are set and how to check one, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### Transfer to a phone number

The agent hands the caller on and doesn't stay. If the number is busy or doesn't answer, the call ends. Nothing needs to be installed or turned on at Vonage.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    On the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff** and pick **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off, and enter an ordinary phone number, such as a queue's or a person's. Vonage limits a trial account to registered numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number whoever answers sees:** always the agent's Vonage number, never the caller's. Vonage requires the caller ID to be one of your Vonage numbers.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Not available on a Vonage number. When the agent has only Vonage numbers, Vapi for CX grays this type with the reason "A Vonage line can't pass a call to a SIP address." When the agent also has a line that allows it, the type is offered, but it doesn't work for calls on the Vonage number.

### Return the call to their system

Not available on a Vonage number: Vonage has no call flow to take the caller back. For more information, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Other routes

- **A SIP trunk from Vonage (Forward to SIP, or Vonage SIP Trunking):** not available at launch.
- **Forward the Vonage number to the agent's number.** In the Vonage dashboard, set the number's voice setting to **Forward to Phone** and enter the agent's number in full international format, without the `+`. The agent's number is a number from Vapi, such as the free starter number. It is an ordinary call, with a second Vonage leg billed.
- **Your own Vonage Voice API application.** For developers who host their own answer address and send the call to the agent's [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address) with caller details. Not self-serve.
- Vonage AI Studio is Vonage's own agent builder, not a way to connect to Vapi.

## Calls out

Vapi places the call through Vonage from your Vonage number. The caller ID must be one of your Vonage numbers.

## Costs and limits

- Vonage bills the call at its Voice API per-minute rates plus a WebSocket connection, and Vapi bills its own minutes.
- A handoff keeps a second Vonage leg open for the rest of the call.
- Vonage limits trial accounts to registered numbers; check your account. Upgrade before connecting a real line.
- A number's old handler stops getting calls as soon as the number is connected. **Disconnect** puts it back.

## Limitations

- Handoffs go to phone numbers only, with no summary or caller details.
- Whoever answers a handoff sees the agent's Vonage number, never the caller's.
- If nobody answers a handoff, the call ends.
- Vapi doesn't port numbers.

For what a SIP address and UUI are, see the [overview](/phone-systems).
