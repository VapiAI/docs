Connect a Vonage number to your agent by pasting your Vonage API key and secret into [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) and picking your numbers.

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes. Whoever answers sees the agent's number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No.
- **Return the call to their system:** Only when a contact center or phone system that takes calls back is linked to the line. See [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).
- **Place calls:** Yes. The caller ID must be one of your Vonage numbers.

## Send calls to the agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your API key and secret">
    In the Vonage dashboard, open **Settings › [API Settings](https://dashboard.vonage.com/settings)**. Copy the **API key** and reveal the **API secret**. For more information, see Vonage's [API settings](https://developer.vonage.com/en/dashboard/control/api-settings) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Another carrier or my own phone system**, then **Vonage**. Paste the key and secret and choose **Find my numbers**. Vapi checks them, then creates an application named "Vapi" in your Vonage account. Vapi for CX doesn't keep them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your numbers">
    Vapi for CX lists each number and what answers it today (an application, or a forward). Select the numbers this agent answers and choose **Connect**. The button shows how many numbers you selected. Vapi for CX saves each number's old setting so that **Disconnect** can put it back.

    Connecting a number to the agent stops its old application getting calls at once. A forward may keep getting them: Vapi for CX says so, and you take the forward off in the Vonage dashboard. Vapi for CX warns you before you confirm.

    Vapi links each number to the "Vapi" application, and calls then reach the agent over a live audio connection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. Vonage sends only the caller's number and the dialed number.

**Check it:** when Vapi for CX shows **Call this number from your phone**, call the number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

**Disconnect.** Disconnecting a Vonage number never deletes it. Vapi for CX asks you to paste the key and secret again, puts back what answered the number before, then detaches the agent. The number stays in Vapi. If that fails, the agent stays attached and the message says what to set in the Vonage dashboard.

## Hand callers to your team

A Vonage number hands callers to phone numbers only. The agent hands the caller on and doesn't stay. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    On the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card and pick **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off, and enter an ordinary phone number, such as a queue's or a person's. Vonage limits a trial account to registered numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number whoever answers sees:** always the agent's Vonage number, never the caller's. Vonage requires the caller ID to be one of your Vonage numbers.

## Limitations

- Handoffs go to phone numbers only, with no summary or caller details, written or spoken. If nobody answers, the call ends.
- A Vonage number can't pass a call to a SIP address. When the agent has only Vonage numbers, Vapi for CX grays **Transfer to a SIP address**. When the agent also has a line that allows it, the type is offered, but it doesn't work for calls on the Vonage number.
- A Vonage number alone doesn't allow **Return the call to their system**. See [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).
- Vonage bills the call at its Voice API per-minute rates, and Vapi bills its own minutes.
- Upgrade a trial account before connecting a real line.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Vonage">
- **A SIP trunk from Vonage (Forward to SIP, or Vonage SIP Trunking):** not available.
- **Forward the Vonage number to the agent's number.** In the Vonage dashboard, set the number's voice setting to **Forward to Phone** and enter the agent's number in full international format, without the `+`. The agent's number is a number from Vapi, such as the free starter number.
- **Your own Vonage Voice API application.** For developers who host their own answer address and send the call to the agent's [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address). Not self-serve.
- **Vonage AI Studio:** Vonage's own low-code builder for conversational flows, not a way to connect to Vapi.
</Accordion>
