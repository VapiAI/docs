Vonage is a phone carrier: it sells numbers and calls, not a contact center. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Vonage is **Connect in the app**: you paste your Vonage API key and secret, pick your numbers, and Vapi for CX connects them. Handoffs from a Vonage number go to phone numbers only. Vapi publishes no other Vonage guide.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. Paste a key and secret; the number is linked to a Vapi application in your Vonage account.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To phone numbers only; their people see the agent's number.
- **Calls out:** Works today. Vapi places the call through Vonage.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. Paste the key and secret and pick numbers.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your API key and secret">
    In the Vonage dashboard, open **Settings › [API Settings](https://dashboard.vonage.com/settings)**. Copy the **API key** and reveal the **API secret**. Keys are account-wide. Docs: [Vonage: API settings](https://developer.vonage.com/en/dashboard/control/api-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into Vapi for CX">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose Vonage and paste the key and secret. Vapi checks them, then creates an application named "Vapi" in your Vonage account. Vapi never shows the secret again, and Vapi for CX does not keep it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your numbers">
    In Vapi for CX: you see each number and what answers it today (an application, **Forward to SIP** or **Forward to Phone**), and Vapi for CX saves that so **Disconnect** can put it back.

    A Vonage number has one voice handler. Connecting it to the agent stops its old application or forward getting calls at once. Vapi for CX warns you before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vapi for CX connects the numbers">
    In Vapi for CX: Vapi links the number to the "Vapi" application. Calls then reach the agent over a live audio connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the number from your own phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent.

    <Note>Needs a test call.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number. Vonage sends only the caller's number and the dialled number; no other caller details arrive.

<Note>Not run: read from Vapi's code. The first call to the line confirms it.</Note>

**Check it:** call the number from your phone. The agent answers and sees your number.

**Disconnect.** Disconnecting a Vonage number never deletes it. Vapi for CX first puts back what answered the number before, then detaches the agent. To do that it asks you to paste the key and secret again. If that fails, the agent stays attached and the message says what to set in the Vonage dashboard.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** no. No summary and no caller details travel on a Vonage number, written or spoken.

<Note>Not run: read from Vapi's code.</Note>

**Warm handoff (the agent waits with the caller):** Can't, on a Vonage number.

**Check it:** once the handoff's version is live, call the line from your own phone and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent", then asks whoever answered to confirm that it arrived. The phone that answers shows the agent's Vonage number.

<Note>Needs a test call.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. It is the only handoff a Vonage number allows. The agent hands the caller on and does not stay. If the number is busy or does not answer, the call ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have the number ready">
    Your team: an ordinary phone number that takes the handoff, such as a queue's or a person's number. Nothing to install or switch on at Vonage. On a trial account, Vonage only calls registered numbers (see [Costs and limits](#costs-and-limits)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**, say when the agent should hand off, and enter the number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** always the agent's Vonage number, never the caller's. Vonage requires the caller ID to be one of your Vonage numbers.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Not on a Vonage number: a transfer to a SIP address from it fails. When no line of the agent allows it (a Vonage number alone, or only Vonage numbers), Vapi for CX greys this type, with the reason "A Vonage line can't pass a call to a SIP address." When the agent also has a line that allows it, it is offered, and its row says it does not work on the Vonage number.

<Note>Not run: read from Vapi's code.</Note>

### Return the call to their system

Vonage is a carrier, with no call flow of its own to take the call back. Vapi for CX offers this type only when a phone system or contact center that takes calls back is linked to the agent's line. When no line of the agent has a platform linked, it is greyed, with a link to Organization settings and the reason "Link a contact center or phone system to this agent's line in Organization settings first." When a platform is linked but does not take calls back, the reason names the platform instead. The type is judged for the agent's lines together: when another of the agent's lines has one, it is offered, but it does not work for calls on the Vonage number.

## Other routes

- **A SIP trunk from Vonage (Forward to SIP, or Vonage SIP Trunking):** not available at launch. It has not been proven on Vapi.
- **Forward the Vonage number to the agent's number.** In the Vonage dashboard, set the number's voice setting to **Forward to Phone** and enter the agent's number in full international format without the `+`. The agent's number is a number from Vapi, such as the free starter number; organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so they connect the Vonage number by key instead (see [Receiving calls](#receiving-calls)). Works with a caveat: it is an ordinary call with a second Vonage leg billed.

  <Note>Needs a test call: which caller number the agent sees on a forwarded call.</Note>
- **Your own Vonage Voice API application.** For developers who host their own answer address and send the call to the agent's [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address) with caller details. Not self-serve.

  <Note>Needs a test call: no call has gone through a customer-built Voice API application.</Note>
- **Can't:** Vapi does not port numbers. Vonage AI Studio is Vonage's own agent builder, not a way to connect to Vapi.

## Calls out

Works today. Vapi places the call through Vonage from your Vonage number. The caller ID must be one of your Vonage numbers.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done, on a Vonage number:

- A handoff to a SIP address (not run: read from Vapi's code).
- A summary or caller details with a handoff.
- The agent waiting with the caller.
- Showing the caller's own number to the people who take the handoff.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does a second connection with the same key make a second "Vapi" application in your account? Connect twice and look at the application list. By reading, yes: each one adds an application.
- Can a trial account buy numbers? Vonage's pages do not say.

## Costs and limits

- Vonage bills the call at its Voice API per-minute rates plus a WebSocket connection, and Vapi bills its own minutes. Prices were not checked.
- A handoff keeps a second Vonage leg open for the rest of the call.
- Trial accounts can only call registered numbers (Vonage's pages disagree: the registered number only, or up to five test numbers). Upgrade before connecting a real line.
- A number's old handler stops getting calls at once when it is connected. **Disconnect** puts it back.
- HIPAA and a BAA (the HIPAA contract) at Vonage: not checked.
