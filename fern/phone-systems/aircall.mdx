Redirect calls from an Aircall call flow (also called a Smartflow) to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) with the **Redirect to** widget. The agent hands callers to your people by transferring them to a second Aircall number whose flow rings a team.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a call flow's **Redirect to** widget.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a second Aircall number that rings a team.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No.
- **Return the call to their system:** Coming soon, through Aircall's partner route.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Aircall redirects the call to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. Use a call flow's **Redirect to** widget, not a user's "Forward call to an external number": that one makes whoever answers press 1 within 10 seconds, and the agent can't. You need Aircall Dashboard admin rights. Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so connect a number of your own first: see the [overview](/phone-systems).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Aircall**, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Aircall wants it in full international format, with the country code and no spaces (for example `+14155550124`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the number's call flow">
    In the Aircall Dashboard, go to **Numbers**, select the number, then the **Call distribution** tab (**Call flow** on some accounts), and choose **Edit**. For more information, see Aircall's [call flow overview](https://support.aircall.io/en-gb/articles/10375394403101).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Redirect to widget">
    In the flow editor, select **+** where the call should leave, choose **Redirect to**, then **External number**, and enter the agent's number. For more information, see Aircall's [Ring to and Redirect to widgets](https://support.aircall.io/hc/en-gb/articles/15408879614365).

    To ring your team first, put a **Ring to** widget before **Redirect to**. For after hours only, use a **Time Rule** widget and put **Redirect to** on its "At any other time" branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the flow">
    In the flow editor, select **Publish**. Widgets after **Redirect to** don't run: the flow ends once the call leaves for an outside number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the line">
    In Vapi for CX, choose **Check the line**, then call the Aircall number from your own phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number the agent sees** is usually the caller's own number. Aircall shows every caller who withholds their number as one fixed number, +266696687, so the agent can't tell those callers apart.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent transfers the caller to a second Aircall number, the escalation number, whose flow rings a team. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an escalation number">
    In the Aircall Dashboard, buy or assign a second Aircall number for the team. Don't use the number whose flow redirects to the agent: Aircall warns about loops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make its call flow ring a team">
    Go to **Numbers** › **All numbers**, select the escalation number, then **Call flow** › **Edit** (top right). Choose **Add Widget** › **Ring to** › **Team** and type the team's name. Choose a ringing rule (Simultaneous, Randomly or Longest idle time), then **Publish**. Keep the flow a bare **Ring to**, with no welcome message before it. For more information, see Aircall's [managing users and teams in a call flow](https://support.aircall.io/en-gb/articles/22302751602845).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the escalation number. For more information, see [Transfer calls](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the caller's own number when the agent's line is a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX. On the starter number, it's the agent's number unless the handoff shows the caller's number.

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- The call flow ends at **Redirect to**, so Aircall can't take the caller back after the agent.
- Aircall can't route to a SIP address.
- No summary or caller details travel with a handoff. Aircall's waiting music can't be turned off, so a briefing the agent speaks before a person answers is lost.
- A user's "Forward call to an external number" can't reach the agent, because of its press-1 step.
- Aircall bills a redirected call as outbound minutes on your plan, for the whole call, and a handoff adds a third call. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- Aircall starts calls only from a logged-in user's app, so the agent places calls from its own line.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Aircall">

- **Aircall's partner route** (a partner widget in the call flow): **Coming soon**. It's the only route where the flow continues after the agent, and it needs a partnership with Aircall. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about it.
- **A Twilio number trunked into Aircall:** a number in your own Twilio account can go to Vapi at Twilio instead, connected in Vapi for CX. It must first come off the Aircall trunk.

</Accordion>
