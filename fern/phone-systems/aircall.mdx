Aircall is a cloud phone system whose admins build call flows (also called Smartflows). A call flow's **Redirect to** widget sends the call to the agent's number, and the agent hands callers to your people by transferring them to a second Aircall number whose flow rings a team. There is no way back into the same call flow, and no SIP route. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Aircall is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: you copy the agent's number there, and your Aircall admin does the steps below. Aircall's partner route is **Coming soon**: <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. A call flow's Redirect to widget sends the call to the agent's number.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To a second Aircall number whose flow rings a team; no caller details travel.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. Aircall starts calls only from a logged-in user's app; the agent uses its own line.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. An admin adds one widget and publishes; a test call proves it.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

Aircall redirects the call to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. Use a call flow's Redirect to widget, not a user's "Forward call to an external number": that one makes whoever answers press 1 within 10 seconds, and the agent cannot.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and redirect to that number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number with country code">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose **A contact center**, and pick **Aircall** from its list of platforms. Copy the agent's number in full international format, with the country code and no spaces (for example `+14155550124`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the number's call flow">
    In the Aircall Dashboard, under Numbers › the number › **Call distribution** tab, choose **Edit**. Some accounts label the tab **Call flow**. This needs Dashboard admin rights. Docs: [Aircall: call flow overview](https://support.aircall.io/en-gb/articles/10375394403101).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Redirect to widget">
    In the flow editor, press **+** where the call should leave, choose **Redirect to**, then **External number**, and enter the agent's number. Docs: [Aircall: Ring to and Redirect to widgets](https://support.aircall.io/hc/en-gb/articles/15408879614365).

    To ring your team first, put a **Ring to** widget before Redirect to. For after hours only, use a **Time Rule** widget and put Redirect to on its "At any other time" branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the flow">
    In the flow editor, press **Publish**. Widgets placed after Redirect to will not run: the flow ends once the call leaves for an outside number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the Aircall number from your own phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

    <Note>Not run.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** probably the caller's own number. Aircall documents that withheld and unknown callers all show as one fixed number, +266696687, so the agent cannot tell them apart.

<Note>Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page. Aircall doesn't say that identified callers show their own number; one test call settles it.</Note>

**Check it:** one test call. The agent answers, so the redirect reaches it. Then read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** no. No summary travels in the call. Aircall's waiting music cannot be switched off, so a briefing the agent speaks before a person answers is lost.

<Note>Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page. The waiting music was not read there.</Note>

**Check it:** call the Aircall number whose flow redirects to the agent, ask for a person, and let the agent transfer to the escalation number. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when a team member's Aircall app rings; note which number the app shows as the caller.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. The agent transfers the caller to a second Aircall number (the escalation number) whose flow rings a team. No caller details travel with the call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a second number: the escalation number">
    In the Aircall Dashboard: buy or assign a second Aircall number for the team. Never use the number whose flow redirects to the agent: Aircall warns about loops.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page. How to buy or add the number was not read there.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make its call flow ring a team">
    In the Aircall Dashboard, under Numbers › All numbers › the escalation number › **Call flow** › **Edit** (top right), choose **Add Widget** › **Ring to** › **Team** and type the team's name. Choose a ringing rule (Simultaneous, Randomly or Longest idle time), then **Publish**. Keep the flow a bare Ring to, with no welcome message before it. Docs: [Aircall: managing users and teams in a call flow](https://support.aircall.io/en-gb/articles/22302751602845).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page. The welcome message was not read there.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter the escalation number. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number by default. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number. Carriers may change the caller number on numbers Aircall does not host.

<Note>Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page. That carriers may change the caller number on numbers Aircall does not host was not read there.</Note>

### Transfer to a SIP address

Aircall gives you no SIP address to send callers to; use "Transfer to a phone number".

### Return the call to their system

Aircall can't take the call back. Aircall: "The current call flow ends, and the call flow cannot continue processing the call with additional widgets after this step." Only a partner widget lets the flow continue, and that route is Coming soon: <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.

## Other routes

- **Aircall's partner route** (a partner widget in the call flow): Coming soon. It is the only route where the flow continues after the agent, and it needs a partnership with Aircall. <a href="https://vapi.ai/sales" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact sales</a> to ask about it.
- **Twilio number trunked into Aircall:** a number held in your own Twilio account can go to Vapi at Twilio instead, connected in Vapi for CX. It must first come off the Aircall trunk.
- **Can't:** a user's "Forward call to an external number". Whoever answers must press 1 within 10 seconds, only Aircall Support can turn that off, and only one forwarded call runs at a time per user.
- **Can't:** a SIP address as a destination. None appears in Aircall's documentation.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. Aircall's API starts calls only from a logged-in user's app. The agent dials from its own line and shows its own number.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Resuming the same call flow after the agent: Aircall's flow ends at Redirect to.
- Sending a call to a SIP address.
- Telling apart callers who withhold their number: Aircall shows them all as +266696687.
- A user's forwarding to the agent, because of its press-1 step.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does an identified caller's own number reach the agent on Redirect to? One redirected call; read the caller's number on the call.
- Is there a cap on simultaneous redirected calls? Ask Aircall, then place two redirected calls at once.

## Costs and limits

- A redirected call uses two legs for the whole call. Aircall shows redirected inbound calls as outbound and bills them as outbound minutes on your plan. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- A phone-number handoff adds a third leg and needs a second Aircall number.
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only.
- Aircall plans are about $30 (Essentials) and $50 (Professional) per licence per month. Not verified: not read on Aircall's own page.
- HIPAA: not researched for Aircall.
