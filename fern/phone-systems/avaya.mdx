Avaya makes business phone systems (Aura and IP Office) and cloud contact-center products (Avaya Experience Platform and Avaya Infinity). In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), connecting Avaya is **Coming soon**, so this page has no setup steps. [Contact sales](https://vapi.ai/sales) to ask about connecting Avaya.

- **Receive calls:** Coming soon.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Coming soon.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Coming soon.
- **Return the call to their system:** Coming soon.
- **Place calls:** Coming soon.

## Limitations

- Every part of an Avaya setup is your Avaya administrator's work: Vapi for CX can't set anything up on Avaya for you.
- Avaya's own caller details (UUI and Avaya's call IDs) can't reach the agent: Vapi reads only `X-` headers and can't send [UUI](/phone-systems#uui) back.
- Vapi can't show why Avaya refused a call, so troubleshooting happens on the Avaya side.
- Avaya licenses apply: Aura SIP trunks, SBC sessions and IP Office trunk channels.
