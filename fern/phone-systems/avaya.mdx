## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Vapi for CX cannot set anything up on Avaya for you: every part is your Avaya administrator's work.
- Avaya's own caller details (UUI and Avaya's call IDs) cannot reach the agent: Vapi reads only `X-` headers and cannot send [UUI](/phone-systems#uui) back.
- Vapi cannot show why Avaya refused a call; troubleshooting is on the Avaya side.
- On Aura, the call-routing script does not take the caller back when the agent hangs up. On IP Office, a forwarded call does not return. On Avaya Experience Platform, nothing found says the workflow gets the caller back. Not verified: not read on Avaya's own page.

Not yet known:

- Which caller number reaches the agent. On Avaya Experience Platform a caller ID must be chosen, so it may be a company number.
- Every Avaya menu name: Avaya's documentation could not be read, so each must be checked with your Avaya administrator.

## Costs and limits

- Avaya licences apply: Aura SIP trunks, SBC sessions and IP Office trunk channels.
- Avaya Experience Platform's public cloud needs 200 seats; Avaya's AI add-ons and services are paid extras.
- Plan for weeks of change control on the Avaya side.
- HIPAA is a Vapi add-on that needs a BAA (the HIPAA contract).
