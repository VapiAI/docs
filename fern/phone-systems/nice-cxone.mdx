Send calls from a NICE CXone Studio script to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a CXone number whose script requests an agent with the right skill.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from a **BLINDXFER** action
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a CXone number for a skill
- **Transfer to a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address):** Not with these steps
- **Return the call to their system:** Not with these steps: the caller is cut off when the agent ends its part

## Send calls to the agent

**BLINDXFER** hands the caller to the agent's number over the phone network, and CXone lets go of the call. NICE's documentation says this frees the CXone port, that CXone no longer records, reports on or controls the call, and that the agent sees the original caller's number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, in the agent's **Connect your phone line** step, choose **Connect your phone system**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **NICE CXone**, and copy the number under **Forward to** (for example +14155550124).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a BLINDXFER action to the phone script">
    In CXone Studio, open the phone script that answers the number and add **BLINDXFER**, which transfers an existing call to another phone number. It needs Studio access and no add-on. For more information, see NICE's [BLINDXFER](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/studio/actions/blindxfer/blindxfer.htm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Destination to the agent's number">
    Set the action's **Destination** property to the agent's number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the OnDialingError branch">
    Send the **OnDialingError** branch somewhere a person can pick up. If the transfer fails, CXone can't recover the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the script">
    Choose **File**, then **Save**. Nothing after BLINDXFER runs for the call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** on the contact-center screen, choose **Check the line**, then call the CXone number. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands callers to a CXone number whose script requests an agent with a skill. Because BLINDXFER freed the first call, the handoff arrives as a clean second contact. Add one handoff for each skill, each with its own CXone number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask NICE for a number for each skill">
    Your NICE CXone account representative sets up an inbound number (DNIS) for each inbound voice ACD skill; you can't add one yourself. For more information, see NICE's [Set up inbound voice](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/acd/channels/voice/setupibvoice.htm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a skill for the team">
    In CXone, if there is no skill yet, go to **ACD › Contact Settings › ACD Skills**, choose **Create New**, then **Single Skill**. Set **Media Type** to **Phone Call**, choose **Inbound**, give a **Skill Name** and a **Campaign**, then choose **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a script that requests an agent">
    In CXone Studio, choose **New Script**, then **Phone**. Connect **BEGIN** to **REQAGENT**, which requests an agent with a specific ACD skill. With **Skill** left at **Default**, it uses the point of contact's skill. Choose **File**, then **Save**. For more information, see NICE's [REQAGENT](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/studio/actions/reqagent/reqagent.htm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tie the number to the skill and script">
    In CXone, go to **ACD › Contact Settings › Points of Contact**, choose **Create New**, then **Single Point of Contact**. Set **Media Type** to **Phone Call**, give a **Name**, pick the number in the **Point of Contact** drop-down, choose the **ACD Skill** and the **Script**, then choose **Create Point of Contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Name it, say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the CXone number for the skill (for example +14155550177). Under **More options**, set **Their people see** to **The caller's number** if your line offers it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over, and a signed-in CXone agent with that skill is offered the contact.

## Limitations

- After BLINDXFER, CXone can't take the caller back when the agent ends its part, so the agent hands callers over by phone number only. With these steps, don't add **Return the call to their system**: the caller is cut off when the agent ends its part.
- Your script isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.
- NICE issues the handoff numbers. You can't set them up yourself.
- A call handed to your people has three legs: the caller's call into CXone, the call to the agent, and the handoff back into CXone as a second contact. Long-distance charges may apply on NICE's side, and Vapi minutes are on top.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect NICE CXone">
- **Studio's TRANSFER action to the agent's number:** described in NICE's [TRANSFER](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/studio/actions/transfer/transfer.htm) page. CXone keeps recording and reporting, but the call holds two CXone ports for the whole conversation.
- **Spawn, Placecall and Link to the agent's number:** described in NICE's [PLACECALL](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/studio/actions/placecall/placecall.htm) and [LINK](https://help.nicecxone.com/content/studio/actions/link/link.htm) pages, with scripting in two scripts. The agent sees whatever caller ID the script sets. This page has no steps for it.
- **A SIP trunk NICE sets up, with a reason sent through CXone's Signal API:** not available. NICE builds the trunk, its numbers and the API credentials for each customer; it isn't self-serve, and takes NICE days rather than minutes.
- **Virtual Agent Hub ("Bring Your Own Bot"):** not supported. CXone sends the bot one sentence at a time, and the agent needs live two-way audio.
- **VOICEBOT STREAM:** not supported. NICE supports only Google Dialogflow CX there.
- **Moving the number to Vapi:** not supported. CXone numbers stay with NICE.
</Accordion>
