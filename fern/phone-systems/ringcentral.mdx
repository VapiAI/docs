RingCentral sells a business phone system, RingEX (formerly MVP), and a contact-center product, RingCX. In both, calls reach the agent's number over the public phone network, and the agent hands callers back by transferring them to a queue's own number. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), RingCentral is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: you copy the agent's number there, and your RingCentral admin does the steps below. The cleanest route is a RingCX Route node to the agent's number.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. A RingCX Route node (type PSTN), or a RingEX menu key or forward, calls the agent's number.
- **Transferring out:** Works with a caveat. To a queue's own number; no caller details travel.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. RingCentral gives third parties no outbound connection; the agent uses its own line.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. The phone-number routes need only your RingCentral admin and this page.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

RingCentral sends calls to the agent's number: the free US starter number, or a number connected in Vapi for CX. On RingCX, a Route node in Workflow Studio does it. On RingEX, use a user's forwarding or a phone menu (IVR) key.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and route RingCentral calls to that number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose **A contact center**, and pick **RingCentral** from its list of platforms. Copy the agent's number. Nothing is needed from RingCentral beforehand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingCX: open the workflow">
    In the RingCentral Admin Portal, under Contact Center › Admin › **RingCX Admin** tile › Categorization › **Workflows**, open the workflow, then **Workflow Studio**. This needs RingCX admin rights for workflows. Docs: [RingCentral: Route node](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Using-the-Route-node-in-the-RingCX-IVR-Studio.html).
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingCX: add a Route node to the agent's number">
    In Workflow Studio, add a **Route** node, set **Type** to PSTN (any number on the ordinary phone network) and enter the agent's number in **Destination**. Set **Caller ID** to ANI (the caller's own number) and set **Ring Duration**. Leave **TT Accept** off: it makes the answerer press a key, and with no key the call counts as unanswered. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingEX, instead: forward a user's calls">
    In RingEX, under the user's profile picture › **Call rules** › **Forward all calls**, for incoming calls choose **Forward the call** › **Number**, enter the agent's number, optionally **Add schedule**, and Save. The same works in the Admin Portal for an extension. Docs: [RingCentral: forwarding calls](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Forwarding-calls-external-number.html).
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingEX, instead: a phone menu (IVR) key">
    In the RingCentral Admin Portal, under Phone System › Company › **IVR Menus** › the IVR › **Key Presses** › **Add Key**, pick the key, set **Action** to **External Transfer** with the agent's number in **Transfer to**, and Save. The classic view keeps the older Auto-Receptionist path. Menus built in the IVR Visual Editor or the XML editor can't be edited here. Docs: [RingCentral: IVR menus](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Create-New-IVR-Menu-RingCentral.html?brand=RingCentral&product=RingEX&language=en_US).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call your RingCentral number from your own phone so it reaches the agent. The line is marked as working when the first call arrives. Then choose **Next: add a handoff**.

    <Note>Not run. Not yet known: whether a "press 1 to accept" prompt plays on a RingEX forward or menu key. Listen for one on this call.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:**

- **RingCX:** the caller's own number, with Caller ID set to ANI on the Route node.
- **RingEX:** not known. Community reports disagree: one says the caller's number shows, another says RingCentral Support treats the forward as a new call that shows the company number. If it is the company number, the agent cannot recognise callers and every call in an experiment lands on one version.

<Note>Community reports only, and they disagree. One forwarded test call settles it.</Note>

**Check it:** one test call. The agent answers, so the route reaches it. On RingEX, read the caller's number on the call in Vapi for CX.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** no. No summary or other details travel on a phone-number transfer. If the queue's greeting answers first, nobody would hear a spoken briefing, so switch the queue's greeting, wait messages and music off: Phone System › Groups › Call Queues › the queue › **Greeting & Hold Music**, untick Enable on each, Save.

<Note>Not verified: not read on RingCentral's own page (where the queue greeting is switched off).</Note>

**Check it:** call the agent and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when the queue's number rings and a queue member picks up; note the number that member sees.

<Note>Not run.</Note>

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. The agent transfers the caller to a number that rings a queue. No caller details travel with the call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="RingEX: give the call queue a direct number">
    In the RingCentral Admin Portal, under Phone System › Groups › **Call Queues** › the queue › **Direct Numbers** › **Add Direct Number**, choose Location and Number Type, then State/Province and Area Code, pick a number, **Add Numbers**, **Next**, tick the acknowledgement, **Next**, **Done**. Docs: [RingCentral: create a call queue](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Create-Call-Queue.html?brand=RC_US&product=RingEX&language=en_US).
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingEX, or an IVR menu's own number">
    In the RingCentral Admin Portal, under Phone System › **IVR Menus**, in Actions choose the three-dot More icon › **Add number**, then Location and Number Type (Local Number, Toll-Free Number, Vanity), pick a number, **Add Numbers**, **Next**, confirm, **Done**. In the classic view: Phone System › Auto-Receptionist › IVR Menus. Docs: [RingCentral: add a direct number to an IVR menu](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Add-Direct-Number-IVR-Menu-RingCentral.html?brand=RC_US&product=RingEX&language=en_US).
  </Step>
  <Step title="RingCX, instead: a second number that routes to a queue">
    In RingCX Workflow Studio (as above), set up a second number whose workflow has a **Route** node with Type **Voice queue** ("Transfers calls to an existing inbound queue"). That number is the one the agent hands off to. Docs: [RingCentral: Route node](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Using-the-Route-node-in-the-RingCX-IVR-Studio.html).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter the queue's, menu's or workflow's number. It must not be the number that forwards to the agent. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call](/tools/transfer-call).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** from a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX, the caller's own number by default. From the starter number, the agent's number, unless the handoff is set to show the caller's number; the carrier may refuse a number the line does not own.

### Transfer to a SIP address

RingCentral gives you no SIP address to send callers to: RingEX takes phone numbers only, and nothing documents RingCX taking a call the agent passes on over SIP. Use "Transfer to a phone number".

### Return the call to their system

Not documented on the routes this page sets up. RingEX releases a forwarded call, and a RingCX Route node to a phone number is not documented as resumable: treat the call as released when the agent hangs up. RingCX's IVA node can keep the caller in the workflow and carry on after the agent, but that route is not available at launch, so this page gives no steps for it.

<Note>Needs a test call: RingCX only, on a Vapi trunk.</Note>

## Other routes

- **RingEX call queue "Forward to External number":** a queue can send callers to the agent's number when the queue is full, the maximum wait is reached or no member answers. Whether your plan includes it is not settled: look in your Admin Portal.

  <Note>Not verified: not read on RingCentral's own page.</Note>
- **RingCX IVA Integrations** (RingCentral's own route for an outside voice agent over SIP): not available at launch. Use a RingCX Route node to the agent's number (above) instead.
- **Not recommended:** registering the agent as a RingEX SIP desk phone.
- **Not a route to the agent:** your own carrier (BYOC) or Cloud Connector. BYOC is sales-led through a gateway you own, and Cloud Connector is a migration link; neither sends chosen calls to an outside agent.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. RingCentral gives third parties no outbound connection. The agent dials from its own line and shows its own number.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Typing a SIP address in RingEX: menus, queues and call rules take numbers only.
- Returning to the flow after a RingEX forward or a RingCX Route node to a phone number.
- Turning off the press-1 prompt on call queue calls. Not verified: not read on RingCentral's own page.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does a "press 1 to accept" prompt play on menu, queue and Forward all calls forwards? Forward one call each way and listen.
- Which caller number arrives on a RingEX forward? One forwarded call; read the caller's number on the call.
- Is queue "Forward to External number" in your plan? Look in your Admin Portal.
- Is there a cap on simultaneous forwarded calls? Ask RingCentral; not found.

## Costs and limits

- Phone-number routes use two call legs for the whole call, and a phone-number handoff adds a third. RingCentral may charge for forwarded minutes. Vapi bills its minutes on top.
- RingEX queues hold 25 waiting callers for 15 minutes by default, unless RingCentral support raises them. Not verified: not read on RingCentral's own page.
- Starter numbers from Vapi are US only.
- HIPAA: not researched for RingCentral.
