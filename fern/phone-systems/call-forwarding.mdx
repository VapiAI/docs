Forward every call to your business number to your agent in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) by dialing your phone company's forwarding code. The number stays with your phone company.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, by your phone company's forwarding code.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a number other than your business number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No. A forwarded phone line gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Your phone company forwards every call to the agent's number: its free US starter number from Vapi. An agent with no starter number forwards to a US number it already answers on; if there are several, pick one under **Forward to**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your phone company">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent and choose **Connect your phone line**. Then choose **Forward my business number to it** (or **Forward my business number**, when the agent has no starter number). Under **Your phone company**, pick your company. Vapi for CX shows the code to dial, with the agent's number already in it.

    If you use a business phone system, pick **A business phone system** instead. It opens the contact-center screen, which links to your system's page in this section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your business number">
    Optionally, enter **Your business number (optional)**. Vapi for CX then refuses it as a handoff destination, so a handoff never sends a call back to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial the forwarding code">
    Dial the code from the business line you are forwarding. Wait for a tone or a message, then hang up. Verizon says the confirmation when you turn forwarding off may sound like a busy signal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call your business number">
    Choose **I’ve dialled it: check**, then call your business number from another phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These codes are published on the phone companies' own pages. In the examples, `4155550124` stands for the agent's 10-digit number. AT&T mobile and T-Mobile use other codes. Use the code Vapi for CX shows when you pick your company. Three examples:

- **Verizon mobile:** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/call-forwarding-faqs/) publishes.
- **Verizon home phone:** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/residential/homephone/calling-features/star-codes-other-features) publishes.
- **Xfinity Voice (home):** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/phone-quick-reference-guide) publishes.

If your phone company isn't in the **Your phone company** list in Vapi for CX, ask the company for its code to forward all calls. Dial that code as the company tells you, with the agent's number.

### Business phone systems

On a business phone system, an admin sets forwarding in the system's portal and enters the agent's number. [Microsoft Teams Phone](/phone-systems/microsoft-teams), [Google Voice](/phone-systems/google-voice), [Zoom](/phone-systems/zoom), [Dialpad](/phone-systems/dialpad), [RingCentral](/phone-systems/ringcentral), [8x8](/phone-systems/8x8), and [Aircall](/phone-systems/aircall) each have their own page. For another system, follow its own guide to forwarding calls to an outside number. If it asks whoever answers to press a key before the call connects, turn that off. The agent can't press keys.

## Hand callers to your team

A forwarded call is a plain phone call, so the agent hands callers on to a phone number. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a second number">
    Pick a number that reaches your people and isn't the forwarded business number, such as a mobile, a direct extension, or a ring group's own number. A call to the business number would come straight back to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the second number. For more information, see [Blind transfer](/tools/transfer-call/blind-transfer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the agent's number. For how to show the caller's number instead, see [Caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

**Check it:** Once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call your business number from your phone, and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Forwarding is one way. The agent can't place calls as your business number.
- While every call is forwarded, your business phone doesn't ring.
- Your Vapi subscription sets how many calls can run at once. For more information, see [Call concurrency](/calls/call-concurrency).
- Vapi can't tell a forwarded call from a direct one, so the check shows that a call reached the agent, not which line it came through.
- Only a spoken summary can travel with a handoff, and only when the agent's line is a Twilio number and a person, not a queue or a menu, answers.
- If the agent or its starter number is removed while forwarding is on, callers reach a dead number.
- Your phone company may bill the forwarded part of each call, depending on your plan, and Vapi bills the call as usual. Verizon says texts aren't forwarded.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to forward calls">

- **Forwarding only when busy or unanswered:** Some phone companies have a code for this, such as Verizon mobile's `*71` with the agent's number for unanswered calls. Otherwise, ask your phone company's support.
- **Porting your number into Vapi:** Not supported.

</Accordion>
