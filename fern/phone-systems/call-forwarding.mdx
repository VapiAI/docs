Forward every call to your business number to your agent in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai) by dialing your phone company's forwarding code. The number stays with your phone company.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, by your phone company's forwarding code.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a number other than your business number.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** No: a forwarded phone line gives you no SIP address to send callers to. Use **Transfer to a phone number**.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** No.

## Send calls to the agent

Your phone company forwards every call to the agent's number: its free US starter number from Vapi, or a US number it already answers on. If the agent answers on several, pick the one to forward to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your phone company">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent, choose **Connect your phone line**, then **Forward my business number to it** (or **Forward my business number**, when the agent has no starter number). Under **Your phone company**, pick your company. Vapi for CX shows the code to dial, with the agent's number already in it.

    If you use a business phone system, pick **A business phone system** instead. It opens the contact-center screen, which links to your system's page in this section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your business number">
    Optionally, enter **Your business number (optional)**. Vapi for CX then refuses it as a handoff destination, so a handoff never sends a call back to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial the forwarding code">
    Dial the code on the phone you are forwarding: a mobile code on the mobile itself, a landline or cable code on the desk phone. Wait for a tone or a message: on Verizon it can sound like a busy signal, on AT&T mobile it can take three seconds, and on an AT&T landline the agent itself answers the set-up call, which is the confirmation, so hang up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call your business number">
    Choose **I’ve dialled it: check**, then call your business number from another phone. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These codes are published on the phone companies' own pages. In the examples, `4155550124` stands for the agent's 10-digit number. `*72` doesn't work on AT&T mobile or T-Mobile. For those, use the code Vapi for CX shows when you pick your company. If your company isn't in the list, ask it for its code to forward all calls, and dial that code followed by the agent's number.

- **Verizon mobile:** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/call-forwarding-faqs/) publishes.
- **Verizon home phone:** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/support/residential/homephone/calling-features/star-codes-other-features) publishes.
- **Xfinity Voice (home):** dial `*72` `4155550124` to turn on and `*73` to turn off, as [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/phone-quick-reference-guide) publishes.

**The number the agent sees** is the caller's own number when your phone company passes it on a forwarded call.

### Business phone systems

On a business phone system, an admin sets forwarding in the system's portal and enters the agent's number. [Microsoft Teams Phone](/phone-systems/microsoft-teams), [Google Voice](/phone-systems/google-voice), [Zoom](/phone-systems/zoom), [Dialpad](/phone-systems/dialpad), [RingCentral](/phone-systems/ringcentral) and [8x8](/phone-systems/8x8) each have their own page. For others:

- **Nextiva:** in the Admin portal, go to **Users** › **Actions** › **Voice Settings** › **Forwarding** › **Call Forward Always**.
- **Vonage Business:** in the Admin Portal, go to **Phone System** › **Extensions**, select the pencil icon, then **Call Forwarding** › **Forward Calls** › **Send To Ext. or Number**. Destinations outside your plan cost extra.
- **GoTo Connect:** in the dial plan editor, add an **External number** node.
- **Grasshopper:** in the Admin portal, go to **Call management** › **Extensions**, select the extension, then **Call forwarding numbers**.
- **Ooma Office:** in the End User Portal or the desktop app, go to **Call Handling**, choose "Forward it immediately", then "My External Number". Turn off "Require key press on answer": the agent can't press a key, so it would never get the call.

## Hand callers to your team

A forwarded call is a plain phone call, so the agent hands callers on to a phone number. For how handoffs work, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a second number">
    Pick a number that reaches your people and isn't the forwarded business number, such as a mobile, a direct extension, or a ring group's own number. A call to the business number would come straight back to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and enter the second number. For more information, see [Blind transfer](/tools/transfer-call/blind-transfer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**The number your people see** is the agent's number. For how to show the caller's number instead, see [Caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call your business number from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over.

## Limitations

- Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so an agent there forwards only to a number it already answers on.
- Forwarding is one way: the agent can't place calls as your business number.
- Vapi can't tell a forwarded call from a direct one, so the check shows that a call reached the agent, not which line it came through.
- A handoff carries no summary: whoever answers gets the call only.
- Forwarding stays on until you dial the turn-off code. If the agent or its starter number is removed while forwarding is on, callers reach a dead number.
- Your phone company bills the forwarded part of each call, and Vapi bills the call as usual. Texts aren't forwarded.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to forward calls">

- **A toll-free number:** set the agent's number as the "ring-to number" in your toll-free provider's portal.
- **Forwarding only when busy or unanswered:** on AT&T mobile and traditional landlines no code is published, so ask your phone company's support to set it.
- **Porting your number into Vapi:** not supported.

</Accordion>
