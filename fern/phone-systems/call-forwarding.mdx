Call forwarding sends every call to your business number on to the agent's number. It works for almost any US line, and nothing about the number changes: it stays with your phone company. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), call forwarding is **Connect in the app**: Vapi for CX shows the agent's number and your phone company's code, you dial the code, then you ring your business number to check it. A business phone system forwards through its admin portal instead, and many have their own page in this section.

## At a glance

| | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receiving calls | Works today | Your phone company or phone system forwards every call to the agent's starter number. |
| Transferring out | Works with a caveat | To a number that is not the forwarded line; silent, and the agent's number shows. |
| Calls out | Doesn't apply | Forwarding is one-way: the agent cannot call out as your business number. |
| Self-serve | Works with a caveat | Yes on mobile, landline and cable lines, by a code; partly on business phone systems and Google Voice. |

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

Your phone company forwards every call to the agent's starter number, the free US number from Vapi. Organizations in Vapi's EU region have no starter number, so this route is not offered to them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your phone company">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent and choose to forward your business number. Pick your company under **Your phone company**. Vapi for CX shows the agent's number and the code for that company, each with **Copy**. This forwards every call.

    For a business phone system, choose **A business phone system**. It opens Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, which links that system's page here.

    Docs: [Vapi: free telephony](/free-telephony).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial the forwarding code">
    On your phone: dial the code from the table below, followed by the agent's number where the table shows it. In the examples, `4155550124` stands for the agent's 10-digit number. Mobile codes are dialled on the phone itself; landline and cable codes on the business desk phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the confirmation">
    On your phone: expect a tone or a message. Verizon says it "may sound like a busy signal". On AT&T mobile, wait at least three seconds for the tone. On AT&T landlines the agent itself answers the set-up call: that is the confirmation, so hang up.

    <Note>Not verified: not read on AT&T's own page.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: ring your business number from another phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Forwarding codes.** `*72` is right for Verizon and the cable mobile brands, and wrong for AT&T and T-Mobile.

| Phone company | Turn on (turn off) | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verizon mobile | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | From the phone. [Verizon's page](https://www.verizon.com/support/call-forwarding-faqs/) |
| AT&T mobile | `*21*` `4155550124` `#` (`#21#`) | From the phone only. Needs a test call. Not verified: not read on AT&T's own page. AT&T's business products (Collaborate, Voice DNA) use `*21*` differently: not for those lines. |
| T-Mobile | `**21*1` `4155550124` `#` (`##21#`) | Needs a test call. Not verified: not read on T-Mobile's own page. If the code fails, ask T-Mobile to forward all calls. |
| Xfinity Mobile | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | From the Xfinity Mobile device only. Not verified: not read on Xfinity's own page. |
| Spectrum Mobile | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | Both codes not confirmed. Not verified: not read on Spectrum's own page. |
| Verizon home phone | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | From the desk phone, or in My Verizon. [Verizon's page](https://www.verizon.com/support/residential/homephone/calling-features/star-codes-other-features) |
| AT&T home phone | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | Not confirmed for the digital AT&T Phone product. Not verified: not read on AT&T's own page. |
| Xfinity Voice (home) | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | From the desk phone, or Voice settings on xfinity.com. [Xfinity's page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/phone-quick-reference-guide) |
| Spectrum home phone and Spectrum Business Voice | `*72`, wait for dial tone, `4155550124` `#` (`*73`) | Or Voice Manager › Global Call Settings › Incoming Calls › Call Forwarding › Forward All Calls. Not verified: not read on Spectrum's own page. |
| Comcast Business (VoiceEdge) | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | Or the portal › Feature Settings › Call Forwarding; the portal address is not settled. The number must be allowed by your outgoing calling plan. Not verified: not read on Comcast's own page. |
| Cox Business | `*72` `4155550124` (`*73`) | Or MyAccount › Voice Tools › Call Settings › Call Forwarding. One Cox table says `*72#` and `*73#`: not settled. Not verified: not read on Cox's own page. |

**Business phone systems.** An admin changes a setting in the system's portal and enters the agent's number.

- [Microsoft Teams Phone](/phone-systems/microsoft-teams) and [Google Voice](/phone-systems/google-voice): see their pages.
- Zoom, Dialpad, RingCentral and 8x8: each has its own page in the Phone systems group.
- **Nextiva:** Admin portal › Users › Actions › Voice Settings › **Forwarding** › Call Forward Always. The guides found are from 2023; labels may have moved.
- **Vonage Business:** Admin Portal › Phone System › Extensions › pencil icon › **Call Forwarding** › **Forward Calls** › **Send To Ext. or Number**. Destinations outside your plan cost extra.
- **GoTo Connect:** in the dial plan editor, an **External number** node; or dial `*72` and the number (off: `*73`).
- **Grasshopper:** Admin portal › Call management › Extensions › the extension › **Call forwarding numbers**. One forwarding number per extension on True Solo; up to 50 otherwise.
- **Ooma Office:** End User Portal or desktop app › **Call Handling** › "Forward it immediately" › "My External Number". "Require key press on answer" must be off, or the agent, which cannot press a key, never gets the call.

<Note>Not verified: not read on Nextiva's, Vonage's, GoTo's, Grasshopper's or Ooma's own page.</Note>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number, if your phone company passes it on a forwarded call. Most do, but this was not read from each company's own page. The number dialled is always the agent's starter number.

<Note>Not verified: not read on AT&T's or T-Mobile's own page. One forwarded call from each settles it.</Note>

**Check it:** ring your business number from another phone. The first call to reach the agent proves that a call reached the agent, not that it came through your business line: Vapi cannot tell a forwarded call from a direct one.

**Turning it off.** Forwarding stays on until someone turns it off with the "turn off" code. If the agent or its starter number is removed while forwarding is on, callers reach a dead number.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

### Transfer to a phone number

Works with a caveat. A forwarded call is a plain phone call, so a phone number is the handoff that fits it. On a starter number, Vapi dials the destination itself and joins the two calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have a second way to reach your people">
    Your phone company or phone system: a number that is not the forwarded line, such as a mobile, a direct extension number, a ring group's own number, or "only when nobody answers" forwarding so your people get the first chance. Never the forwarded business number: the call would come straight back to the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff">
    In Vapi for CX, on the **Handing callers to people** card, add a handoff of type **Transfer to a phone number**. Say when the agent should hand off and enter that second number. If you gave Vapi for CX your business number, it refuses that number here, as it refuses the agent's own.

    Docs: [Vapi: blind transfer](/tools/transfer-call/blind-transfer).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the agent's number, by default. Vapi for CX can set the caller's number on the transfer instead. Docs: [Vapi: transfer call, caller ID](/tools/transfer-call#caller-id).

<Note>Not verified: whether the starter number's carrier lets through a caller's number it does not own. One transfer with the caller's number set settles it.</Note>

**Does a summary travel:** no. The handoff is silent: a spoken summary needs a Twilio number, and a written summary needs a SIP address. The agent cannot wait with the caller on this line.

**Check it:** with forwarding on, ring your business number and ask the agent for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent". It passes when the second number rings and the call does not come back to the agent.

### Transfer to a SIP address

A forwarded phone line gives you no SIP address to send callers to; use "Transfer to a phone number".

### Return the call to their system

Doesn't apply. A forwarded call is a plain phone call, with no call flow of yours to return to.

## Other routes

- **Toll-free number.** A toll-free provider forwards to a "ring-to number" set in its portal; the starter number can be that number. Not verified: not researched per provider.
- **Can't:** port your business number into Vapi. Vapi has no porting.

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. Forwarding is one-way: the agent cannot place calls as your business number. To call out as that number, move it to a carrier Vapi for CX can connect, such as a Twilio number connected in Vapi for CX or a trunk set up in Vapi's dashboard.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Knowing which business line a call came through, or that it was forwarded at all. The check proves only that a call reached the agent.
- One code for every phone company.
- Forwarding only when busy or unanswered, by code, on AT&T mobile and traditional landlines: none is published, so the phone company's support must set it.
- Call forwarding for organizations in Vapi's EU region: there is no starter number to forward to.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Do AT&T's and T-Mobile's codes work as written, and does each company pass the caller's own number? Forward one Verizon, one AT&T and one T-Mobile phone, ring each from a fourth phone, and read the caller on each call.
- Can the starter number take every call of a busy main line? It answers 10 calls at once by default; check your plan's limit before forwarding every call.
- Can the starter number show the caller's number on a handoff? One transfer with the caller's number set.
- Where the phone companies' own pages disagree: Verizon mobile forwarding in My Verizon online; Cox's `*72` against `*72#`; Comcast Business's portal address. Dial each on a real line.
- Other mobile brands (Google Fi, Mint, Visible, US Cellular, Cricket, Metro), toll-free providers and Quo (OpenPhone) were not researched.

## Costs and limits

- A forwarded call has two legs: the caller to your business line, then the forwarded leg to the agent, which your phone company bills (Verizon: "as if you'd answered on your mobile"). Business phone systems charge it as an outside call; Dialpad and Vonage Business warn of per-minute or out-of-plan charges.
- A handoff adds a third leg, which Vapi dials from the starter number.
- Vapi bills the call as usual; the starter number itself is free.
- While every call is forwarded, your business phone does not ring.
- Texts to your business number are not forwarded.
- HIPAA: nothing special on Vapi's side; the audio crosses the public phone network like any call.
