Talkdesk is a cloud contact center whose call-flow builder, Studio, can send a caller to an outside phone number. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), Talkdesk is **Vapi for CX's contact-center screen, then this page**: it connects by phone number in both directions. A Studio flow forwards callers to the agent's number, and the agent hands callers back to a Talkdesk number whose flow queues them for your people. The caller's number is the only detail that travels. Talkdesk's own route for outside AI agents over SIP ("Bring Your Own AI") has not been tested with Vapi and is not available at launch.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. Studio's "Forward to External Number" calls the agent's number.
- **Transferring out:** Works today. The agent calls a Talkdesk number whose flow queues the caller; caller ID only.
- **Calls out:** Doesn't apply. Talkdesk isn't a carrier the agent can dial through.
- **Self-serve:** Works today. Your Studio admin edits one flow and assigns numbers.

<Note>
Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.
</Note>

## Receiving calls

Talkdesk stays in the call path for the whole conversation and places a second call to the agent's number.

If your organization is in Vapi's EU region, it has no starter number. Connect a phone line first (Twilio, Telnyx or Vonage, or a number set up in Vapi's dashboard) and send Talkdesk's calls to that number.

<Note>Read from Talkdesk's documentation on Nov 22, 2026. Not run.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, open the agent, choose **Connect your phone line**, and pick **Talkdesk**. Copy the agent's number in full international format, starting with + and the country code (for example +14155550124). Talkdesk doesn't forward to a number in any other format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Forward to External Number to the flow">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, open the **Incoming call** flow and add **Forward to External Number** on the exit that should go to the agent. See Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Contact number as caller ID">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, in the component's **Preferences**, under **Caller Configuration**, choose **Contact number**: "The phone number of the person who made the call." The other choices, **Touchpoint** (your company number) and **Other account number**, hide the caller. See Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number: Preferences](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/preferences-forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the agent's number and a ring time">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, on the same **Preferences** tab, paste the agent's number under **External Number** and set the maximum ring time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the failure exits to your people">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, connect the **No answer**, **Invalid Number** and **Error Handling** exits to **Assignment and Dial**, so a call that can't reach the agent still reaches a person. See Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number: Exits](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/exits-forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish, then assign the flow to the number">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, publish the flow. Then, on the flow's card, open the numbers drop-down (it reads **No numbers** until one is assigned), choose **Manage numbers**, tick the number, and choose **Save**, then **Confirm**. A number can be assigned to only one flow at a time. See Talkdesk's [Assigning a flow to a number](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/assigning-a-flow-to-a-number).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number, when **Caller Configuration** is **Contact number** (Talkdesk's own page; it is a setting). Talkdesk doesn't say which choice is the default. On a Do-Not-Originate number, Talkdesk says to pick another account number, so the caller's number doesn't reach the agent. There, and with **Touchpoint**, every call shows your company number, and an experiment on the agent would put every call on one version.

**Check it:** in Vapi for CX, the check says "Call this number from your phone". Call the number the flow is assigned to. The line is marked working when the first call reaches the agent. Then open that call and read the caller's number: with **Contact number** chosen, it should be yours.

## Handing callers to people

Use "Transfer to a phone number", with one Talkdesk number per queue. Talkdesk can't take a caller back after "Forward to External Number", so "Return the call to their system" isn't available with these steps.

**Does a summary travel:** no. Talkdesk reads extra details sent with a call only on numbers from your own carrier (BYOC), not on ordinary numbers. The flow isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller goes to the queue and may be asked again.

**Warm handoff (the agent waits with the caller):** only when the agent's line is a Twilio number, and only to a person's own line. A Talkdesk number is answered by its flow first, so a spoken briefing would be heard by the flow, not a person.

<Note>
Not verified: not read on Talkdesk's own page. Talkdesk has nothing to set for this; keeping the flow free of greetings and menus is our reasoning.
</Note>

**Check it:** once the agent's version with the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row in Vapi for CX. Call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows "Handoff sent" when the agent hands over, then asks whoever answered to confirm. A Talkdesk agent in that queue should be offered the call showing your number.

### Transfer to a phone number

Works today, on any Talkdesk account, with nothing to license. The agent calls a Talkdesk number whose Studio flow puts the caller in a queue. Talkdesk counts it as a second interaction while the first one is still up.

<Note>Read from Talkdesk's documentation on Nov 22, 2026. Not run.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have a Talkdesk number for each queue">
    In Talkdesk, your admin picks a Talkdesk number for each queue the agent may send callers to: one number per queue.
    <Note>
    Not verified: not read on Talkdesk's own page. Talkdesk's page on assigning numbers doesn't give a menu path for finding the number.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign each number to an Incoming call flow">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, on a published **Incoming call** flow's card, open the numbers drop-down, choose **Manage numbers**, tick the number, and choose **Save**, then **Confirm**. A number goes to one flow only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the call in the queue">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, in that flow, go straight to **Assignment and Dial**, which assigns the call to a group of agents by queue, attribute or named agent. Keep the flow free of greetings and menus. See Talkdesk's [Assignment and Dial](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/assignment-dial).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Handing callers to people** card, choose **Add a handoff**. Say when the agent should hand over, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Talkdesk number for the queue (for example +14155550177). Under **More options**, choose that their people see the caller's number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number their people see:** the caller's own number on a Twilio line, or when the handoff is set to show it. Otherwise the agent's number, and on a starter, SIP-address or trunk line the carrier may still refuse a caller's number the line doesn't own. Talkdesk matches a contact on whichever number arrives.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Vapi for CX offers this type. On Talkdesk's side it goes through Talkdesk's "Bring Your Own AI" route, which takes callers back over a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address) with a short note of which queue; it needs Talkdesk's Conversation Orchestrator licence and has never been tried with Vapi. This page has no Talkdesk steps for it.

<Note>
Needs a test call. Nobody has placed a call between Talkdesk and Vapi on this route.
</Note>

### Return the call to their system

Can't after "Forward to External Number". Its exits are only for calls that never connect, so when the agent ends its part, the call is over and nothing takes the caller back. Use "Transfer to a phone number" instead: where the reason matters, add one handoff per queue, each to a Talkdesk number that reaches that queue.

Talkdesk's "Bring Your Own AI" route keeps the call in your Studio flow, so the flow could carry on when the agent ends its part. Its calls-in route is not available at launch (see [Other routes](#other-routes)).

## Other routes

## Calls out

Doesn't apply. Talkdesk hands calls to the agent; it isn't the agent's carrier. Calls the agent places go out on a phone line connected in Vapi for CX.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Taking the caller back after "Forward to External Number".
- Sending a summary on the phone-number route. The caller's number is the only detail.
- Seeing the caller's number on a Do-Not-Originate number.
- Assigning one number to more than one Studio flow.

Not yet known:

- Which **Caller Configuration** choice is the default. Add the component, leave the setting alone and call in once.
- Whether "Bring Your Own AI" works with Vapi: whether Talkdesk's call reaches the agent, and whether Talkdesk accepts the agent's hand-back. One call in a Talkdesk trial with Conversation Orchestrator on settles it.
- Whether Studio can use SIP Interconnect, and whether Vapi can be a pre-built connector. Ask Talkdesk.

## Costs and limits

- Talkdesk charges for "Forward to External Number" for the whole conversation, because it stays in the call, on top of its inbound minutes. Vapi minutes are on top. Ask your Talkdesk Customer Success Manager for rates.
- A handoff by number adds a second Talkdesk interaction while the first is still up.
- "Bring Your Own AI" has a per-minute Conversation Orchestrator charge from Talkdesk instead.
- A number belongs to one flow; an account has up to 500 flows.
- HIPAA: not researched for Talkdesk.
