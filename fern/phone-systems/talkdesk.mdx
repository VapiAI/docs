Send calls from a Talkdesk Studio flow to your agent's number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), and hand callers back to a Talkdesk number that queues them. No extra Talkdesk license is needed.

- **Receive calls:** Yes, from **Forward to External Number**
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Yes, to a Talkdesk number that queues the caller
- **Transfer to a [SIP address](/phone-systems#sip-address):** Not with these steps
- **Return the call to their system:** No

## Send calls to the agent

Talkdesk stays on the call for the whole conversation and places a second call to the agent's number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the agent's number">
    In Vapi for CX, in the agent's **Connect your phone line** step, choose **Connect your phone system**, then **A contact center**. Under **Your contact center or phone system**, pick **Talkdesk**, and copy the number under **Forward to**. Talkdesk forwards only to a number in full international format, starting with + and the country code (for example +14155550124).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Forward to External Number to the flow">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, open the **Incoming call** flow and add **Forward to External Number** on the exit that should go to the agent. For more information, see Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Contact number as caller ID">
    In the component's **Preferences**, under **Caller Configuration**, choose **Contact number** (the number of the person who made the call). The other choices, **Touchpoint** (your company number) and **Other account number**, hide the caller. For more information, see Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number: Preferences](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/preferences-forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the agent's number and a ring time">
    On the same **Preferences** tab, paste the agent's number under **External Number** and set the maximum ring time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the failure exits to your people">
    Connect the **No answer**, **Invalid Number** and **Error Handling** exits to **Assignment and Dial**, so a call that can't reach the agent still reaches a person. For more information, see Talkdesk's [Forward to External Number: Exits](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/exits-forward-to-external-number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the flow and assign it to the number">
    Publish the flow. Then, on the flow's card, open the numbers drop-down (it reads **No numbers** until one is assigned), choose **Manage numbers**, select the number, and choose **Save**, then **Confirm**. For more information, see Talkdesk's [Assigning a flow to a number](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/assigning-a-flow-to-a-number).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** Talkdesk's documentation says the caller's own number is sent when **Caller Configuration** is **Contact number**. With **Touchpoint**, or on a Do-Not-Originate number (where Talkdesk says to pick another account number), the agent sees your company number instead.

**Check it:** on the contact-center screen, choose **Check the line**, then call the number the flow is assigned to. The check passes when your call reaches the agent.

## Hand callers to your team

The agent hands callers to a Talkdesk number whose Studio flow queues them. Add one handoff for each queue, each with its own Talkdesk number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a Talkdesk number for each queue">
    In Talkdesk, pick a Talkdesk number for each queue the agent may send callers to: one number per queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give each number its own Incoming call flow">
    In the Talkdesk Studio app, use a separate published **Incoming call** flow for the handoff, not the one that forwards callers to the agent. On that flow's card, open the numbers drop-down, choose **Manage numbers**, select the number, and choose **Save**, then **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the call in the queue">
    In that flow, go straight to **Assignment and Dial**, which assigns the call to a group of agents by queue, attribute or named agent. The flow must never reach **Forward to External Number**, or the caller comes back to the agent. For more information, see Talkdesk's [Assignment and Dial](https://studio.talkdesk.com/docs/assignment-dial).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the handoff on the agent">
    In Vapi for CX, on the agent's **Call handling** tab, choose **Add a handoff** on the **Handing callers to people** card. Name it, say when the agent should use it, choose **Transfer to a phone number**, and paste the Talkdesk number for the queue (for example +14155550177). Under **More options**, set **Their people see** to **The caller's number** if your line offers it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it:** once the handoff is live, choose **Check** on its row, call the agent from your phone and ask for a person. The check shows **Handoff sent** when the agent hands you over, and a Talkdesk agent in that queue is offered the call.

## Limitations

- Talkdesk can't take the caller back after **Forward to External Number**, so the agent hands callers over by phone number only.
- The caller's number is the only detail that travels. Your flow isn't told why the caller was handed over, so the caller may be asked again.
- A number can be assigned to only one Studio flow at a time, and an account has up to 500 flows.
- Talkdesk charges for **Forward to External Number** for the whole conversation, on top of its inbound minutes, and a handoff adds a second Talkdesk interaction. Vapi minutes are on top. Ask your Talkdesk Customer Success Manager for rates.

## Other options

<Accordion title="Other ways to connect Talkdesk">
- **Connect to Autopilot Voice over SIP ("Bring Your Own AI"):** not available. It would keep the call in your Studio flow, so the flow could take the caller back when the agent ends its part. It needs Talkdesk's Conversation Orchestrator license.
- **Connect to Autopilot Voice over WebSocket:** not available.
- **Your own carrier with Talkdesk (BYOC):** not available.
- **Forward to External Number to a SIP address:** not supported. **External Number** takes a phone number only.
- **Moving the number to Vapi:** not supported. The number would leave Talkdesk.
</Accordion>
