SignalWire is a phone carrier: it sells numbers and calls, not a contact center. In [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai), SignalWire is **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number**: Vapi for CX has no SignalWire form. A short SWML script (SignalWire's call-handling script) on your number sends its calls to a SIP trunk (a standing internet phone connection) that you set up in Vapi's dashboard; then you pick the number in Vapi for CX with **Use a number already in Vapi**. Vapi publishes no other SignalWire guide; this page gives both scripts to paste.

## At a glance

- **Receiving calls:** Works today. A SWML script on the number sends each call to your Vapi trunk's address.
- **Transferring out:** Needs a test call. Vapi passes a handoff on to SignalWire, and SignalWire does not document accepting that.
- **Calls out:** Works with a caveat. The second script places the call, with one caller ID.
- **Self-serve:** Works with a caveat. Two short scripts and two assignments in SignalWire, the trunk in Vapi's dashboard, then pick the number in Vapi for CX.

<Note>Nothing on this page has been proven with a real call yet.</Note>

## Receiving calls

You need a SignalWire space with a number, out of Trial Mode: Trial Mode allows calls only to and from purchased and verified numbers, so real callers are blocked. Leaving it takes a card and at least $5.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect in Vapi: add the SIP trunk and the number">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open **Keys**, **Add New SIP Trunk**. Under **Gateway Configuration**, add SignalWire's 15 signalling IP addresses, from [SignalWire's Vapi guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi), each with **Allow inbound calls**.

    Then open **Phone Numbers**, **Create Phone Number**, **BYO SIP Trunk Number**. Enter each number once, in full international format with a leading `+`, and pick the trunk under **SIP Trunk Credential**.

    Keep this one trunk and one entry per number for both directions. SignalWire's guide registers the number a second time, on a second trunk, for calls out. On this page the calls-out gateway goes on the same trunk instead.

    <Note>Not run: read from Vapi's code, which refuses a number it already holds. SignalWire's guide expects Vapi to flag the second entry; whether Vapi refuses it in practice is not known.</Note>

    <Note>The names in Vapi's dashboard come from Vapi's dashboard code; not clicked through.</Note>

    <Note>SignalWire says its addresses "can change". Check the list with `dig sip.signalwire.com` before you add it.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the trunk's Credential ID">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open the trunk and copy its **Credential ID**. The script in the next step sends calls to `<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region use `<credential id>.sip.eu.vapi.ai`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the inbound SWML script">
    In your SignalWire space's dashboard, choose **Resources**, **Add**, **Script**, **SWML Script**. Paste this script, with your Credential ID in the address:

    ```yaml
    version: 1.0.0
    sections:
      main:
        - connect:
            to: 'sip:%{call.to}@<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai'
    ```

    `%{call.to}` is the number that was dialled, in full international format. Docs: [SignalWire: Vapi inbound](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the script to the number">
    Open **Phone Numbers**, the number, **Edit Settings**, then **Inbound Call Settings** and **Assign Resource**, and pick the script. Or, on the script, choose **Addresses & Phone Numbers**, **+ Add**, **Phone Number**. Docs: [SignalWire: ElevenLabs guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/elevenlabs).

    <Note>Not verified: not read on SignalWire's own page. SignalWire's Vapi guide does not give it; the menu wording comes from SignalWire's ElevenLabs guide.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number in Vapi for CX">
    In Vapi for CX, open **Connect your phone line** on the agent, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, and pick the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    In Vapi for CX: call the number from another phone. The line is marked as working when the first call reaches the agent.

    A refused call leaves no record in Vapi, so if a SignalWire address is missing from the trunk, Vapi for CX can only say that nothing arrived.

    <Note>Needs a test call.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number, unless the script changes it. The script can add `X-` headers to the `connect` step; the agent reads them as caller details.

<Note>Not run: read from SignalWire's script reference and Vapi's code. The first call to the line confirms it.</Note>

**Check it:** call the number from another phone. The agent answers, so the script and the addresses line up. This does not prove handoffs or calls out. Calls out need the outbound script below.

**Disconnect.** In Vapi for CX, disconnecting a number set up in Vapi's dashboard only detaches the agent. Nothing changes at SignalWire or in Vapi's dashboard.

## Handing callers to people

Handoffs are set on the agent, on the **Handing callers to people** card on the Call handling tab.

**Does a summary travel:** not to a phone number: the summary rides as a SIP header, which is lost on the phone network.

**Warm handoff (the agent waits with the caller):** not on a SignalWire line. Vapi for CX offers it on Twilio numbers only.

**Check it:** once the handoff's version is live, call the line from your own phone and ask for a person. Vapi for CX shows "Handoff sent", then asks whoever answered to confirm that it arrived.

### Transfer to a phone number

Needs a test call on a SignalWire line. Vapi for CX offers this type, and on a SignalWire trunk Vapi passes the call on to SignalWire (SIP's own transfer message) rather than placing a new call. SignalWire documents only sending a passed-on call, not accepting one on a call it joined to Vapi. This page gives no steps for it. Docs: [SignalWire: passing calls on](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/voice/sip-refer).

<Note>Needs a test call: one handoff to a phone number, with the trunk as set up above.</Note>

### Transfer to a SIP address

Needs a test call on a SignalWire line. Vapi for CX offers the type on a trunk line, and Vapi passes the call on to SignalWire, which documents only sending a passed-on call, not accepting one. This page gives no steps for it.

When the agent also answers on a Twilio number, a SIP handoff is dialled on every line, this one included, so its written summary and details are greyed, with the reason naming the Twilio number.

<Note>Needs a test call: one transfer to a SIP address, then SignalWire's logs.</Note>

### Return the call to their system

SignalWire is a carrier. Vapi for CX offers this type only when a phone system or contact center that takes calls back is linked to the agent's line. When no line of the agent has a platform linked, it is greyed, with a link to Organization settings and the reason "Link a contact center or phone system to this agent's line in Organization settings first." When a platform is linked but does not take calls back, the reason names the platform instead. The type is judged for the agent's lines together: when another of the agent's lines has one, it is offered, but it does not work for calls on the SignalWire number.

A second step in the inbound script, after `connect`, might take the caller back to your own queue when the agent hangs up. Not available at launch: whether the caller's leg survives the agent hanging up is not known, and this page gives no steps for it.

## Other routes

- **A SIP gateway resource instead of the inbound script.** A gateway in SignalWire whose address is `sip:<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`. The number arrives without a `+`, and no custom headers travel. Untested with Vapi; one call settles it.

  <Note>Needs a test call: not run with Vapi.</Note>
- **A Compatibility (cXML) application.** Same effect as the script, with more hosting.

  <Note>Not verified: not read on SignalWire's own page.</Note>
- **Forward the number to a Vapi number.** A `connect` to a number in full international format. Works with a caveat: it goes over the phone network, bills a second SignalWire leg, and carries no caller details.
- **Can't:** Vapi has no SignalWire number import or audio streaming, and Vapi does not port numbers.

## Calls out

Works with a caveat, through a second SWML script and a gateway on the same trunk.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the outbound SWML script">
    In your space's dashboard, choose **Resources**, **Add**, **Script**, **SWML Script** again, and paste:

    ```yaml
    version: 1.0.0
    sections:
      main:
        - connect:
            answer_on_bridge: true
            from: '+15551234567'
            to: '%{call.to.replace(/^sip:/i, '''').replace(/@.*/, '''')}'
    ```

    Replace `+15551234567` with a SignalWire number from your space, in full international format, or a verified caller ID. One script has one caller ID. Docs: [SignalWire: Vapi outbound](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi), [SignalWire: caller ID](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/voice/how-to-set-caller-id-or-cnam).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a SIP Address with a password">
    On that script, choose **Addresses & Phone Numbers**, **Add**, **SIP Address**. Keep **User** as `*` and set a password. The address's host looks like `<your space>-<domain>.dapp.signalwire.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Vapi: add the host to the same trunk">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open the trunk under **Keys**. Add the SIP Address's host as a gateway with **Allow outbound calls** only. Under **Advanced Settings**, turn on **Leading Plus for Outbound**. Under **Authentication (Optional)**, enter the number in full international format as the username, and the SIP Address's password. SignalWire checks only the password.

    <Note>Not run: the settings are SignalWire's Vapi guide's; the names in Vapi's dashboard were not clicked through. Needs a test call: one call in and one out on a trunk that holds both kinds of gateway.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The caller ID is the script's `from`: a SignalWire number in full international format or a verified caller ID. One script, one caller ID.
- International calling stays off until it is turned on, after the space leaves Trial Mode.
- Calls out get STIR/SHAKEN attestation level C by default; levels A and B need vetting through SignalWire support.

## What can't be done, and what is not yet known

Can't be done:

- Showing the caller's own number on a call out without editing your script.
- Learning which SignalWire address Vapi refused: a refused call leaves no record in Vapi.

Not yet known, and the test that settles it:

- Does one trunk with 15 calls-in gateways and one calls-out-only host work both ways? One call in and one out, on a space out of Trial Mode.
- Does a SIP gateway resource reach the agent, and does the number without a `+` match? One call in.
- Does a handoff, passed on from Vapi, work on a call SignalWire joined? One transfer to a phone number, and one to a SIP address.
- Does the inbound script carry on after the agent hangs up? Add a step that plays a tone, then end the call from the agent.
- Does Vapi accept the same number on a second trunk, as SignalWire's guide does? Not run: read from Vapi's code, which refuses it. One request in a test organization.
- Is the list of 15 addresses current? Run `dig sip.signalwire.com` and compare.

## Costs and limits

- SignalWire bills the call in and its connection to Vapi for the whole call, and Vapi bills its own minutes. Prices were not checked.
- Trial Mode: one number, calls only to and from purchased and verified numbers, no calls out from SIP addresses, no international calls. Leaving it takes a card and at least $5.
- A joined call is capped at 4 hours by default.
- HIPAA and a BAA (the HIPAA contract) at SignalWire: not checked.
