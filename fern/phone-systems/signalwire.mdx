Put a short SWML script (SignalWire's call-handling script) on your SignalWire number to send its calls to a SIP trunk (a standing internet phone connection) in Vapi's dashboard, then pick the number in [Vapi for CX](https://cx.vapi.ai). SignalWire is **Connect in Vapi, then pick the number**: Vapi for CX has no SignalWire form. This page gives both scripts to paste.

## At a glance

- **Receive calls:** Yes.
- **Transfer to a phone number:** Offered. Vapi passes the call to SignalWire.
- **Transfer to a SIP address:** Offered. Vapi passes the call to SignalWire.
- **Return the call to their system:** No.
- **Place calls:** Yes, through a second script with one caller ID.

## Receiving calls

You need a SignalWire space with a number, out of Trial Mode. Trial Mode allows calls only to and from purchased and verified numbers, so real callers are blocked. Leaving it takes a card and at least $5.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the SIP trunk and the number in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open **Keys** and choose **Add New SIP Trunk**. Under **Gateway Configuration**, add SignalWire's 15 signaling IP addresses, from [SignalWire's Vapi guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi), each with **Allow inbound calls**. SignalWire says these addresses can change, so check the list with `dig sip.signalwire.com` before you add it.

    Then open **Phone Numbers**, choose **Create Phone Number**, then **BYO SIP Trunk Number**. Enter each number once, in full international format with a leading `+`, and pick the trunk under **SIP Trunk Credential**.

    Keep this one trunk, and one entry per number, for both directions. SignalWire's guide adds the number a second time, on a second trunk, for calls out. On this page, the calls-out gateway goes on the same trunk instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the trunk's Credential ID">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open the trunk and copy its **Credential ID**. The script in the next step sends calls to `<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`. Organizations in Vapi's EU region use `<credential id>.sip.eu.vapi.ai`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the inbound SWML script">
    In your SignalWire space's dashboard, choose **Resources**, **Add**, **Script**, then **SWML Script**. Paste this script, with your Credential ID in the address:

    ```yaml
    version: 1.0.0
    sections:
      main:
        - connect:
            to: 'sip:%{call.to}@<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai'
    ```

    `%{call.to}` is the number that was dialed, in full international format. For more information, see [SignalWire's Vapi guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the script to the number">
    Open **Phone Numbers**, then the number, and choose **Edit Settings**. Under **Inbound Call Settings**, choose **Assign Resource** and pick the script. Or, on the script, choose **Addresses & Phone Numbers**, **+ Add**, then **Phone Number**. SignalWire's [ElevenLabs guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/elevenlabs) shows these menus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the number in Vapi for CX">
    On the agent's setup checklist, open **Connect your phone line**, choose **Use a number already in Vapi**, and pick the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call this number from your phone">
    Call the number from another phone. The agent answers, which shows that the script and the addresses line up. Calls out need the outbound script below.

    A refused call leaves no record in Vapi. If a SignalWire address is missing from the trunk, Vapi for CX can only say that nothing arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Number the agent sees:** the caller's own number, unless the script changes it. The script can add `X-` headers to the `connect` step, and the agent reads them as caller details.

**Disconnect.** In Vapi for CX, disconnecting a number set up in Vapi's dashboard only detaches the agent. Nothing changes at SignalWire or in Vapi's dashboard.

## Handing callers to people

On a SignalWire trunk, Vapi passes each handoff to SignalWire (SIP's own transfer message) rather than placing a new call. SignalWire documents [sending a passed-on call](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/voice/sip-refer), not accepting one on a call it joined to Vapi. The summary rides as a SIP header, so it doesn't reach a phone number. For where handoffs are set and how to check one, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

### Transfer to a phone number

Vapi for CX offers this type on a SignalWire line, and Vapi passes the call to SignalWire.

### Transfer to a SIP address

Vapi for CX offers this type on a SignalWire line, and Vapi passes the call to SignalWire.

### Return the call to their system

Not available on a SignalWire number. For more information, see [Handing callers to people](/phone-systems#handing-callers-to-people).

## Other routes

- **A SIP gateway resource instead of the inbound script.** A gateway in SignalWire whose address is `sip:<credential id>.sip.vapi.ai`. The number arrives without a `+`, and no custom headers travel.
- **A Compatibility (cXML) application.** The same effect as the script, with more hosting.
- **Forward the number to a Vapi number.** A `connect` to a number in full international format. It goes over the phone network, bills a second SignalWire leg, and carries no caller details.
- Vapi has no SignalWire number import or audio streaming.

## Calls out

Calls out go through a second SWML script and a gateway on the same trunk.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the outbound SWML script">
    In your space's dashboard, choose **Resources**, **Add**, **Script**, then **SWML Script** again, and paste:

    ```yaml
    version: 1.0.0
    sections:
      main:
        - connect:
            answer_on_bridge: true
            from: '+15551234567'
            to: '%{call.to.replace(/^sip:/i, '''').replace(/@.*/, '''')}'
    ```

    Replace `+15551234567` with a SignalWire number from your space, in full international format, or a verified caller ID. One script has one caller ID. For more information, see SignalWire's [Vapi guide](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/ai/vapi) and its [caller ID](https://signalwire.com/docs/platform/voice/how-to-set-caller-id-or-cnam) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a SIP Address with a password">
    On that script, choose **Addresses & Phone Numbers**, **Add**, then **SIP Address**. Keep **User** as `*` and set a password. The address's host looks like `<your space>-<domain>.dapp.signalwire.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the host to the same trunk in Vapi's dashboard">
    In Vapi's dashboard, open the trunk under **Keys**. Add the SIP Address's host as a gateway with **Allow outbound calls** only. Under **Advanced Settings**, turn on **Leading Plus for Outbound**. Under **Authentication (Optional)**, enter the number in full international format as the username, and the SIP Address's password. SignalWire checks only the password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The caller ID is the script's `from`: a SignalWire number in full international format, or a verified caller ID.
- International calling stays off until you turn it on, after the space leaves Trial Mode.
- Calls out get STIR/SHAKEN attestation level C by default. Levels A and B need vetting through SignalWire support.

## Costs and limits

- SignalWire bills the call in and its connection to Vapi for the whole call, and Vapi bills its own minutes.
- Trial Mode: one number, calls only to and from purchased and verified numbers, no calls out from SIP addresses, and no international calls. Leaving it takes a card and at least $5.
- A joined call is capped at 4 hours by default.

## Limitations

- A space in Trial Mode can't take calls from real callers.
- Calls out show the one caller ID set in the script, not the caller's own number.
- A refused call leaves no record in Vapi, so Vapi for CX can't say which address was refused.
- Vapi doesn't port numbers.

For what a SIP address and UUI are, see the [overview](/phone-systems).
